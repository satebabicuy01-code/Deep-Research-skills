## 3. Pesaing TIDAK LANGSUNG

### 3a. Spesialis kasur & tidur (kategori kasur di Informa)
- **King Koil, Serta, Simmons, Florence, Airland, Comforta, Elite, Tempur, Lady Americana** — gerai brand/galeri kasur di mal & dealer ⚠️

### 3b. Spesialis furnitur kantor (overlap INFORMA Business/kantor)
- **Chitose** (PT Chitose Internasional Tbk), **Indachi**, **Highpoint**, **Modera**, **Vivere/Working by Vivere** ✅/⚠️

### 3c. Home décor, dapur & lifestyle
- **MINISO, MR.DIY, Daiso, Usupso** — dekor & storage murah (MR.DIY 1.200+ toko ✅)
- **Zara Home, H&M Home** (fashion-home, segmen atas) ⚠️
- **AZKO / Ace Hardware (MAP)** — peralatan dapur, storage, outdoor ✅
- Brand peralatan masak dengan gerai sendiri (mis. Tupperware dealer, Lock&Lock) ⚠️

### 3d. Elektronik rumah tangga (vs INFORMA Electronics)
- **Electronic City, Hartono Elektronika, Best Denki, Erablue/Erafone (Erajaya)** dan brand store (Samsung, LG, Sharp) ⚠️

### 3e. Plastik & furnitur ekonomis
- **Napolly, Lion Star, Olymplast, Shinpo** — lemari/rak plastik dijual di hipermarket & toko kelontong ⚠️

### 3f. Online / marketplace
- **Shopee, Tokopedia–TikTok Shop, Lazada, Blibli** — furnitur knock-down murah; **IKEA online**, **Dekoruma**, **Livaza** (online furniture ✅ via CB Insights)

### 3g. Developer & jasa interior
- Paket “fully furnished” dari developer properti, kontraktor interior & kitchen set custom — mengambil belanja furnitur rumah baru ⚠️

## 5. Insight Kunci
1. **IKEA = pesaing langsung paling kuat** di Jabodetabek/Bandung, namun jaringannya kecil dan 2026 tidak agresif menambah gerai → keunggulan Informa adalah **jangkauan kota** ✅.
2. Pasar **knock-down murah** dikuasai marketplace + Olympic Group; Informa bermain di tengah.
3. Sinergi “toko bangunan + furnitur” juga dilakukan pesaing: **Mitra10 + Atria** (CSAP) ✅.
4. Startup furnitur online rapuh (Fabelio pailit; Dekoruma diakuisisi Blibli) ✅ — omnichannel tetap kunci.
