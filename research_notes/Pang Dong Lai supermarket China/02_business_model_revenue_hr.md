# Pang Dong Lai (胖东来): Business Model, Financial Performance and People/HR Model

Notes compiled 20 Nov 2026. All money figures are in RMB unless stated. Conversions are approximate and use **USD 1 = RMB 7.1** and **RMB 1 ≈ IDR 2,300**. These are working assumptions, not checked against a November 2026 rate. PDL is a private company. **No audited financials exist in the public domain.** Almost every sales, profit and wage figure comes from founder Yu Donglai (于东来), through his social posts or the company's real-time sales dashboard on its official site, and is then repeated by the media. Treat all of them as **self-reported**.

## Q1. Revenue, profit and unit economics (by year)

### Takeaway
Sales were flat at about RMB 6–7 bn for roughly a decade (2011–2022). They then tripled in three years: ~RMB 10.7 bn (2023), RMB 16.96 bn (2024), RMB 23.53 bn (2025). By 15 Nov 2026 they had reached RMB 25.84 bn year-to-date (+26%). All of this comes from about 13–14 stores in two Henan cities, so roughly RMB 17–18 bn per store in 2025. Disclosed profit is thin to moderate: RMB 140 m in 2023 (one report; conflicts with a 300–400 m estimate) and "over RMB 800 m" in 2024 (~4.7% net margin). No 2025 profit figure was found.

### Inferences
- **Approximate conversions:** 2024 RMB 16.96 bn ≈ USD 2.39 bn ≈ IDR 39 tn. 2025 RMB 23.53 bn ≈ USD 3.31 bn ≈ IDR 54 tn. 2026 YTD RMB 25.84 bn ≈ USD 3.64 bn ≈ IDR 59 tn.
- **Sales per store, 2025:** RMB 23.53 bn / ~13 full-year stores ≈ **RMB 1.8 bn per store** (≈ USD 254 m). If counted over 14 stores, ≈ RMB 1.68 bn. The figure is heavily skewed: Times Square alone was ~25% of group sales in 2025 and ~22% in 2026 YTD.
- **Sales per employee, 2025:** RMB 23.53 bn / ~10,194 ≈ **RMB 2.3 m per employee per year** (≈ USD 324k). Using the ~8,000 headcount of mid-2025 gives ≈ RMB 2.9 m.
- **Net margin:** 2024 ≈ 800 m / 16.96 bn ≈ **4.7%**. 2023 ≈ 1.3% on the RMB 140 m figure, or ~3–4% on Yu's estimate. All of these are after a large staff bonus pool (see Q2). For a grocery-led retailer, 4.7% after paying above-market wages is strong.
- **Growth drivers since 2023:** viral "tourism retail" (out-of-town visitors), daigou (resellers buying for others), and DL private-label growth (see Q6). FoodTalks frames 2025 as "代购退潮、自营发力、布局高端" (daigou receding, own-brand rising, moving upmarket). — [FoodTalks headline](https://www.foodtalks.cn/news/60813)
- 2026 growth (+26%) is slower than 2025 (+39%) even with the new Xinxiang store, which is consistent with deliberate throttling and/or traffic normalising.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2019 or 2020 sales figures were found. They were likely ~RMB 6–7 bn given the plateau, but this is unverified.
- No 2025 or 2026 profit disclosure was found. There is also no audited statement, and the 2023 profit conflict (140 m vs. 300–400 m) is unresolved.
- **Sales per sqm cannot be computed:** no reliable store floor areas were found (Times Square, Angel City, etc.).
- No explicit 2026 sales target was found. In Nov 2025 Yu said there was "no scale-expansion plan" ([21jingji](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260101/herald/f2c55c446db14826e985aecbdc4fb2be.html)).
- The 2025 split across department store, electrical, clothing and food service (~31% of sales) was not found.

## Q2. Ownership, corporate structure and profit distribution

### Takeaway
PDL (许昌市胖东来商贸集团有限公司) is private and has registered capital of only RMB 56.6 m. Yu Donglai holds about 70% of the legal equity, says he will never IPO, and takes no outside investment. Economically, it runs as a profit-sharing partnership. In March 2026 Yu announced that the ~RMB 3.8–4 bn of accumulated assets would be converted into share capital allocated to staff. Going forward, **50% of annual profit goes to team bonuses and 50% to shareholders**, and Yu says his own economic share is only ~5%.

### Cited Findings
- Founded 1995 in Xuchang, starting as a ~40 m² rented shop. — [Wikipedia: Pangdonglai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai); [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai). China Daily gives 1997 as the founding year and says ownership is shared among Yu and partners. Registered capital is RMB 56.6 m. — [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202603/18/WS69b9fc4fa310d6866eb3e61f.html)
- **No IPO:** on 5 Nov 2025 Yu said PDL has no expansion plan and no listing plan, and will become a "school-style" enterprise. — [21jingji](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260101/herald/f2c55c446db14826e985aecbdc4fb2be.html). TMTPost headlines his retirement coverage "永不上市" (never list). — [TMTPost](https://www.tmtpost.com/7880945.html)
- **Legal control:** per Tianyancha, Yu remains chairman with a **69.96%** controlling stake but no longer runs day-to-day operations. He stepped down as general manager in Nov 2025. — [TMTPost](https://www.tmtpost.com/7880945.html) (via search summary); [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3711244692484617)
- **March 2026 asset and profit distribution plan** (announced 8 Mar 2026, clarified 14 Mar):
  - Total assets were ~RMB 3.793 bn and total staff 10,194.
  - Management (718 people, 7.0% of staff) was allocated RMB 1.514 bn (39.9%). This includes **12 store managers at ~RMB 20 m each (RMB 240 m in total)**.
  - Technical staff (563 people) were allocated RMB 468 m (12.3%). The two most senior get ~RMB 10 m each.
  - Frontline staff (8,913 people) were allocated ~RMB 1.811 bn (47.7%): 280 team leaders (班长) at a RMB 300k standard and **8,633 ordinary employees at a RMB 200k standard**.
  - These amounts are **converted to share capital, not paid in cash**. Funds remain in the company under a decision committee.
  - Going forward, annual profit is split **50% to team bonuses and 50% to shareholder returns**. Yu says his share is ~5%, about RMB 200 m out of the ~4 bn.
  - — [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-03-14/doc-inhqyhwm0284098.shtml); [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260310A054UK00); [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202603/18/WS69b9fc4fa310d6866eb3e61f.html)
- PDL says it has distributed profits by job grade to all staff **since 2000**. Early shareholdings were gifted or held by nominees, and Yu says the company will "later move to a true shareholding model". — [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-03-14/doc-inhqyhwm0284098.shtml); [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202603/18/WS69b9fc4fa310d6866eb3e61f.html)
- The rationale given was clarifying asset ownership so the company could build "梦之城" (Dream City), which some reports place in Zhengzhou. Commentary questioned whether it was as generous as headlines suggested ("于东来没你们想的那么傻白甜"). — [Sina Finance commentary](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-03-23/doc-inhrynrr7932642.shtml); [Tencent](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260314A06CRE00)

### Inferences
- **Conflict to flag:** the 69.96% legal stake (Tianyancha) sits beside the claimed ~5% economic share. The likely explanation is that Yu holds equity as nominee for a broader profit pool. The new share-capital conversion had not visibly changed the registry as of the reports found.
- A **50/50 profit split** means the reported RMB 800 m 2024 "profit" may be either pre- or post-bonus. The difference matters a lot for margin benchmarking.
- Because the allocated "assets" are retained as equity and not paid out in cash, the plan is a retention and incentive device (deferred ownership) as much as a payout.

### Gaps
- No public business-registry extract of the current shareholder list after March 2026 was found.
- The definition of "profit" in the 50/50 split (pre-tax or post-tax, pre- or post-bonus) was not found.

## Q3. Cost structure, pricing and margin philosophy

### Takeaway
PDL deliberately caps gross margins and publishes cost and margin on many price tags. Examples: clothing gross margin capped at about 30%, jewelry ~15–16%, a co-branded baijiu at 15.87%. Group gross margin was reported at about 30% in 2020, against Yonghui's 21%. It runs few, very productive stores in a low-cost fourth-tier city with no bank debt. No disclosure was found on opex or on owned vs. leased real estate.

### Cited Findings
- Clothing and shoe tags have shown purchase price for 2–3 years, some with the supplier's phone number. Yu says clothing gross margin may **not exceed 30%**; one example is a down vest at RMB 189 retail on a RMB 137 cost. — [ctdsb](https://ctdsb.net/c1716_202511/2588347.html) (via search summary); [17173 news: down jacket cost 430 / retail 597](https://news.17173.com/content/11282025/164408077.shtml)
- The 2025 co-branded baijiu "酒鬼自由爱" with Jiuguijiu retails at RMB 200 against a disclosed all-in cost of RMB 168.26, a **15.87% gross margin**. — [Jiemian](https://m.jiemian.com/article/14366109.html) (via search summary)
- Jewelry gross margin is reported at only 15–16% and Hetian jade at ~20%. This comes from a secondhand report and is unverified. — [Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1351020.html) (via search summary)
- In April 2026, after a blogger challenged a "RMB 249 luxury towel", PDL promised to publish pricing and gross margin, eventually scannable by QR code, and started legal action against the blogger. — [Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202604303726098354.html); [Tencent](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260429A078BG00)
- Group gross margin was ~30% in 2020 (Southern Weekly) vs. Yonghui 21%. PDL charges **zero slotting fees**, which CIW sees as a moat listed rivals struggle to copy. — [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai)
- The company has RMB 4.1 bn cash and no loans (Oct 2025). — [21jingji](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260101/herald/f2c55c446db14826e985aecbdc4fb2be.html)
- Expansion is restrained: there was a RMB 20 bn 2025 sales cap and "no expansion plan". At least one store, Life Square, is being closed. — [FoodTalks](https://www.foodtalks.cn/news/57083); [MyDrivers](https://news.mydrivers.com/1/1148/1148005.htm); [FoodTalks EN](https://www.foodtalks.cn/en/news/57756)
- Xuchang Commerce Bureau has published a "诚信兴商" (integrity in commerce) case study on PDL. — [Xuchang 商务局 PDF](https://swj.xuchang.gov.cn/uploadfile/e2672917-adfe-42ad-a76f-1035f4da0063/%E8%83%96%E4%B8%9C%E6%9D%A5%E2%80%9C%E8%AF%9A%E4%BF%A1%E5%85%B4%E5%95%86%E2%80%9D%E5%85%B8%E5%9E%8B%E6%A1%88%E4%BE%8B.pdf) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- **Rough cost stack:** gross margin ~25–30% minus net margin ~4.7% (2024) leaves ~20–25% of sales for opex, including labor, bonuses, services and compensation. This is an inference from mixed-year data, not a disclosure.
- The economics rest on very high throughput per store (~RMB 1.7–1.8 bn) spreading fixed costs, not on high unit margins.
- No found source says no-slotting-fee or cost-plus pricing is applied group-wide. The disclosed examples are category-specific (clothing, jewelry, liquor).

### Gaps
- No opex breakdown, rent or property ownership data was found (owned vs. leased stores).
- No official group-wide gross margin for 2023–2025 was found.

## Q4. HR model: wages, hours, leave, turnover, labor cost

### Takeaway
PDL pays roughly RMB 9,000–9,600 per month on average (after tax), which is high for Xuchang. It closes most stores on Tuesdays and grants ~40 days of annual leave plus 10 days of "unhappy leave". Daily hours are about 7 today, moving to a formal 5-day, 35-hour week from March 2027. Reported turnover is very low (0.50% in H1 2026). Management and technical staff earn about RMB 700k a year.

### Cited Findings
- **Wages:**
  - 2024: average employee monthly income over RMB 9,000. — [Sina/SSN](https://finance.sina.cn/2025-01-08/detail-ineehmtr0383943.d.html)
  - June 2025: average post-tax monthly salary RMB 9,000; management and technical staff ~RMB 700k per year. — [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202603/18/WS69b9fc4fa310d6866eb3e61f.html)
  - **Q1 2026: average wage RMB 9,598 per month.** — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4895020.html); [Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610013888189490.html) (via search summary)
  - A 2023 social-media claim that the "minimum take-home is RMB 7,000" is not officially confirmed. — [NBD](https://www.nbd.com.cn/articles/2023-12-02/3143738.html)
- **Local benchmark:** no Xuchang retail wage figure was found. Nationally, the 2024 average urban non-private-sector wage was RMB 124,110 per year (~RMB 10,300 per month, pre-tax), and private-sector and Henan wages are considerably lower. — [Xinhua/NBS](https://www.news.cn/politics/20250518/4a82e403a0a14e3bb78e4573568bcf05/c.html)
- **Hours:**
  - The internal standard in 2025 was 42 hours per week.
  - In June 2025 Yu set a target of no more than 36 hours per week and at least 40 days of annual leave.
  - On 30 Sep 2026 he announced that **from March 2027 all staff and management will work 5 days a week, 7 hours a day (35 hours per week) with no pay cut**, covering cashiers, stockers and cleaners.
  - — [Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202610013888189490.html); [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4895020.html); [Jiemian](https://m.jiemian.com/article/15158387.html)
  - China Daily (Mar 2026) already describes the regime as "seven hours a day, 40 days of annual leave". — [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202603/18/WS69b9fc4fa310d6866eb3e61f.html)
- **Leave choice survey:** in Feb 2026, 82% of staff preferred the current leave system. The rest chose more leave with modest pay cuts. — [China Daily](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202603/18/WS69b9fc4fa310d6866eb3e61f.html)
- **Tuesday closure (周二闭店):** still in force in Oct 2026. Apart from some stores, PDL stores close every Tuesday. — [ctdsb, Oct 2026](https://www.ctdsb.net/c1476_202610/2873236.html) (via search summary; fetch failed)
- **"Unhappy leave" (不开心假):** 10 days, announced March 2024 at the China Supermarket Week forum. Managers may not refuse it. — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3578699.html); [CBNData](https://cbndata.com/information/293220) (via search summary)
- **Grievance award (委屈奖):**
  - March 2023: PDL confirmed the award exists and pays **RMB 500–5,000** depending on the case, not the rumoured 5,000–8,000. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/2397143292534150); [China.com](https://news.china.com/socialgd/10000169/20230630/45125458.html)
  - June 2023 incident: an employee who was shouted at received RMB 5,000. The customer was offered a RMB 500 complaint award and refused it. — [China.com](https://news.china.com/socialgd/10000169/20230630/45125458.html)
  - July 2024 policy: an employee who is unjustly accused gets RMB 5,000+, insulted RMB 10,000+, assaulted RMB 30,000+. — [iFeng](https://news.ifeng.com/c/8oMD46Srhe7) (via search summary). A later (Feb) scheme reportedly sets level-1 compensation at RMB 10k–50k. Tiers differ between reports and the original was not verified.
  - A claim of "up to RMB 100k" comes from low-reliability sources only.
- **Turnover:** H1 2026 staff attrition was **0.50%**. — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4895020.html) (via search summary)
- **Career rules:**
  - Top management must leave power positions **before age 60**, and base-level branch managers by 45.
  - Rotation: store manager and below rotate daily, format GMs monthly, the group GM quarterly.
  - — [TMTPost](https://www.tmtpost.com/7880945.html); [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3711244692484617) (via search summary)
- **Non-cash rewards:** Yu reportedly bought 310 Great Wall Tank 700 SUVs to reward the team (2026). — [Sina Weibo/news](https://www.sina.cn/weibo/detail/5366201264112360.html) (headline only)

### Inferences
- **Labor cost estimate:** 10,194 staff × RMB 9,598 × 12 ≈ **RMB 1.17 bn per year in average take-home pay**, about 5% of 2025 sales. Adding employer social insurance, taxes and the management/technical pay tier (718 + 563 people at up to ~RMB 700k) could push total people cost to roughly **7–9% of sales**. This is an analyst estimate, not disclosed. It is low as a % of sales because sales per employee is so high (~RMB 2.3 m).
- 9,598 RMB ≈ USD 1,350 ≈ IDR 22 m per month.
- The 50% profit-to-team rule means part of the compensation is variable and self-funding.

### Gaps
- No official Xuchang retail average wage was found for a like-for-like comparison.
- The "average" wage definition (whether it includes bonuses, is pre- or post-tax, and whether it covers all staff) is inconsistent across reports.
- No disclosed labor cost % of revenue.
- Training system details (PDL's "学校型企业" concept) were not researched in depth.

## Q5. Customer-service policies with cost implications

### Takeaway
PDL publicly over-compensates customers when quality fails, which is costly in one-off cases but very cheap marketing. The 2024 擀面皮 (cold rice noodle) incident cost about RMB 8.83 m in customer payouts plus a RMB 100k whistleblower reward.

### Cited Findings
- **擀面皮 incident, June 2024:** a customer exposed poor hygiene at a concession food stall in the Xinxiang stores.
  - PDL refunded **and paid RMB 1,000 each** to customers who bought the noodles between 9–19 June: **8,833 claims, RMB 8.833 m in total**.
  - The complaining customer got **RMB 100k**.
  - Staff were dismissed or demoted and lost year-end benefits, and the vendor's contract was terminated.
  - — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/102170001.html); [China.com](https://news.china.com/socialgd/10000169/20240627/46759760.html); [BBT News](https://www.bbtnews.com.cn/2024/0627/519887.shtml); [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3181281.html)
- Customer complaint award: RMB 500 in the June 2023 case. — [China.com](https://news.china.com/socialgd/10000169/20230630/45125458.html)

### Inferences
- RMB 8.8 m is about 0.05% of 2024 sales, or ~1.1% of 2024 profit. The cost is small relative to the brand equity it bought.

### Gaps
- No-reason return policy terms, and free services (pet storage, microwaves, wheelchairs, medicine boxes), were not verified with cost data in this pass. No cost quantification was found.

## Q6. Consulting (帮扶调改), private label and other revenue streams

### Takeaway
PDL says it **charges nothing** for makeovers of Yonghui, Bubugao, Jiabaile and others, and absorbs its own costs. The economic link is the **DL private-label range**, supplied to makeover stores (reportedly at cost), which spreads PDL's sourcing scale. Private label is now roughly a third of PDL's own sales. No franchise or licensing revenue was found.

### Cited Findings
- Makeovers (调改) are free and involve no fees or returns, with PDL bearing the costs. The same applied to Jiabaile and Bubugao. DL products supplied to these stores are reportedly priced at cost. — [Winshang](https://m.winshang.com/news723621.html); [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3334756714096902). CIW notes fees are "not disclosed". — [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai)
- Yu said the assistance would run about two more years (from ~2025), after which PDL would stop sending teams. — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3334756714096902) (via search summary)
- **Private label (DL):**
  - Q1 2025: group sales RMB 5.866 bn, of which DL > RMB 1.76 bn (+42% YoY). — [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3503302210263940) (via search summary)
  - A WeChat-sourced article claims DL grew from ~RMB 75 m (2022) to RMB 3.862 bn (2025), ~30% of the total. **This conflicts with the Q1 2025 figure** (1.76 bn in one quarter implies more than 3.86 bn a year) and is unverified. — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4892294.html)
  - BofA research is quoted as putting private label at ~⅓ of sales, versus near zero in 2020. — [Huxiu](https://m.huxiu.com/article/4898676.html) (via search summary)
- DL goods sold through "胖永辉" (Yonghui), "胖物美" (Wumart) and other makeover stores had reached 41 cities, with a target of 100 cities in 2025. — [Winshang](https://m.winshang.com/news725565.html). At Bubugao's first makeover store (Changsha Meixihu), PDL products were 28.22% of store sales in May 2024. — same source

### Inferences
- The "free" makeover is a channel-building and goodwill play. The likely monetisation is DL wholesale volume (scale purchasing and supplier leverage) plus brand reach. A modest wholesale margin may exist, but "at cost" claims suggest it is minimal.

### Gaps
- No disclosure was found of DL wholesale revenue to third-party retailers, or its margin.
- No franchise, licensing or brand-fee revenue was found. None appears to exist.

## Q7. Competitive context

### Takeaway
PDL is small in absolute terms but has elite productivity per store. Its ~RMB 1.7–1.8 bn per store sits near Sam's Club China (~RMB 22 bn per store) and far above traditional hypermarkets. The chains copying it (Yonghui) are loss-making and paying heavily to change.

### Cited Findings
- **Yonghui:**
  - 2024: revenue ~RMB 67.6 bn, net loss ~RMB 1.47 bn. — [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai)
  - 2025: revenue **RMB 53.508 bn (−20.82%)**, attributable net loss **RMB 2.552 bn**, deducted (adjusted) loss RMB 3.42 bn. It deeply remodelled 284 stores (its earlier forecast said 315) and closed 381.
  - 2025 remodel costs: write-offs and one-offs ~RMB 910 m, plus ~RMB 300 m of lost gross profit during closures. Liability-to-asset ratio was 94.61%.
  - It guided to an H1 2026 profit of RMB 250 m.
  - — [Jiemian](https://m.jiemian.com/article/14265138.html); [Lanjinger](https://www.lanjinger.com/d/1776676769195786354); [10jqka](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20260729/c678498793.shtml)
- Yonghui's first remodelled store (Zhengzhou 信万广场) took RMB 1.88 m on reopening day, ~14× its prior daily average. More than 100 Yonghui stores had been remodelled by June 2025. — [Winshang](https://m.winshang.com/news723616.html); [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai)
- **Sam's Club China:** 2025 sales above RMB 140 bn (vs. RMB 100.5 bn in 2024) across 63 stores, with more than 10.7 m paid members. The figures come from market sources, not Walmart disclosure. — [Winshang](https://m.winshang.com/news738163.html); [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3717112864126593)
- **Industry:** the CCFA top-100 supermarkets had RMB 900 bn sales in 2024 across 25,200 outlets (+0.3%). — [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai)

### Inferences
- **Sales per store, 2025:** PDL ~RMB 1.7–1.8 bn vs. Sam's ~RMB 2.2 bn vs. CCFA top-100 average ~RMB 36 m (900 bn / 25,200, 2024).
- **Net margin:** PDL ~4.7% (2024, self-reported) vs. Yonghui −4.8% (2025).

### Gaps
- No verified like-for-like data was gathered for Bubugao, Wumart, Costco China or Hema (sales density, margins, wages).

## Q8. Sustainability, scalability and risks

### Takeaway
The main risks are key-person and culture dependence (Yu retired to an adviser role in Feb 2026 but keeps ~70% control), extreme geographic and store concentration (Xuchang and Xinxiang; one store ≈ 22–25% of sales), traffic that may depend on hype and daigou, and reputational exposure from radical transparency.

### Cited Findings
- **Succession:**
  - On 11 Feb 2026 Yu announced his retirement to an adviser role. A decision committee of internally developed managers (e.g. 宋占伟, 冯延军) now runs the company on rotation, while Yu keeps the chair and 69.96%.
  - Commentators question whether a highly personalised culture can survive the founder's exit.
  - — [TMTPost](https://www.tmtpost.com/7880945.html); [36Kr](https://www.36kr.com/p/3711244692484617); [GMW](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-02/11/content_1304341087.htm)
- CIW sees the model as dependent on founder-driven culture and fourth-tier-city economics, with scalability "questions persisting". — [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai)
- An academic case study finds PDL unclear about its competitive advantages and positioning. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/376131980_Case_Study_Based_on_Pang_Donglai_Trading_Group)
- Commentary on the 35-hour week says the model is hard to replicate because it relies on profit and cash flow plus no blind expansion. — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4895020.html)
- **Reputational and legal risk:** the 249-yuan towel controversy and lawsuit against a blogger (Apr 2026). — [Eastmoney](https://finance.eastmoney.com/a/202604303726098354.html)

### Inferences
- **Concentration:** in 2026 YTD, Times Square + Angel City = RMB 9.06 bn ≈ 35% of group sales.
- **Copyability:** the copyable elements for an Indonesian concept are cost-transparent pricing, high-wage/low-turnover staffing, a strong private label and over-compensation service recovery. The non-copyable ones are founder charisma, national media virality and a 25-year accumulated trust base.

### Gaps
- No sell-side (e.g. BofA) report on PDL was directly accessed.
- No independent audit or regulatory filing exists to verify any figure.
