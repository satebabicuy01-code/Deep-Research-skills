## Q1. What is Pang Dong Lai? (Checking the user's description, "a supermarket in China called Pang Dong Lai")

### Takeaway
The user's description is only partly right. Pang Dong Lai is not just a supermarket. It is a regional **multi-format retail group**: 许昌市胖东来商贸集团有限公司 (Xuchang Pangdonglai Commerce & Trade Group Co., Ltd.). It runs 14 stores (as of 2025–2026), all in Henan province. The formats are supermarkets, department stores and malls, community supermarkets, a clothing and footwear store, jewelry counters (gold and jade), and earlier an appliance store. Supermarkets bring in the most revenue. The correct spellings are **胖东来 / Pangdonglai** (also written "Pang Donglai"; short brand "PDL"). Avoid "Pang Dong Lai" in formal writing.

### Cited Findings
- **Name and romanization**: In Chinese it is 胖东来 (pinyin: Pàng Dōnglái). English Wikipedia uses "Pangdonglai". Baidu Baike English uses "Pang Donglai" and "PDL". The legal entity is "Xuchang Pang Donglai Trade Group Co., Ltd." (许昌市胖东来商贸集团有限公司). — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai); [Baidu Baike EN – Pang Donglai](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Pang%20Donglai/3205808); [Baidu Baike EN – Xuchang Pang Donglai Trade Group](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Xuchang%20Pang%20Donglai%20Trade%20Group%20Co.,%20Ltd./986179)
- **Founder**: 于东来 (Yú Dōnglái). — [Wikipedia EN – Yu Donglai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yu_Donglai)
- **Formats**: Its formats include supermarkets, department stores, clothing and footwear stores, community supermarkets and shopping centres. — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- An earlier format breakdown of 13 stores in Xuchang and Xinxiang: 5 department-store or mall-type "综合型百货商场", 7 mid-sized community supermarkets, and 1 apparel and footwear store. [Likely 2023–2024 count] — [Chinese search summary of media reports; see 36Kr](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368)
- **2025 sales by format**: supermarkets ¥12.643 bn; jewelry ¥2.451 bn; department stores ¥2.379 bn. These three add up to only about ¥17.5 bn of the ¥23.5 bn total. The source does not explain the rest, which may be apparel, appliances, food service or other categories. — [楚天都市报 / ctdsb, Jan 2026](https://www.ctdsb.net/c1476_202601/2629667.html)
- **Appliances**: The 电器城 (appliance store) opened in May 2006. It later moved to the 4th floor of 天使城 (Angel City). The standalone Appliance City closed on 2 January 2023. — [Wikipedia ZH](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%83%96%E4%B8%9C%E6%9D%A5%E5%95%86%E8%B4%B8%E9%9B%86%E5%9B%A2); [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- **Jewelry and jade**: The jewelry department (珠宝部) sells 和田玉 (Hetian jade) and 翡翠 (jadeite). Jade sales in Q1 2025 were ¥21.9 m, at a 20% gross margin, or 0.34% of group sales. — [Sina Finance, 7 May 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)

### Inferences
- For an Indonesian store-concept team, the better comparison is a regional department-store-plus-hypermarket group (a mix of something like Matahari Department Store and a hypermarket) with very strong service standards. "One supermarket" understates it. Its famous flagships, Times Square (时代广场) and Angel City (天使城), are multi-floor mall or department-store complexes. The largest is around 70,000 m².

### Gaps
- No official, audited per-format store floor-area table was found. Official PDL figures come from Yu Donglai's own social-media posts. The company is private and does not publish audited accounts.
- I could not confirm whether pharmacy counters exist. I found no reliable source on a pharmacy format.

## Q2. Founding and early history (1995 start, 1998 fire, naming)

### Takeaway
Yu Donglai opened **望月楼胖子店** (Wàngyuèlóu Pàngzi Diàn, "Wangyuelou Fatty's Shop") in Xuchang in **March 1995**. It was a store of about 40 m² selling sweets, tobacco and alcohol. In **August 1997** it was renamed **胖东来烟酒有限公司**, and its first branch opened in October 1997. On **15 March 1998** the flagship store was hit by an arson fire that killed 8 people. The fire is the key moment in the company's founding story. The 1995, 1997 and 1998 dates appear consistently across sources. The fire's loss amount does not.

### Cited Findings
- March 1995: Yu Donglai founded 望月楼胖子店 in Xuchang, a shop of just over 40 m² selling sweets, tobacco and alcohol. Beforehand he had been laid off and had sold ice pops and worked as a miner. He promised genuine goods and returns for any dissatisfied customer. — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- Starting capital: he borrowed ¥10,000 from his elder brother and sister-in-law and pooled about ¥60,000 with four laid-off workers. [Secondary or self-published source; treat as narrative] — [Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/26492858675)
- August 1997: the shop was renamed 胖东来烟酒有限公司. October 1997: the first branch opened, marking the move into supermarket retail. — [Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/26492858675); [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai) (gives October 1997 for the first branch)
- **Fire of 15 March 1998**: arson at the 望月楼 store in central Xuchang, with petrol used as an accelerant. Reports blame a local gang after Yu threw out members who had caused trouble in the shop. **8 people died.** — [Zhihu narrative](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/26492858675); [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/915705263_121161827)
- **Loss figures conflict**: one source says over ¥3 m in goods, another over ¥5 m, another over ¥1 m. None is authoritative. — [Sina](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7857201856_1d45362c001901jzny.html); [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/735991663_120548298)
- After the fire: by late 1998 Yu reopened with about ¥500k in savings plus loans. A popular story says an elderly woman he did not know brought him ¥20,000 in cash. [Anecdotal and unverified] — [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/735991663_120548298)
- Later milestones: May 1999, large general-merchandise format; Aug 2001, 劳动路店 (Laodong Rd); 1 Jan 2002, 生活广场 (Life Plaza), the first big flagship; Dec 2005, entry into Xinxiang with a department store; Mar 2007, Xinxiang Life Plaza (二胖); 25 Apr 2009, Xuchang 时代广场 (Times Square). — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai); [Wikipedia ZH](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%83%96%E4%B8%9C%E6%9D%A5%E5%95%86%E8%B4%B8%E9%9B%86%E5%9B%A2)
- **Pulling back in 2012**: PDL closed 13 profitable Xuchang supermarkets, including 五一店, 六一店 and 新许店. Yu said expansion had outrun the company's management capacity, and more stores closed in 2014. Store count fell from a peak of "30-plus" to 13. — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai); [Wikipedia ZH](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%83%96%E4%B8%9C%E6%9D%A5%E5%95%86%E8%B4%B8%E9%9B%86%E5%9B%A2). Note: Huxiu dates the deliberate downsizing to 2013, so the year differs by source. — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4539198.html)
- Dec 2015: the original Xinxiang department store closed when its 10-year lease ended. Jan 2016: PDL bought about 38,000 m² of property in Xinxiang for ¥196 m. Sept 2016: it reopened as 胖东来·大胖 (Dapang). — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)

### Inferences
- The name 胖东来 appears to combine "胖子" (fatty), carried over from the first shop's name, with Yu's given name 东来. I found no primary source that explains the naming. Present this as a likely reading, not a fact.
- The fire and comeback story ("the community helped him rebuild, so he repays society") is central to PDL's culture of gratitude and reciprocity. Much of the detail comes from narrative articles, not contemporaneous reporting.

### Gaps
- No contemporary (1998) news report or court record of the fire was found. The death toll of 8 and the loss amounts come from later secondary articles.
- No primary source on why the name was chosen.

## Q3. Current store network, scale, employees and expansion policy (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
As of November 2026, PDL has **14 stores**: about 10 in Xuchang city, 1 in Yuzhou (禹州, a county-level city under Xuchang) and 3 in Xinxiang (新乡). It has **no stores outside Henan**. Sales were **¥23.53 bn in 2025** (+38.7%) and **over ¥25.8 bn in 2026 up to 15 November**. Headcount is "8,000+" (Yu's figure, 2025) to **10,194** (Wikipedia/Baidu, 2026). In 2026 the company is shrinking in one place and growing in another. **Life Plaza (Xuchang) closes in December 2026** because of a lease dispute. The **first Zhengzhou store** has slipped repeatedly and is now expected around **March–April 2027**. A large **梦之城 (Dream City)** complex in Xuchang began construction in May 2026, with opening targeted for 2029.

### Cited Findings
**Store count and list**
- Dec 2025: 14 physical stores, of which 10 are in Xuchang city, 1 in Yuzhou and 3 in Xinxiang. — [Wikipedia ZH](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%83%96%E4%B8%9C%E6%9D%A5%E5%95%86%E8%B4%B8%E9%9B%86%E5%9B%A2); English Wikipedia counts this as "11 in Xuchang area + 3 in Xinxiang" — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- The 14th store, 三胖 (Sanpang) in Xinxiang, about 68,300 m², began trial operations on 23 December 2025. It was the first new store in about two years, and it recorded ¥92.4 m in sales in its opening period, with the headline claiming "nearly ¥100 m in one week". — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai); [ctdsb, Jan 2026](https://www.ctdsb.net/c1476_202601/2629667.html); [天府财经](https://www.tfcaijing.com/article/page/7a496535356545474b314532475447503675506733413d3d)

Sales passed ¥20 bn in November 2025 anyway. — [ctdsb](https://www.ctdsb.net/c1476_202601/2629667.html)
- **Zhengzhou**: entry was announced on 23 Feb 2025, with a site at 经纬华悦广场 near Zhengzhou East Station. The opening slipped from around New Year 2026, to May Day 2026, to October 2026. Yu said: "我们也想为河南去做点贡献，为郑州去献一份力". — [Sohu, Nov 2026](https://www.sohu.com/a/1102691217_100293569); [Wikipedia ZH](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%83%96%E4%B8%9C%E6%9D%A5%E5%95%86%E8%B4%B8%E9%9B%86%E5%9B%A2); [Sina Finance, May 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2025-05-14/doc-inewnuth1242886.shtml)
- In September 2026, the developer of Zhengzhou's 宏方 (Hongfang) complex announced a **second** Zhengzhou PDL store. — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- **Life Plaza closure**: announced in Yu's livestream on 7 August 2026. The 24-year-old store, which earns about ¥100 m in profit and about ¥2 bn in sales a year (2025 sales cited as ¥824 m elsewhere, a **conflict**), will close in **December 2026** when its lease ends. Yu blamed unfair rent terms ("触及公平底线"), not poor performance. — [Sina Finance](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/7880068914/1d5b04f3206801getk); [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1102691217_100293569); [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai); [Foodtalks](https://www.foodtalks.cn/en/news/57756)
- **梦之城 (Mengzhicheng / Dream City)**, Xuchang: construction began on 20 May 2026, with debut planned for 2029. One source says September 2029. — [Wikipedia ZH](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/%E8%83%96%E4%B8%9C%E6%9D%A5%E5%95%86%E8%B4%B8%E9%9B%86%E5%9B%A2); [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)

### Inferences
- The "won't expand" image is overstated. PDL is still entirely in Henan and growing slowly. Yet in 2025–2026 it opened the giant Sanpang, committed to two Zhengzhou stores (its first move to the provincial capital) and started a 2029 mega-project. Its strategy is slow, deep, local expansion. It has no plan to go nationwide. Instead it "exports" its model by consulting for and remodelling other chains (covered by other researchers).
- Its sales density is extraordinary. About ¥23.5 bn across 14 stores (2025) is roughly ¥1.7 bn per store on average.

### Gaps
- No audited financial statements exist. All sales and profit figures come from Yu Donglai or company posts.
- The Life Plaza revenue conflict (¥824 m vs. about ¥2 bn) is unresolved.
- No authoritative per-store floor-area list was found, except Angel City (about 70,000 m²), Sanpang (about 68,300 m²) and Zhengzhou (about 49,000 m², planned).

## Q4. Why is it so crowded? Retail tourism, the Xuchang tourism effect, social media, and endorsements by business leaders

### Takeaway
PDL has become a **destination in its own right**. Netizens call it "a 6A scenic spot with no off-season" ("没淡季的6A景区"). China's highest official scenic-spot grade is 5A, so "6A" is a joke. Holiday visitor numbers rival or exceed Xuchang's population. For National Day 2025, Xuchang said PDL's Xuchang stores received over 4.5 m visits in 8 days, against 7.76 m tourist visits citywide. The city runs free shuttle buses for tourists going to PDL. Endorsements by business leaders (Lei Jun around 2017 and Jack Ma) and constant Douyin and Xiaohongshu content keep the myth going. All visitor figures come from the local government or the company and are not independently audited.

### Cited Findings
**Holiday visitor and sales figures**
- **2025 Spring Festival**: Xuchang received 8.544 m tourist visits and over ¥5.9 bn in tourism revenue. PDL daily revenue topped ¥100 m. PDL drew 1.775 m visitors from 2 to 4 February 2025. The Xuchang government also claimed "approximately 30,000 overseas visitors daily", with top source countries USA, Singapore, Thailand, Nigeria and others. **Flag**: 30,000 foreign visitors a day looks implausible. It may be a translation error for "out-of-town" (外地) visitors. — [Xuchang Government (English), 11 Feb 2025](https://xuchang.gov.cn/English/20250211/ec903af6-3fb6-41fc-828f-771058d70343.html)
- **2025 Mid-Autumn plus National Day (8 days)**: Xuchang received 7.76 m tourist visits and ¥5.28 bn in tourism revenue. PDL's Xuchang stores received over 4.5 m visits with sales of about ¥820 m. CCTV gave a different scope: "Xuchang and Xinxiang PDL stores together over 4 m visits, more than 80% from outside the area". — [NetEase / Xuchang tourism report](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KBEDH8FG0550G7XH.html); [Chinese search summary of CCTV reports]. Peak day: on 3 Oct 2025, the Xuchang store saw over 300,000 visits in a day and queues of 3+ hours. — [Tencent News, 3 Oct 2025](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20251003A05YUD00)
- Xuchang introduced **free bus services** to carry out-of-town tourists to PDL. — [Sina](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5293548733465402.html); [The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_31917995)
- "没淡季的6A景区" ("a 6A scenic spot with no off-season") and "一个超市带火了一座城" ("one supermarket made a whole city popular") are widely used media framings. Media in April 2026 (ahead of May Day 2026) were still using "6A景区". — [Sina](https://k.sina.cn/article_2258727970_m86a1742203302emi2.html?from=travel); [Sina Finance, 28 Apr 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-04-28/doc-inhwaawa8388135.shtml)

**"Study tours" and business pilgrimages**
- A Xuchang tour operator told Sixth Tone her agency alone has hosted 80,000+ clients over three years, many travelling only to study PDL. Sixth Tone calls it a model "people now pay to study". — [Sixth Tone](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1018429)

**Endorsements by business leaders (verified only via secondary sources)**
- **Lei Jun (雷军, Xiaomi)**: around 2017 he posted photos on Weibo from a visit to Times Square, used the word "朝圣" (pilgrimage), and said "胖东来，在中国零售业一直是神般存在" ("PDL has always been a god-like presence in Chinese retail"). — [ifeng Finance, Aug 2017](https://finance.ifeng.com/a/20170822/15592423_0.shtml); [Wikipedia EN ref title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- **Jack Ma (马云)**: widely quoted as saying PDL "引发了中国零售商的新思考，是中国企业的一面旗子" ("it has prompted new thinking among Chinese retailers; it is a flag for Chinese enterprises"). — [36Kr (2022)](https://www.36kr.com/p/2007112831542150); [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4810347.html). **Not verified**: I found no original video, speech or post with this quote, nor its date. - 叶国富 (Ye Guofu, founder of MINISO) also visited Yu Donglai. — [Chinese search summary citing 中国企业家]

**What drives the visits (the service and product "magic" stories that go viral)**
- Known for exceptional customer service and staff welfare. — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- Own-brand products (the famous giant mooncake, beef jerky, beer, tea) became hot items for 代购 (resellers who buy on others' behalf). Resellers earn about 10% commission, up to "¥1,000 a day". — [Huxiu, 12 Dec 2024](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3786155.html); [Jiemian](https://www.jiemian.com/article/12126245.html)

### Inferences
- Crowds come from a loop. Extreme service and trust stories go viral on Douyin and Xiaohongshu. Business leaders' "pilgrimages" add authority. Holiday trips by train or car from other provinces follow. Local government then promotes PDL as Xuchang's top attraction with free buses and tourism stats. Paid "study tours" from other retailers add to the traffic.
- For an Indonesian concept, the crowding comes from **trust, service and the "Henan-only" scarcity**. Store design alone does not explain it. PDL cannot be visited elsewhere, so people make the trip.

- 2024 holiday figures (Spring Festival, May Day) and May Day 2026 numbers were not retrieved in this pass.
- No quantitative data was found on Douyin or Xiaohongshu content volume or hashtag views.

## Q5. Culture and philosophy ("自由·爱", not chasing scale)

### Takeaway
Yu Donglai presents PDL as a values-driven organisation. Its creed is **"自由·爱" ("Freedom · Love")**. He calls the company more an "educational institution" than a profit machine. Staff are well paid, rules are voted on by employees, growth is capped on purpose, and senior leaders must retire by 60. Yu announced his own retirement on **11 February 2026** (taking effect after Chinese New Year 2026). He became an adviser, and a **胖东来决策委员会** (PDL decision committee) now runs the company.

### Cited Findings
- Yu proposed "自由·爱" as the corporate creed and positions the company as an "教育机构" (educational institution), not just a profit-making organisation. — [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4539198.html)
- Many rules are decided by staff votes, and staff score their superiors. — [Chinese search summary; Digitaling](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1608319.html)
- Scale restraint: PDL closed 13 profitable stores in 2012 because expansion exceeded management capacity. In 2025 Yu said he would try to cap sales at ¥20 bn to avoid staff overtime. — [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai); [ctdsb](https://www.ctdsb.net/c1476_202601/2629667.html)

## Q6. Controversies and how PDL responded

### Takeaway
PDL's crisis responses are themselves part of its brand. The 2024 **擀面皮 (ganmianpi, rolled cold noodles) hygiene incident** ended with about ¥8.83 m in refunds and compensation plus a ¥100k reward to the whistle-blower. The 2025 **jade "暴利" (profiteering) accusation** was answered with margin disclosure, a lawsuit, open third-party testing, and a city regulator's inspection finding average margins of 20% or less. The 2024 **彩礼 (bride-price) guidance** was clarified as "advocacy only, not a rule". **Resellers and fake livestreams** prompted purchase limits and anti-counterfeit statements.

### Cited Findings
**擀面皮 hygiene incident (June 2024)**
- Mid-June 2024: a netizen video showed poor hygiene at a concession stall's processing site in PDL Xinxiang. On 26 June PDL shut all concession stalls in the two Xinxiang stores' food departments. The investigation report came out early on 27 June 2024. — [CLS](https://www.cls.cn/detail/1716005); [BBT News](https://www.bbtnews.com.cn/2024/0627/519887.shtml); [Guangming](https://m.gmw.cn/2024-06/28/content_1303775542.htm)
- Remedy: the whistle-blowing customer received a ¥100,000 reward. Customers who bought ganmianpi or 香辣面 between 9 and 19 June 2024 got a refund plus ¥1,000 each. That covered **8,833 portions, totalling ¥8.833 m**. Wikipedia rounds this to "¥8.8 m (US$1.2 m)". — [BBT News](https://www.bbtnews.com.cn/2024/0627/519974.shtml); [Wikipedia EN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pangdonglai)
- Staff discipline: the Xinxiang food department's first supervisor and the QC officer were dismissed, managers at team-leader level and above were removed from their posts, and the store manager's annual bonus was cancelled. The vendor was shut down, its contract terminated, and legal action reserved. A food-safety whistle-blower reward scheme was announced. — [CLS](https://www.cls.cn/detail/1716005); [Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3181281.html)
- Note on the "100x compensation" framing: the payout was a refund plus a flat ¥1,000 per purchase, and the 6-page report has five sections. The "100x" description is a popular simplification. The ratio depends on the item price, which was not given.

**Jade "profiteering" controversy (April–May 2025)**
- April 2025: influencer "柴怼怼" claimed PDL sold jade costing a few hundred yuan for tens of thousands, calling it a "智商税" (a tax on gullible buyers). PDL sued for commercial defamation and reputational damage, and the court accepted the case. — [Sina Finance, 7 May 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)
- On 9 April PDL disclosed jade figures: Q1 sales ¥21.9 m, 20% gross margin, 0.34% of group sales. On 10 April it opened third-party testing, with a full refund if a customer disputes quality or price. On 2 May it published its jade pricing standards, with mark-ups of 5–15% on "sensitive" goods. — [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml); [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A08BK800)
- On 6 May 2025 the Xuchang Market Supervision Bureau reported 4,177 Hetian jade items sold from January to April 2025, worth ¥29.592 m. The average gross margin was **20% or less**. Prices were properly displayed, and the 13 sampled items had complete purchase records and valid certificates. — [The Paper](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30777223); [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)
- On 8 May PDL issued a jade and jadeite return policy with no deduction of fees or taxes. Demand then spiked and purchases moved to online appointments. Yu said that if 柴怼怼 were not held accountable, he would close PDL or leave it for good ("退圈", leaving the scene). — [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A07OYT00); [Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)

**彩礼 (bride-price) guidance (late 2024)** [OLDER]
- PDL encouraged staff not to demand or pay high bride prices. The local labour department said it was an internal matter and not illegal. PDL said it was "只是倡导，未形成制度" ("only advocacy, not a formal rule"). — [现代快报 PDF, 22 Nov 2024](https://szb.xdplus.cn/xdkb/pc/attachment/202411/22/f53cb147-9f8d-44e0-8fb6-18da7dac8a98.pdf)

**Resellers (代购), scalpers and fake livestreams** [OLDER, late 2024]
- Own-brand goods drew organised resellers and scalpers. PDL imposed **purchase limits**, which forced large resellers to gather more membership accounts. Resellers then used self-checkouts to get around the limits. Many "PDL" livestream channels are unofficial, and PDL has repeatedly cracked down on them and issued public statements. — [Huxiu, 12 Dec 2024](https://www.huxiu.com/article/3786155.html); [Winshang](https://m.winshang.com/news729666.html)

### Inferences
- The pattern is the same each time: fast, public, over-compensating responses backed by data (refund plus ¥1,000, margin disclosure, open third-party testing). This turns scandals into trust-building moments.

### Gaps
- The outcome of the 柴怼怼 defamation lawsuit was not found.
- No 2025–2026 updates were found on reseller limits or livestream bans.
- 红内裤 (the "red underwear" item in the brief) could not be verified. I found no source and may be misremembering the event name.
