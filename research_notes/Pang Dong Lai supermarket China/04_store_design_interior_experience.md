# Pang Dong Lai (胖东来): Store Design, Interior, Layout, Visual Merchandising and In-Store Experience

Research date: 20 November 2026. Each claim carries its report date where known. **Overall caveat:** PDL does not publish architectural specs for its own stores: no aisle widths, lux levels, color temperatures, or material schedules. Hard numbers mostly come from (a) media floor/area figures and (b) the PDL-guided 调改 (makeover) stores at Yonghui, Bubugao, Zhongbai, Wumart and others, where media reported before/after dimensions. Where a number exists only for a makeover store, these notes say so.

---

## 1. Store portfolio and formats (sizes, floors, dates, designers, 2024–2026 pipeline)

### Takeaway
As of late 2026, PDL runs about 14 stores in two Henan cities (Xuchang, Xinxiang), anchored by mall-scale "综合体" flagships: Xuchang 时代广场, 天使城, 生活广场; Xinxiang 大胖, 小胖, and since Dec 2025 三胖. Each flagship has a ~3,000–3,200 m² supermarket inside a 40,000–78,000 m² building. The pipeline is changing fast. 生活广场 closes in Dec 2026 and is being replaced by an underground 科技广场 store (~43,000 m²). A Zhengzhou East Station store (~50,000 m², 10,000+ m² supermarket, "international first-class design team") has slipped to Mar/Apr 2027. The 6.5bn-RMB "梦之城" mega-complex started construction in May 2026, with completion planned for 2029. No architect is publicly confirmed for PDL's existing stores. One unverified report names Aedas for 梦之城.

### Cited Findings

**Store suppliers**
- PDL's shopping trolleys are supplied by Wanzl. The EL115 model was reportedly chosen for sturdy styling and easy handling — (search snippet; the reference page returned 404 on fetch) [Wanzl reference](https://wanzl.com/en_DE/references/pangdonglai-cn~s2495)

### Inferences
- PDL's format is a self-operated mall plus supermarket, not a standalone hypermarket. The grocery box is relatively small (~3,000–3,200 m² in Xuchang flagships) inside a much larger destination building. The newest projects are scaling the supermarket up (10,000+ m² in Zhengzhou, 20,000+ m² of sales floor at 科技广场) and moving underground, linked to transit (Zhengzhou East Station tunnel, 科技广场 B1).
- The shift from leased buildings to owned or self-built ones (bought 大胖 property, 梦之城, 生活广场 exit) is partly driven by landlord risk. That is a relevant lesson for Indonesian operators deciding between leased mall anchors and owned boxes.
- The use of "international first-class design teams" for Zhengzhou and 梦之城 is new. Older stores appear to have been designed and fit out in-house, though this is not documented.

### Gaps
- No official floor-by-floor directory for 时代广场 or 生活广场 was retrieved, and the PDL official site (pdl.com.cn) returned HTTP 503 during this research.
- No architect or interior designer is publicly named for any existing PDL store. Aedas for 梦之城 is unverified.
- 科技广场 opening date, floor plan and design are unknown as of Nov 2026.
- No published renovation history (dates/scope) for 时代广场 or 大胖 was found.
- 三胖 interior design details were not found in fetched sources. A search snippet claimed another chain (欧德福) copied 三胖's style, citing "simple colors, ultra-wide-angle spotlights, a flower zone at the escalator landing", but the source could not be confirmed.

## 2. Layout and circulation (aisles, shelf heights, flow, adjacency, entrance, checkout, queues)

### Takeaway
The core PDL layout principles are wide aisles (deliberately giving up a shelf row), low shelves (~1.6 m) for sightlines, no forced one-way circulation path (取消强制动线), no promotional floor stacks (堆头), and a large share of floor space for fresh, bakery, deli and on-site processing. These are confirmed mainly through the PDL-led makeovers. Typical figures there are main aisles 3–5 m (from <2 m), secondary aisles ~3 m, and shelves cut from 1.8–2.4 m to 1.6 m. PDL's own stores have no published aisle or shelf measurements.

### Cited Findings
- PDL deliberately keeps wide spacing between shelves, "even at the cost of one fewer shelf row" (宁可少摆一排货架) — [21经济网/砺石商业评论 "100 details", 2026-09-24](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html)
- Bloomberg describes PDL aisles as wider and more open than the cramped ones common in Chinese supermarkets — [Bloomberg via BNN, 2024-07-30](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/business/international/2024/07/30/chinas-mega-grocers-turn-to-an-upstart-to-win-back-shoppers)
- Yonghui's official PDL makeover rules (May 2024): restructure the layout, remove the mandatory one-way route, widen main and secondary aisles, clear crowded 堆头, and optimize display positions — [第一财经](https://www.yicai.com/news/102126019.html)
- Yonghui 郑州信万广场店 after the makeover (reopened 19 Jun 2024 after 19 days): U-shaped plan, no forced route, wider aisles. Leased concessions and e-commerce were removed. A tobacco/liquor cabinet zone and customer rest area were added, and bakery, deli and on-site processing were enlarged — [新浪财经头条](https://t.cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/3587105661/d5cee37d001014k8q?finpagefr=p_103). Checkouts were added at both the entrance and the exit, with fewer stacks and clearer layout — (search snippet) same source
- Bubugao 长沙梅溪湖店 (makeover Apr 2024, 25 days): forced circulation removed, main aisles widened to 4–5 m, secondary aisles to 3 m, promo stacks removed, shelves lowered and shelf tiers simplified. Customer rest area, pet holding, hand-wash basins and stroller ramps were added — (search snippet; sources not fetched) [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/695869776); [中新网湖南, 2024-04-23](https://www.hn.chinanews.com.cn/news/shsh/2024/0423/490532.html). All advertising posters were removed storewide — same search summary
- 中百仓储关山店, Wuhan (reopened 20 Jul 2024): aisles widened from <2 m to 3–4 m, shelves lowered from 1.8 m to 1.6 m, promo stacks removed. About 6,000 SKUs were removed and about 80% of products replaced. The exit cart corral became a customer rest area with charging, drinking water and handwashing. Cashier stools and entrance/exit greeters were added — [武汉洪山区政府, 2024-07-23](https://www.hongshan.gov.cn/hdjl/hygq_1/202407/t20240723_2432093.shtml)
- Yonghui 昆明海乐世界店 (opened 17 Apr 2025): all shelves lowered from 2.4 m to 1.6 m, and main aisle widened so families with strollers pass easily (no figure given). Self-checkout was added and the checkout area reorganized — [赢商网](https://m.winshang.com/news732561.html)
- Yonghui Guangzhou PDL-model store: shelves lowered to 1.6 m — (search snippet) [澎湃新闻](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_31688244)
- Wumart 学清路店, Beijing (reopened 21 Mar 2025 after 40 days, 10m+ RMB): shopping aisles widened to 4 m. Shelf height is "basically at eye level". Fresh, deli and bakery expanded to about half of the 3,000+ m² store. A dedicated PDL-product zone sits immediately right of the entrance. Railings separate mall and store traffic, and entry is metered at about 40–50 people every half hour — [澎湃新闻](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30492830). Another Beijing Wumart makeover reports aisles "widened by nearly double" — (search snippet) [首都文明网](https://www.bjwmb.gov.cn/yw/10088116.html)
- Checkout staffing at PDL: each self-checkout station has 2 staff dedicated to bagging — [人民日报/中国经济周刊, 2024-07-15](http://paper.people.com.cn/zgjjzk/html/2024-07/15/nw.zgjjzk_20240715_2-02.htm); also [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html)
- Queue/crowd management at makeover openings: on a 37°C day at 信万广场, shade awnings, cooling fans and ice were provided for people queuing — [人民日报/中国经济周刊](http://paper.people.com.cn/zgjjzk/html/2024-07/15/nw.zgjjzk_20240715_2-02.htm)
- Staff walk shoppers to the item rather than pointing — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html)

### Inferences
- A practical "PDL-style" spec set, derived from the makeover stores rather than PDL's own drawings: gondolas ≤1.6 m (eye level for most adults, so the shopper sees across the store), main aisles 3–5 m, secondary aisles about 3 m, no floor stacks or promo islands, and a free-flow (not racetrack-forced) plan. A U-shape is used in at least one case. For Indonesian shoppers, whose average height is lower than northern Chinese shoppers, 1.4–1.5 m might give an equivalent sightline. That is an inference, not a PDL figure.
- Removing the exit cart corral or leased concessions to create rest areas shows PDL trades selling space for comfort space.
- Fresh, bakery and deli at roughly 50% of floor area (Wumart case) reflects the PDL category emphasis.

### Gaps
- No measured aisle widths, shelf heights, ceiling heights or gondola run lengths for PDL's own stores (时代广场, 天使城, 大胖, 三胖) were found. "Wide aisles" there is qualitative.
- No published PDL checkout counts or ratios (manned vs self) and no detailed department adjacency diagram for a PDL store.
- The Bubugao 4–5 m figure comes from a search snippet whose original source was not fetched.

## 3. Lighting

### Takeaway
PDL's documented lighting principle is true-color rendering, not "beautifying" fresh food: produce lighting was reportedly tuned over 100+ trials without raising brightness or shifting hue. Makeover stores consistently move to warm-tone, spotlight-focused lighting with light/dark rhythm. No PDL color temperatures, lux levels or fixture brands are published. China banned colored "生鲜灯" from 1 Dec 2023, so honest lighting is now also a legal requirement in China.

### Cited Findings
- Fresh-area lighting was reportedly adjusted 100+ times to show food's true color, without boosting brightness or shifting hues — [21经济网/砺石商业评论, 2026-09-24](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html)
- Media mention PDL repeatedly adjusting shelf spacing and lighting angles, but give no values — (search snippet) [腾讯新闻, 2026-09-04](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260904A07ZIK00)
- Wumart 学清路 makeover: lighting was "carefully reset and adjusted, becoming warm-toned" — [澎湃新闻](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30492830)
- Yonghui 昆明 makeover: warm, soft spotlights focus on products, with alternating brighter and dimmer zones to create depth — [赢商网](https://m.winshang.com/news732561.html)
- Another Beijing Wumart makeover used a "无主灯" design (no main ceiling luminaires, accent and linear lighting only) and relied on color coordination for display effect — (search snippet) [首都文明网](https://www.bjwmb.gov.cn/yw/10088116.html)
- China's revised rules ban "生鲜灯" (colored lights that alter fresh food appearance) from 1 Dec 2023 — [新浪财经, 2023-11-24](https://finance.sina.cn/2023-11-24/detail-imzvsncu5528398.d.html)
- Industry reference values (not PDL): one lighting vendor suggests 2700–3000K for bakery/deli and 4000–4500K for fresh produce — [宜琳照明](http://www.ilin-lighting.com/blog/7331-cn.html). A fixture maker suggests CRI Ra≥90 for fresh, ≥85 for food, ≥80 elsewhere — [极成光电 FAQ](https://geosheen.cn/sckjdgfaq/). These are vendor figures, not standards.

### Inferences
- For an Indonesian concept: high-CRI (Ra≥90), neutral-to-warm, non-tinted lighting in fresh, with warmer accents in bakery. Use spotlight accenting on product rather than a uniformly bright ceiling. Do not use red or pink meat lights: PDL's credibility depends on what you see being what you get. The precise K values are industry practice, not PDL data.

### Gaps
- No PDL color temperature, illuminance (lux), CRI, fixture type or lighting supplier data was found for any department. Claims like "warm light for bakery" at PDL's own stores are not documented in fetched sources.

## 4. Materials, finishes, color palette, aesthetic

### Takeaway
Sources describe PDL stores as clean, bright, uncluttered and "通透" (transparent/open), with no advertising clutter. They do not specify materials, floor finishes, ceiling treatments or color values. Spending goes visibly into fixtures and amenities (e.g., Dyson wash/dry units) rather than decorative finishes.

### Cited Findings
- Angel City restrooms have 87 Dyson wash-and-dry units (1.45m RMB total, about 20,000 RMB each per the article), smart coated toilet seats, a dedicated children's restroom, and a women's dressing/makeup room — [新浪财经/联商, 2023-01-11](https://finance.sina.cn/2023-01-11/detail-imxzunxs2175844.d.html)
- The Bubugao makeover result was described as "通透、明亮、干净、整洁" (open, bright, clean, tidy), with all ad posters removed — (search snippet) [中新网湖南](https://www.hn.chinanews.com.cn/news/shsh/2024/0423/490532.html)
- PDL stores feel clean and comfortable, with goods neatly displayed — [BambooWorks](https://thebambooworks.com/a-supermarket-david-defeats-goliath-in-small-town-china-outracing-the-times)
- No promotional announcements over the loudspeakers — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html)
- Angel City's self-run coffee bar reportedly has no separate signboard and follows the cinema's blue-and-white minimalist style — (search snippet; source not identified among results, unverified)
- Commentators warn that many imitators copy decoration and slogans but ignore the operating logic, and that makeover investment averages "千万元级别" (around 10m RMB) per store — (search snippet) [36氪 / 虎嗅 results set](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4865921.html)
- The 梦之城 renderings show large "DL" logos as a brand element — [楚天都市报](https://www.ctdsb.net/c1476_202607/2809254.html)

### Inferences
- PDL's aesthetic is restraint: neutral background, product as hero, no POP clutter, quiet audio, and premium spend on touchpoints customers physically use (taps, hand dryers, carts, seating). Indonesian teams should budget accordingly rather than for decorative ceilings.

### Gaps
- No documentation of floor material (terrazzo, epoxy, tile, vinyl), ceiling type (open vs suspended), wall finishes, brand color codes, or fixture finishes for any PDL store. On-site observation or photo analysis would be needed.

## 5. Visual merchandising and information design

### Takeaway
PDL's VM is informational more than theatrical. Its signature devices are sweetness-ranked cut fruit, sugar-content labels, usage notes (e.g., which bean sprout for which dish), posted lab test reports, price tags showing cost and gross margin on own-brand goods, purchase limits, timed markdowns, and "rational consumption" signage. Magnifiers sit at shelves for older shoppers. A widely circulated "PDL eight display principles" list is not verifiably from PDL.

### Cited Findings
- Fruit is labeled with sugar content. Pre-cut fruit boxes are numbered by sweetness from high to low — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html); numbered sweetness labels outside the wrap are confirmed by [人民日报/中国经济周刊, 2024](http://paper.people.com.cn/zgjjzk/html/2024-07/15/nw.zgjjzk_20240715_2-02.htm)
- Cut fruit is marked down to 80% after 4 hours and 60% after 6 hours, and destroyed after 8 hours. Near-expiry food is discounted by time slot, with deep discounts on fresh produce and meat before closing — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html)
- Six to seven bean-sprout varieties are each labeled with their best culinary use. Grains come in transparent, uniform packaging. Labels show best-before date, supplier and an inspector's phone number — same source
- An in-house lab tests produce and meat, and reports are posted publicly. Live fish are held about 10 days before sale to purge impurities, and the seafood section reportedly runs at a loss — same source
- Own-brand price tags show cost price and gross margin. Example: a ¥599 down jacket with ¥457.9 cost and a 23.55% margin — same source
- A jewelry counter sign reads "理性消费" (rational consumption brings more happiness). Purchase limits apply, e.g., 12 steamed buns per person — same source
- No membership card: one price for everyone. Most promotions have been stopped — same source

- Magnifiers and reading glasses are kept on/at shelves to read ingredient lists — same source; [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dbe487f523fea46a1b1c09266993408b.html)

- The "PDL eight display principles" (FIFO, tag-to-product alignment, category grouping, vertical blocking when >4 variants, related display, color gradation, a 120–160 cm "golden zone", space proportional to sales) circulate widely. The 2023 Sohu article carrying them never actually references PDL in its body, so attribution is unverified — [搜狐, 2023-06-11](https://www.sohu.com/a/684482847_121123722)

### Inferences
- The VM toolkit an Indonesian team can borrow: (1) "how to choose / how to use" cards at fruit, vegetables, meat and seafood; (2) sweetness or ripeness grading on cut fruit and fruit (Brix labels); (3) posted lab or QC results; (4) transparent margin labels on own brand; (5) time-stamped markdown stickers on fresh-cut and deli; (6) limited, calm signage with no shouting promo POP.

### Gaps
- No PDL price-tag template (size, font, color), category-signage system, or wayfinding standard was found. No published produce-display specification (misting, ice beds, pyramid heights) was found for PDL.

Amenities are PDL's most documented design layer. Highlights: 7 cart types (including a seated elderly cart with magnifier and an infant cart); restrooms with an occupancy screen, red/green stall indicators, emergency buttons, more female than male stalls, and a stocked vanity; free pet storage with staff checks; a nursing room with warmer, sterilizer, crib and fridge; microwaves, water, power banks, umbrellas, wheelchairs and strollers; hand dryers at bagging counters; and free alterations, shoe repair and similar services (60+ free services claimed).

## 8. 调改 (makeover) physical changes as a proxy for PDL design principles

### Takeaway
Across Yonghui (from Jun 2024; 338 stores remodeled per Huxiu by late 2025/2026), Bubugao (from Apr 2024), Zhongbai (Jul 2024) and Wumart (Mar 2025), the recurring physical playbook is consistent:
1. Remove forced circulation.
2. Widen aisles: main 3–5 m, secondary ~3 m.
3. Lower shelves to about 1.6 m.
4. Remove floor stacks and ad posters.
5. Cut SKUs drastically (~70–81%) and replace most of the range.
6. Expand fresh, bakery, deli and on-site processing (to ~50% of floor).
7. Re-light warm, with accent spotlights.
8. Convert space to rest areas with water, microwaves, charging, hand basins and BP monitors.
9. Add PDL amenity kits (magnifiers, pet boxes, hand dryers, wash basins).
10. Add staff and staff rooms.

### Cited Findings
- Yonghui rules (published May 2024): delist 70% of products, reach 90%+ similarity to PDL's product structure, add PDL best-sellers (bakery, big mooncake, deli, DL juice/beer/oat/detergent), remove forced route, widen aisles, clear stacks — [第一财经](https://www.yicai.com/news/102126019.html); [界面新闻](https://www.jiemian.com/article/11231722.html)
- 信万广场 (about 3,100 m²) outcome: 10,841 SKUs delisted (81.3%), 12,581 SKUs re-planned of which 80% new. Day-one sales 1.88m RMB (13.9× prior daily average) with 12,926 visitors (5.3×) — [人民日报/中国经济周刊](http://paper.people.com.cn/zgjjzk/html/2024-07/15/nw.zgjjzk_20240715_2-02.htm); [第一财经](https://www.yicai.com/news/102126019.html)
- Bubugao 梅溪湖: daily sales from about 150k to 1.51m RMB, traffic from about 2,000 to 12,600 per day; 20 PDL-recommended products added — [人民日报/中国经济周刊](http://paper.people.com.cn/zgjjzk/html/2024-07/15/nw.zgjjzk_20240715_2-02.htm). Store area is given variously as 5,145 or 6,470 m² — (search snippet) [新浪财经, 2025-07-15](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-07-15/doc-inffpwtm5568630.shtml)
- Yonghui had remodeled 338 stores and still lost 82.69m RMB in Q3 — (search result headline) [虎嗅](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4901986.html)
- Zhongbai, Wumart, Yonghui Kunming and Guangzhou figures are as cited in Sections 2–3 and 6

### Inferences
- The makeover cases are the best proxy for a measurable spec, because the same PDL "一家亲" teams supervised them and media reported before/after numbers. Indonesian teams should treat 1.6 m shelves and 3–4 m main aisles as the transferable baseline. Amenity spend is low relative to the reported ~10m RMB per-store makeover cost, which is dominated by equipment and fit-out.

### Gaps
- A search snippet mentioned regulars at makeover stores feeling prices had risen, but the source URL could not be pinned down, so it is unverified.
- No detailed makeover drawings or official before/after plans were published. Restroom changes in Yonghui and Bubugao makeovers are not documented in fetched sources.
- Makeovers of 嘉百乐 and 物美 chains beyond the two Beijing stores were not researched in depth due to tool-call limits.

## 9. Publicly available PDL standards and manuals

### Takeaway
PDL has publicly disclosed management information since 24 Mar 2025 via its website, reportedly using Feishu dashboards and knowledge bases. However, the PDL site returned HTTP 503 during this research, so no official display or design standard could be read directly. Many "PDL manuals" circulating online (operations assessments, display principles, departmental 实操手册) are third-party compilations of uncertain provenance.

### Cited Findings
- From 24 Mar 2025, PDL began publishing sales data and management systems on its official site via Feishu (飞书) multi-dimensional dashboards and cloud-document knowledge bases, including by store, category and staff turnover — [新浪科技, 2025-03-26](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2025-03-26/doc-ineqwfyr9866716.shtml)
- An employee-handbook table of contents circulating online includes a "卫生间保洁服务标准" chapter, but content was not found — (search snippet) [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/6190075331)
- Third-party "胖东来超市部蔬果课实操手册" and similar documents are shared on document sites but lack official provenance — (search snippet) [人人文库](https://www.renrendoc.com/p-76137842.html)
- The "eight display principles" are unverified (see Section 5) — [搜狐](https://www.sohu.com/a/684482847_121123722)

### Inferences
- The most reliable route to PDL's real standards is direct access to pdl.com.cn's Feishu knowledge base (try again from mainland China or later), or PDL's official WeChat. The report writer should treat circulated manuals as illustrative only.

### Gaps
- Could not access pdl.com.cn (HTTP 503) to confirm which design, display or VM standards are officially published. No official PDL lighting, fixture or layout standard was retrieved.
- Could not verify the 263-page cleaning manual's existence or contents.
