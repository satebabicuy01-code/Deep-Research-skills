# Pang Dong Lai (胖东来, PDL) Makeovers (帮扶调改), Imitators, and Transferability — Research Notes (as of 20 Nov 2026)

Notes on sources: Most figures come from the companies themselves (Yonghui/Bubugao filings and IR statements, PDL/Yu Donglai social-media statements) as relayed by Chinese financial media. Independent checks are mostly media reporting and analyst commentary. Each bullet is dated. "Company-reported" = from filings/IR/PDL statements; "independent" = journalist/analyst assessment.

## 1. The PDL makeover program: who was helped, timeline, terms

### Takeaway
PDL's direct, hands-on 帮扶 (with dispatched teams) covered a small set of firms: 嘉百乐 (Jiangxi, from Jul 2023), 步步高 Bubugao (from Apr 2024), 永辉 Yonghui (from May 2024), plus ~10+ smaller regional chains from the 联商 "president class" (e.g., 湖北雅斯, 青海一家亲). PDL says help was free and cost it ~RMB 83m directly plus ~RMB 1.9bn in foregone sales from supplying its private label at cost. PDL stopped taking on new firms after Sept 2024. By 2026 the program was effectively winding down: Yu Donglai retired to an adviser role in Feb 2026, and Yonghui ended large-scale makeovers.

- PDL was Bubugao's #1 and #3 supplier in 2025, with combined purchases >RMB 300m. A rough estimate puts PDL goods at >10% of Bubugao's annual sales. — [蓝鲸财经 via 证券之星, 18 Sep 2026](https://wap.stockstar.com/detail/IG2026091800032140)

### Inferences
- The program was never a scalable consulting business. It was a free, founder-driven intervention concentrated in roughly 3 large firms plus a handful of small regional chains. Its two biggest "beneficiaries" also leaned heavily on PDL-supplied private label sold at cost, a subsidy that does not exist for an outside imitator.
- With Yu retired and PDL goods reportedly thinning on partner shelves, the "PDL-assisted" phase is ending. Partners' 2026+ results will test whether the model survives without PDL's people and products.

### Gaps
- No official full list of PDL-assisted firms exists. 新乐超市 and 金鹰 involvement is unverified (no sources found).
- No formal PDL statement that the program has ended in 2026. The Huxiu claim is unattributed.

## 2. Before-vs-after results and how sustained the uplift was

### Takeaway
Store-level opening uplifts were dramatic: day-one sales up 5–14x. They partly faded within months, but stores that stabilized stayed well above pre-makeover levels; Yonghui's cohort of 31 stores remodeled in 2024 grew sales 71% in Jan–Sep 2025 (company-reported). At company level, makeovers were very costly. Yonghui lost RMB 2.55bn in 2025 and returned to profit only in 2026, on a much smaller base (~383 stores vs. 1,057 peak). Bubugao is the cleanest "success": 21 remodeled stores, all profitable by Q1 2026, and adjusted profit positive in 2026. That success is concentrated in a small remaining network heavily stocked with PDL goods.

### Inferences
- The pattern is consistent across firms. Store-level KPIs (traffic, sales/m², NPS, gross margin) improve and largely persist beyond the launch spike, settling at perhaps ~50–60% of the opening peak but multiples of the old baseline. Corporate P&Ls deteriorate for 1–2 years, though, because of capex, closures, higher labor cost, and loss of back-end supplier fees.
- Yonghui's 2026 return to profit came alongside a ~60% smaller store base and ~half the headcount. It is not yet evidence that the model generates growth: revenue is still falling, and Q3 2026 was still loss-making before non-operating items.

## 3. What actually changed in makeovers

### Takeaway
The makeovers had five main components:
- Radical SKU churn: ~80% of old SKUs removed and replaced with fewer, higher-quality items, including PDL goods.
- Supplier restructuring: cutting middlemen, dropping back-end fees/slotting, and paying suppliers faster.
- Big wage increases plus more staff and paid leave.
- Wider aisles, better fresh displays and amenities.
- Transparent pricing (cost/markup disclosure in some cases) with no-questions returns.

### Cited Findings
- Yonghui Chongqing 南坪 store: 10,841 SKUs removed (81.3% of the total), >7,500 new items added, 45% of hardware renewed. Ordinary staff wages rose from ~RMB 3,000 to RMB 5,000+. Staff with one year of service get 10 paid vacation days. ~30 training sessions were held. — [界面, 23 Dec 2024](https://www.jiemian.com/article/12155812.html)
- 中百仓储 first store: 13,448 SKUs removed (80%), 6,336 new items, "一价到底" pricing, average price cut 20%. — [上游新闻, Sep 2024](https://c.m.163.com/news/a/JD1C82L2053469M5.html)
- Yonghui labor model:
  - Base pay rose from ~RMB 3,000 to RMB 4,500–5,000.
  - Headcount per store rose from ~100 to 150–250, adding an estimated RMB 9–18m per store per year in labor.
  - Frontline pay +50%; average pay per employee RMB 92.5k in 2024 vs. 80.7k (company-reported). — [新京报, 23 Jan 2026](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1769159897169603.html)
  - Employee dividends >RMB 31m distributed by Aug 2025. — [Caixin Global, 14 Oct 2025](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-10-14/yonghuis-makeover-draws-crowds-but-profits-lag-102371283.html)
- Yonghui supply chain:
  - Cut 60% of middlemen and concentrated on ~200 core suppliers, targeting procurement costs 15% lower.
  - Removed back-end supplier fees and shortened payment terms from 90 to 45 days.
  - An insider said retail prices had to rise to offset lost fee income. — [新京报, 23 Jan 2026](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1769159897169603.html)
  - Yonghui VP 佘咸平: under the old model ~70% of goods were standard items, and most profit came from back-end fees. — [钛媒体, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.tmtpost.com/8106328.html)
- Private label:
  - 品质永辉 developed 63 products and listed 57 in 2025, plus 500 manufacturer-customized items. H1 2026: RMB 583m = 2.32% of sales.
  - Disclosed cost/price example: orange juice priced at RMB 13.5 with RMB 10.86 product cost.
  - Bubugao "BL" brand covers about 20 categories. — [证券之星, 18 Sep 2026](https://wap.stockstar.com/detail/IG2026091800032140)
  - Yonghui's private-label share reached 10.94% in 9M 2026 (broader definition including 永辉定制). — [财联社, 30 Oct 2026](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/2868676035/aafc85c302001z6is)
  - Earlier (2025), Yonghui private-label annual sales were "only slightly over RMB 100m", and employees told 科技星球 the makeover copied PDL only superficially. — [search summary of BigGo/Tech Planet](https://finance.biggo.com/news/ctYN65sBpPlmmn5lFtVM)
- Yu Donglai pushed helped firms to give >50% of profits to staff, and to raise wages rather than bank profits ("赚了200万为什么不去涨工资"). — [上观新闻 via 网易, 29 Apr 2025](https://m.163.com/dy/article/JUA5UCK8055040N3.html)
- Customer pushback: Beijing 九宫 store shoppers said prices rose and a nearby mall was cheaper for the same produce. Yonghui is squeezed between Sam's Club and instant-delivery platforms. — [新京报, 23 Jan 2026](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1769159897169603.html)

### Inferences
- The economic core of a PDL-style makeover is a swap. Back-end supplier income (fees, slotting, long payment terms) is replaced by front-end gross margin earned from curated, quality, private-label goods. Labor cost is raised in exchange for productivity and service. Firms that do the visible parts (layout, SKU cuts, wages) without the margin rebuild get the cost without the payoff.

### Gaps
- No comprehensive SKU counts before vs. after at the company level (only individual stores).
- Gross margin impact by store cohort is disclosed only for Yonghui's 47-store subset.

## 4. Independent imitators (no direct PDL help): results and criticism

### Takeaway
A nationwide "学习胖东来" wave (2024 was called the "year of makeovers") produced mixed results:
- Some small independents succeeded, e.g., Suzhou 欧德福 (revenue ~RMB 50m to >RMB 100m).
- Several high-profile failures: 山西美特好 had mass closures and a stored-value-card run in Oct 2025, and 中百 posted big losses.
- Frequent "surface copying" with no change to pay or supplier economics.
- Some firms used the PDL banner for cost-cutting.
Critics coined phrases like "似胖东来者生，学胖东来者死" and "学徒集体挂科".

### Cited Findings
- 山西美特好 (self-directed "胖改"): in Oct 2025 it was reported to have closed 14 stores, with stored-value-card refund runs and delayed wages. — [镜相/虎嗅 summary, Apr 2026](https://m.huxiu.com/article/4853220.html); [钛媒体, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.tmtpost.com/8106328.html)
- A Hubei regional leader that loudly announced "fully learning from PDL" closed >400 stores and lost >RMB 900m within a year (matches 中百's 2025 figures). — [search summary of 界面/虎嗅 "挂科" articles](https://www.jiemian.com/article/12155812.html); [虎嗅 2026](https://m.huxiu.com/article/4853220.html)
- Suzhou 欧德福: annual revenue rose from ~RMB 50m to >RMB 100m after a PDL-style makeover (secondary source: consumer platform post). — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/awwo05n2/)
- Shanghai 中蔬时代: closed its stores for a self-directed makeover from 11 Mar 2025. It buys PDL goods through resellers and sells at PDL prices, i.e., at a loss, and supply is shrinking as PDL tightens purchase limits. Its suburban store reportedly sells >RMB 10m/month. — [上观新闻 via 网易, 29 Apr 2025](https://m.163.com/dy/article/JUA5UCK8055040N3.html)
- Non-retail imitators: 联商网 shared 100% then 50% of profits with staff (pay +~70%). Greentown Service and 永威物业 property managers "studied PDL". A health-products store adopted no-questions returns and saw no returns and no sales lift after two months. A foot-massage chain raised workloads without pay. One firm added 5% forced-ranking under the PDL banner. — [上观新闻 via 网易, 29 Apr 2025](https://m.163.com/dy/article/JUA5UCK8055040N3.html)
- Industry rule of thumb quoted by practitioners: "without 3,000 m²+, a makeover basically doesn't work". Many firms did "hardware replication" while leaving pay, incentives, supplier entry fees and commission rates untouched. — [搜索摘要: 界面/虎嗅/钛媒体 "挂科" articles](https://www.tmtpost.com/7964679.html)
- Miniso founder 叶国富 (Yonghui's largest shareholder since Sep 2024, 29.4% for RMB 6.3bn) said in 2024 that the PDL model was "the only path" for Chinese supermarkets. — [钛媒体, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.tmtpost.com/8106328.html); [Caixin/BigGo summary](https://finance.biggo.com/news/oHxOnp0BJouf4oEhlLHM)

### Inferences
- Failures cluster where imitators (a) were already financially distressed, (b) took on the labor/supplier cost changes without the scale or private-label margin to pay for them, or (c) copied the cosmetics only. Successes cluster among small, owner-run single-city operators with large-format stores. That profile is closest to PDL itself.

### Gaps
- No systematic dataset of independent imitators. The evidence is anecdotal media reporting, and some success stories come from promotional or low-quality sources.

## 5. Yu Donglai's own view on copying, expansion, and PDL's stance

### Takeaway
Yu says PDL's practices are worth learning from but full copying is "neither realistic nor necessary", because each firm's environment, culture and team differ. He frames 调改 as a change in the owner's mindset ("only if the boss's thinking changes will the methods appear"). PDL itself refuses to scale: it will not list, has stopped expanding, closes profitable stores on principle, and stays concentrated in Xuchang and Xinxiang (13 stores).

### Cited Findings
- Yu told People's Daily that PDL's model has reference value but full replication is unrealistic and unnecessary, and that copying surface practices rarely works. — [新浪/人民日报 via search](https://cj.sina.com.cn/articles/view/5953190046/162d6789e06701xwsq?froms=ggmp)
- Quotes: "只要是老板的思维发生改变，方法就会显现出来". "调改...调的是大家对经营一家美好超市的向往和认知". On a 2022 cohort of 100+ trainees: "100多人太多了，最好是只招收三四家来学". — [上观新闻 via 网易, 29 Apr 2025](https://m.163.com/dy/article/JUA5UCK8055040N3.html)
- "不要把顾客当上帝，把他们当家人" (treat customers as family, not gods). — [界面, Dec 2024](https://www.jiemian.com/article/12155812.html)
- PDL's anti-scale behavior:
  - Closed 13 stores in 2012 to avoid management losses.
  - In 2026 closed its Xuchang 生活广场 store, despite reported ~RMB 2bn annual sales and >RMB 100m profit, over unfair landlord renewal terms.
  - Equity is concentrated in management and the company is unlisted. — [青橙财经 via 钛媒体, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.tmtpost.com/8106328.html)
- Feb 2026: Yu retires to an adviser role, a 决策委员会 takes over, PDL will "never list", and expansion stops. — [虎嗅, Aug 2026 (search summary)](https://m.huxiu.com/article/4883792.html)
- PDL scale and staff metrics:
  - 2024 (independent/aggregator figure): 13 stores generated ~RMB 17bn revenue and >RMB 800m profit. — [Caixin/sinonomad search summary](https://sinonomad.com/blog/sino-nomad-blog/the-supermarket-that-became-a-pilgrimage-what-pang-donglai-tells-us-about-china?gi=15a2beeec8e60386e483a9f4e07316196fcd28e3)
  - 2025 staff turnover 1.05%; average take-home pay RMB 8,974/month (2025) and RMB 9,598 (Q1 2026).
  - H1 2026: 52 departures (0.5%), zero management turnover. These figures are PDL-published. — [红星资本局 via 新浪, 29 Jul 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/wm/2026-07-29/doc-iniknezp2622701.shtml)
- What PDL itself learned from: it reportedly first learned from Sam's Club, then from Aldi's community-discount model, and its private-label approach was modeled on Sam's and other foreign grocers. — [界面 "胖东来你学不来"](https://www.jiemian.com/article/11182280.html); [数英 "全网都在学胖东来，那胖东来在学谁？"](https://www.digitaling.com/articles/1243164.html)

### Inferences
- PDL's own leader expects learners to adapt principles, not clone a format. For a foreign team, the relevant "PDL" is a set of operating principles plus a founder-governed ownership structure. It is not a licensable concept, and there is no franchise, consulting fee or international partner.

### Gaps
- I found no verified primary statement of Yu Donglai citing specific Japanese retailers (Don Quijote, Life, Ito-Yokado) as models. This is unconfirmed and should not be asserted.

## 6. Analyst / academic views: what is and isn't transferable

### Takeaway
There is broad consensus.

Hard to transfer:
- Founder authority and culture.
- Unlisted, concentrated ownership that tolerates low growth.
- Near-monopoly local loyalty in Xuchang (a lower-tier city).
- A dense regional supply chain and a high private-label share.
- Back-end-fee-free economics.
- Capital to absorb 1–2 years of losses.

Transferable with effort:
- Service standards, display and freshness standards.
- Price/quality transparency and no-questions returns.
- SKU curation, fresh direct-sourcing, and private-label development.
- Above-market pay tied to culture, where the margin structure can carry it.

### Cited Findings
- Zhengzhou University professor 周阳敏: PDL's mechanism is directly tied to Yu Donglai himself and is not quickly replicable. — [search summary of CBNData "被神话的胖东来，为何难以复制？"](https://www.cbndata.com/information/293220)
- 赖阳 (Beijing Commerce Economics Society): PDL's success rests on a high private-label share and dense regional supply chains, which Yonghui lacks. Adding cost without supply-chain efficiency could accelerate losses. 文志宏 (和弘咨询): Yonghui's makeover entered "deep water" as closures and back-end overhauls caused losses before the supply chain could support store gains. An expert noted fresh supermarkets earn 15–20% gross margin, so a ~40% labor-cost rise needs roughly 40–60% sales growth to pay for itself. — [新京报, 23 Jan 2026](https://m.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1769159897169603.html)
- Constraints listed by 钛媒体/青橙财经 (Aug 2026):
  - (1) Unlisted, management-concentrated ownership.
  - (2) Founder dependence and succession risk.
  - (3) Labor cost: partner wages still well below PDL's.
  - (4) Geography: Xuchang's near-monopoly traffic vs. imitators in competitive provincial capitals with warehouse clubs, discounters and instant retail.
  - (5) Fee-free supplier model: imitators lost fee income that had masked front-end losses, and cash flow tightened.
  - (6) Local supply chain and taste: PDL's RMB 29.8 mooncake sells ~1m/day in Xuchang but reportedly struggled in Hunan. — [钛媒体, 18 Aug 2026](https://www.tmtpost.com/8106328.html)
- Huxiu analysis: PDL's model rests on a "landlord model" and absolute management control. — [虎嗅](https://m.huxiu.com/article/4828789.html) (title only reviewed)
- Commentary consensus: service standards and display norms can be copied in the short run, but cost structure and decision mechanisms cannot simply migrate. Fresh direct-sourcing, private-label development and strict QC are broadly applicable. — [search summary of 震楠商业策划 "三个硬约束" and similar](https://www.haiawake.com/blog/pangdonglai-xuebuqian-sange-yingshu/)
- 经济日报 commentary (Apr 2025): "what should learning from PDL actually learn". — [经济日报](http://paper.ce.cn/pad/content/202504/02/content_311642.html) (title only)

### Inferences
- Transferability is about economics, not just culture. The PDL formula raises cost in three places at once: labor (pay and headcount), supplier terms (no fees, fast payment, buy-backs), and capex. It only pays off if curation and private label lift gross margin and sales density enough to cover them. Yonghui's data suggest ~22% gross margin and sales density above ~RMB 2,800/m²/month were thresholds for profitability in its context.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed academic study quantifying the PDL model's replicability was found.

## 8. Brief Indonesian modern-trade context (for framing transferability)

### Takeaway
Indonesian modern grocery is fragmented across Superindo (largest supermarket chain, 277 stores), premium formats (Farmers Market, Ranch Market, Foodhall, Hero) and the dominant minimarket duopoly (Alfamart, Indomaret). Hypermart operator MPPA was loss-making for nine straight years through 2025. Rising minimum wages and a weak rupiah are cited cost headwinds, which matters for any PDL-style "pay well above market" strategy.

### Cited Findings
- Supermarket store counts at end-Jun 2026 (AMRT data): Superindo 277, Alfamidi Super 85, Ramayana 57, Foodhall 49, Farmers Market 39, Hero 23, Foodmart Super 21, Ranch Market 21, AEON 13. — [Databoks/Katadata, 7 Aug 2026](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/perdagangan/statistik/6a75360eee7fa/toko-supermarket-di-indonesia-bertambah-sampai-juni-2026-superindo-terbanyak)
- MPPA (Hypermart):
  - 2025 net sales IDR 7.25tn (+1.9%); net loss IDR 152.2bn (vs. IDR 118.1bn in 2024), its ninth straight loss year.
  - Rationalizing underperforming stores.
  - Q1 2026 net profit IDR 1.6bn; H1 2026 sales +3.9% with losses down 41%. — [Bisnis.com, 17 Feb 2026](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260217/192/1953449/pengelola-hypermart-mppa-raih-penjualan-rp725-triliun-sepanjang-2025); [Bisnis.com, 18 Feb 2026](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260218/192/1953588/emiten-grup-lippo-pengelola-hypermart-mppa-cetak-rugi-9-tahun-beruntun); [Warta Ekonomi](https://wartaekonomi.co.id/read601438/penjualan-naik-tapi-pengelola-hypermart-mppa-masih-tekor-rp152-miliar-di-2025); [IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/market-news/hypermart-mppa-raih-penjualan-rp4-triliun-pada-semester-i-2026-rugi-menyusut-41-persen-1/all)
- Broker note (Dec 2025): higher minimum wages and a weak rupiah remain key sector headwinds, pressuring margins amid limited pricing power. — [BRI Danareksa retail note, 19 Dec 2025](https://link.brights.id/brids/storage/43010/20251219-Retail.pdf)
- Katadata (2025): retail and trade are among the Indonesian sectors with long working hours but low wages. — [Databoks](https://databoks.katadata.co.id/en/employment/statistics/6ac47b232a6f6/indonesian-business-sectors-with-long-working-hours-but-low-wages-in-2025)

### Inferences
- Low wages and long hours in Indonesian retail mean a PDL-style above-market pay strategy would be a stronger differentiator in Indonesia than in China. It would also be more expensive relative to grocery margins, especially for a challenger without private-label scale.
- Indonesia's grocery landscape resembles China's competitive provincial capitals more than Xuchang: dense minimarket coverage, premium chains and quick-commerce. That suggests "local monopoly loyalty" (a PDL precondition) would be hard to recreate in Jakarta, and possibly easier in a strong regional city.
- Note: I found no sourced data on Indonesian chains' supplier-fee (listing/trading-terms) practices, return policies or service standards for a direct PDL comparison. Any such claims would need further research.

### Gaps
- No sourced data in this session on Transmart, Lotte Mart, Grand Lucky, AEON Indonesia or Alfamart/Indomaret practices (service, returns, pay, supplier terms) relative to PDL.
- No sector staff-turnover figures for Indonesian grocery found.
