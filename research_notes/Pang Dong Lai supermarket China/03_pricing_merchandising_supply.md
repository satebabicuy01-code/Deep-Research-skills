# Pang Dong Lai (胖东来): Pricing, Assortment, Private Label (DL) and Supply Chain

Research date: 20 Nov 2026. Currency: RMB. IDR figures use an indicative rate of **1 RMB ≈ IDR 2,300**, approximate for 2025–26. Check the live rate before using these figures in any business case. Unless a source says otherwise, every price below was observed at the date given.

---

## 1. Is Pang Dong Lai cheap? (Price comparisons by category)

### Takeaway
No, not as a whole. Evidence from journalists points to PDL being **"fair-priced / premium but worth it" rather than cheap**. Its general and fresh prices often match or exceed Sam's Club, Hema, online promotions and local discount supermarkets. The exceptions are a few DL private-label hero SKUs (beer, mooncake-style pastry, cooking oil, drugs), which are priced very aggressively and work as traffic and trust builders. Prices in categories with high information asymmetry (apparel, jade, gold, towels) are "fair" because margins are capped and disclosed, not because they are low.

### Cited Findings
**Head-to-head SKU comparisons (21世纪经济报道, published 7 Feb 2025, observed around Spring Festival 2025, Xuchang):**
- 5 kg Chilean cherry gift box "JJJ": **PDL Times Square store RMB 398 (≈IDR 915,000)** vs **Sam's Club 5 kg JJJ RMB 328 (≈IDR 754,000)**. PDL is about 21% more expensive. The packaging differs (gift box) — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html)
- Cooked river shrimp (熟食河虾): **PDL ≈RMB 65/jin (500 g) (≈IDR 150,000)** vs local Jiudi Supermarket (九弟超市) **≈RMB 35/jin (≈IDR 80,500)** — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html)
- Five-spice braised beef (五香酱牛肉): **PDL ≈RMB 79/jin (≈IDR 182,000)** vs Jiudi **"no more than RMB 50/jin" (≈IDR 115,000)** — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html)
- DL black-bean light soy sauce 480 ml: **RMB 8.7 (≈IDR 20,000)**, described as "slightly higher" than Haitian (海天), Qianhe (千禾) and other mass brands. DL baked oat flakes 320 g: **RMB 21 (≈IDR 48,300)**, price advantage "not obvious" — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html)
- The same article concludes that PDL's prices "are no advantage over online retailers with frequent promotions, and are not lower than Sam's Club or Hema". A Xuchang resident is quoted: "质量好，但有点贵了" ("good quality, but a bit expensive"). Locals mostly go to PDL for **gold, home appliances and tea**, because of quality and after-sales service — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html)
- 36Kr analysis (English edition), quoted by search summaries: "many of DL's products are priced higher than third-party brands." The same 36Kr 2025 piece claims basic DL items match top-3 industry quality at "about 20% lower prices". The two statements conflict, and neither comes with SKU data — [36氪 解码胖东来](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368)
- A Zhihu/NetEase roundup of netizen opinion is split. Some say PDL is "a bit cheaper" than Sam's. Others note that Sam's big packs look expensive per basket but are cheap per unit, while PDL sells smaller packs — [网易](https://www.163.com/dy/article/JK11NDHI0552RR09.html); [知乎](https://www.zhihu.com/question/1895430350932505139)

**Hero low prices (private label / traffic drivers):**
- DL craft wheat beer (made by Hangzhou Qiandaohu Beer, 千岛湖啤酒): from **RMB 2.5/can or bottle (≈IDR 5,750)**. Reports cite 330 ml × 24 for **RMB 60** and another case at **RMB 45** (observed 2024) — [搜狐](https://www.sohu.com/a/795763404_120392110); [新浪 黄牛正在毁掉胖东来 (Mar 2024)](https://finance.sina.cn/2024-03-28/detail-inapvxxu3636720.d.html)
- DL "big mooncake" (actually 芋泥麻薯酥饼 / 红豆蛋黄酥饼 pastries, 490 g, 7-day shelf life): **RMB 29.8 in store (≈IDR 68,500), RMB 33 per piece in the online gift box** (Sept 2026). The price has reportedly not risen for years — [新浪财经 25 Sep 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-09-25/doc-iniszrua9504962.shtml); [新浪/微博](https://www.sina.cn/weibo/detail/5346034562895307.html)
- Sunflower oil **RMB 49.9 per 5 L (≈IDR 115,000)** is named as a memorable private-label price point. This comes from an English secondary analysis and was not verified in a primary source — [Worldcrunch](https://worldcrunch.com/business-finance/chinas-pangdonglai-grocery-store-business/) / [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai) (search summary)
- Pharmacy: in 2025 PDL disclosed purchase prices for **1,140 drug SKUs sold at purchase price + 10%** — [观察者网 26 Mar 2025](https://www.guancha.cn/economy/2025_03_26_769835.shtml) (via search summary). Secondary sources say most drugs cost under RMB 20 and rarely exceed RMB 50 (unverified).

**Premium / "worth it" categories:**
- Towels: Uchino (内野) towels sold at **RMB 165–639 (≈IDR 380,000–1.47 million)**. A blogger's criticism of a **RMB 249 "luxury towel"** set off the April–May 2026 controversy (see section 2) — [新浪财经 / 北京晚报 2 May 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-05-02/doc-inhwnssi5742233.shtml?froms=ggmp)
- Jade (和田玉): Jan–Apr 2025 sales were 4,177 pieces for RMB 29.592 million. That averages ≈RMB 7,085 per piece (≈IDR 16.3 million), our own derivation — [新浪财经 7 May 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)
- Gold: one Zhihu question claims PDL gold jewellery is "RMB 200/g cheaper" than elsewhere. A search summary of another Zhihu post says the opposite (PDL gold priced about RMB 200/g above the Shanghai Gold Exchange price). This is **unverified and contradictory**. Note that the SGE price excludes fabrication, so it is not a like-for-like comparison — [知乎](https://www.zhihu.com/question/1997605038588129408)

### Inferences
- The best one-line answer for the Indonesian team: **PDL is not a discounter. It sells "honest prices"** — capped and disclosed margins, no hidden fees — **on mostly high-quality, first-tier and private-label goods**, plus a few ultra-cheap DL "hero" SKUs that create viral buzz.
- By category:
  - Fresh produce and deli: often *more expensive* than local wet markets and discounters, with quality, presentation and grading as the justification.
  - Bakery and DL hero products: *cheap / very good value*.
  - Branded packaged goods: roughly *market price*, not below online promotions.
  - Imported goods: no solid comparison found.
  - Jewelry, jade, apparel and towels: *fair, margin-capped*, with absolute prices that can be high.
  - Appliances and electronics: locals choose PDL for after-sales service rather than price (no SKU comparisons found).
- PDL can charge more than local rivals on fresh and deli because its brand trust lowers the shopper's risk on non-standard items, which is 21经济网's argument.

### Gaps
- No systematic basket study (e.g., 50 SKUs PDL vs Yonghui/Hema/JD) was found. All comparisons are anecdotal and mostly from early 2025.
- No verified 2026 head-to-head comparisons on imported goods or electronics.
- The gold price per gram claims are contradictory and unverified.

## 2. Transparent pricing: purchase price / gross margin on tags, margin caps, jade and towel controversies

### Takeaway
PDL has shown **purchase price (进价), selling price and gross margin** on apparel tags since around **March 2021**. The practice has since extended to items such as down jackets and drugs (purchase price + 10% for 1,140 drug SKUs in 2025). Stated rules have changed over time: an early 2021 rule of ≤20% margin for merchant brands; by Oct 2026 a uniform **27% for direct-sourced apparel and 22% for externally supplied goods**; and a general ban on both "暴利" (excess profit) and "负毛利" (negative margin). The 2025 jade controversy ended with a regulator finding average jade margins ≤20%. In 2026 a similar towel controversy surfaced (29.8% composite margin). In both cases PDL sued the critics.

### Cited Findings
**Origin and rules:**
- March 2021: PDL began printing purchase price and gross margin on apparel price tags. Reported rules: mall commission (扣点) no more than 10% of sales, merchant-brand gross margin within 20 points — [大河网 24 Mar 2021](https://4g.dahe.cn/news/20210324816335); [知乎专栏](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/360464330)
- Example tags:
  - Shirt: purchase price RMB 84.6, retail RMB 118, gross margin 28.3% — [Worldcrunch](https://worldcrunch.com/business-finance/chinas-pangdonglai-grocery-store-business/) (search summary)
  - Wool sweater: purchase price RMB 97, retail RMB 133 — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/am9voxnz/)
  - Down jacket: purchase price RMB 214.2, retail RMB 297 — [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/az8776eo/)
  - Down jacket cited during the 2025 jade controversy: purchase price RMB 800, retail RMB 820 (≈2.4% margin) — [腾讯新闻 8 May 2025](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A08BK800)
  - 36Kr cites a down jacket at a "0.3% gross margin" and many items labelled "成本价出售" (sold at cost) — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368)
- Mar 2025: 观察者网 headline "胖东来，彻底'透明'". It reports the disclosure of drug purchase prices: 1,140 drug SKUs at purchase price + 10% — [观察者网](https://www.guancha.cn/economy/2025_03_26_769835.shtml) (via search summary)
- **8 Oct 2026**: the hashtag "胖东来赚钱赚在明处" trended. An apparel staff member said:
  - Self-operated apparel tags show purchase and selling price.
  - The purchase price excludes labour and other costs.
  - The practice has run "for many years".
  - Direct-sourced (源头直采) goods carry a **27%** gross margin. Non-direct externally supplied goods carry **22%**.
  - Some items earn only **10–11%** after market price adjustments.
  - Adjustment details can be checked on **electronic shelf labels** at any time.
  — [新浪科技/快科技 8 Oct 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-10-08/doc-iniupafi3776142.shtml)
- Margin levels reported by others conflict. Southern Weekly (via CIW) says PDL's gross margin was **30% in 2020** vs Yonghui's 21% — [CIW, 7 Jan 2026](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai). 36Kr says supermarket gross margin is **15–20%**, vs an industry 25–30% — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368). A secondary English analysis says gross margin on everyday goods is "capped at 30%", with no slotting fees and no promotional kickbacks — search summary of [CIW](https://www.ciw.news/p/supermarket-pangdonglai)/[the-hyphen](https://www.the-hyphen.com/p/is-pangdonglai-ism-the-answer-to). The "zero slotting fees" claim appears in CIW's summary bullets.

**2025 jade (和田玉) controversy:**
- Early April 2025: jade blogger "柴怼怼" claimed PDL's real profit came from jade, selling "几百块的东西卖几千几万" ("things worth a few hundred sold for thousands or tens of thousands") — [新浪财经 7 May 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)
- 9 Apr 2025: PDL disclosed Q1 2025 和田玉 revenue of RMB 21.9 million at a **20% gross margin**, equal to 0.34% of group sales. Jewelry department margin is about **15–16%** — [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A08BK800); [新浪 search summary](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)
- 10 Apr: PDL offered third-party appraisal and a **full refund** if appraisal raises doubts about quality or price. 30 Apr: it published its jewelry 和田玉 pricing standard and return standard. 8 May: returns at the jewelry counter carry no handling fees or taxes deducted — [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A08BK800)
- 25 Apr: Xuchang Intermediate Court accepted PDL's suit for commercial defamation, claiming ≥RMB 5 million. 1 May: the Weidu district market regulator inspected and found jade sales Jan–Apr 2025 of 4,177 pieces for RMB 29.592 million, **average margin ≤20%**, all items price-marked and purchase documents complete. 3 May: Douyin removed 29 of the blogger's videos. Yu Donglai said he would "close or leave PDL" if the slander went unpunished — [新浪财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml); [澎湃](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30773658)
- After the controversy, demand surged. From 8 May 2025, 和田玉 purchases moved to online booking: **50 slots per day**, one booking per person per month — [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A08BK800)
- The exact pricing formula ("成本价 + 利润" tiers) of the 30 Apr 2025 standard was **not found in the articles retrieved**.

**2026 towel controversy:**
- 29 Apr–2 May 2026: blogger "惊梦人" called a RMB 249 Uchino Yuraku towel a "luxury towel" and questioned PDL's wages. PDL disclosed a **29.8% composite gross margin** on Uchino towels (RMB 165–639). It said the in-store 愉乐 series differs from the online Uchino lines in material, origin and patents, which it verified with Uchino's China agent, Japanese HQ and Thai factory. It reiterated its policy: **no excess profit, no negative margin**; brands with price-system requirements need merchandise-committee approval. Towels carry 5 brands: 洁玉, 那须藤, 三利, DL, 内野. PDL sued the blogger — [新浪财经/北京晚报 2 May 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-05-02/doc-inhwnssi5742233.shtml?froms=ggmp)

### Inferences
- PDL's transparency is real but **partial and self-reported**. Purchase prices exclude operating costs, and except in the jade case, where the local regulator checked, nobody verifies them independently.
- The margin reference points span 0.3% to 30% depending on category, year and source. For a store concept team, the useful pattern is a **category-level margin band disclosed on tags** (around 20–30% on apparel and soft goods, about 10% on drugs, ≤20% on jade), not one uniform cap.
- Both controversies (jade 2025, towels 2026) show the risk: transparency invites scrutiny of high-ticket items. PDL's defence playbook has four parts: disclose numbers, offer third-party appraisal and refunds, get regulator confirmation, and take legal action.

### Gaps
- No official full list of categories carrying 进价/毛利 tags in 2026 (apparel, down, drugs and towels are confirmed; whether food/grocery tags show margins is unclear).
- The 30 Apr 2025 jade pricing-standard document was not retrieved. The outcomes of the lawsuits against 柴怼怼 and 惊梦人 were not found.
- The conflicting overall gross-margin figures (15–20% vs 30%) cannot be resolved without audited data. PDL is private.

## 3. Private label "DL" (自有品牌)

### Takeaway
DL is PDL's fastest-growing engine. By Yu Donglai's own figures, sales went from RMB 75 million (2022) to RMB 250 million (2023), RMB 2 billion (2024) and a targeted RMB 6 billion (2025). It has 100+ SKUs and is said to account for ~30% of sales. Hero products (craft beer, big mooncake, juice, oat flakes, laundry detergent, liquor co-developed with Wuliangye) are routinely sold out, scalped and rationed. DL is also sold at cost to "helped" retailers (Yonghui, Bubugao) and online, but its presence in Yonghui stores has reportedly shrunk in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Yu Donglai (Apr 2025): DL sales were RMB 75 million in 2022, RMB 250 million in 2023 and RMB 2 billion in 2024, with a target of **RMB 6 billion in 2025**. RMB 600 million of DL products went to helped enterprises in 2024 — [新浪财经 21 Apr 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2025-04-21/doc-inetxfvz6379762.shtml?froms=ggmp); [壹览商业](https://www.yilantop.com/news/60005)
- 36Kr (2025):
  - Q1 2025 DL sales over RMB 1.76 billion, +42% YoY.
  - DL is **~30% of sales**, with a target of ≥50% within 3 years.
  - **100+ SKUs**, covering 60% of categories.
  - 4 single items exceed RMB 100 million each. One DL craft beer sells **RMB 500 million per year**.
  - The article also states "total DL sales RMB 1.1 billion", which conflicts with Yu's RMB 2 billion figure for 2024. It is probably older data.
  — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368)
- Products named:
  - Big mooncake, DL juice, DL craft wheat beer, DL oat flakes, DL laundry detergent, cooking oil, and premium items such as air-freighted Shangri-La matsutake.
  - A **RMB 39/bottle light-aroma baijiu co-developed with Wuliangye (五粮液)** as "reverse customization".
  - In-house bakery brand **德丽可思 (Delicious)**.
  - Sources: [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368); [zggxsmlt / search summary](https://www.zggxsmlt.com/portal/article/index.html?id=761&cid=4). There is also a Jiugui Liquor (酒鬼酒) baijiu tie-up — [时代周报](https://time-weekly.com/post/318910)
- Quality story: DL packaging is simple, ingredient lists are short and clean, and the positioning is "健康" (healthy) and "0添加" (zero additives). Items often sell out online and offline — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html). Media report that the mooncakes follow EU standards (Weibo repost, unverified) — [新浪/微博](https://www.sina.cn/weibo/detail/5346034562895307.html)
- Scalping and rationing:
  - Beer: a case priced RMB 45 resold for RMB 65 at the store door. Online resellers sold 6 × 330 ml (RMB 15) for RMB 32.8, and a RMB 60 case for up to RMB 105–120. PDL imposed purchase limits — [搜狐](https://www.sohu.com/a/795763404_120392110); [新浪 Mar 2024](https://finance.sina.cn/2024-03-28/detail-inapvxxu3636720.d.html). Xinhua (Apr 2024) reported that 代购 (resellers) degraded the shopping experience — [新华网](http://www.news.cn/fortune/20240408/17efe43906bf4b0c8cc7874d05a4939a/c.html)
  - Big mooncake 2026: from 11 Sep 2026 it was sold **online only for same-city pickup, 2 sets per person per day**. Several thousand units are released daily and sell out in 2–3 minutes. Resale was at RMB 50–60+ — [新浪财经 25 Sep 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-09-25/doc-iniszrua9504962.shtml). Earlier reports say resale reached RMB 119 and the product sold for RMB 168 overseas — [天府财经](https://www.tfcaijing.com/article/page/6f6d71434541452b4d364d546e64556b6d63333861673d3d); [游民星空 Sep 2025](https://www.gamersky.com/news/202509/2019351.shtml)
- Online channel: an online mall launched in late 2024 with limited categories; bakery, deli and condiments were not listed. Shipping outside Henan is costly, e.g. RMB 16 per piece for DL water to Guangzhou — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html). In Dec 2024 a Beijing reporter found only 13 items available online, and beer was not among them — [search summary of 新华网/北青报](https://www.sohu.com/a/795763404_120392110)
- DL distribution beyond PDL: DL has entered 41 cities including Beijing, Shanghai and Guangzhou, with a plan for 100 cities in 2025. DL sections make up 35% of sales in renovated partner stores — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368). Yonghui's Zhengzhou revamp store first carried 70+ DL SKUs. By July 2026, at the second revamp of Xi'an Daminggong Wanda, only a few remained (juice, oat flakes, baijiu, laundry detergent). Yonghui said stock-outs were temporary — [新浪/蓝鲸](https://k.sina.cn/article_5617041192_14ecd3f2802001q6is.html). Yonghui's own "品质永辉" private label made RMB 583 million in H1 2026 (2.32% of sales) — same source.

### Inferences
- DL works as a **"value hero + trust signal" system**: a small number of very low-priced, high-quality, often co-manufactured SKUs (beer from Qiandaohu, baijiu with Wuliangye) create scarcity and social-media buzz that pulls traffic into full-margin categories.
- Scarcity is partly deliberate (short shelf life, local pickup, purchase limits). It boosts buzz but creates scalping problems that hurt the customer experience.
- The 30% share figure probably mixes DL with self-operated/"自营" goods. Treat it as indicative.

### Gaps
- Actual 2025 full-year DL sales (vs the RMB 6 billion target) and 2026 DL sales were not found.
- No verified complete DL SKU list. 婆婆面 and 果汁 specifics were not confirmed in sources retrieved.
- Margins on DL products are not disclosed (one blog claims >20%, unverified).

## 4. Assortment strategy (SKUs, curation, fresh, deli, bakery)

### Takeaway
PDL runs a **moderately curated hypermarket/supermarket range of roughly 10,000–15,000 SKUs**, far more than Costco, fewer than Walmart. It has spent recent years deliberately cutting unbranded and third-tier products in favour of first-tier brands, DL and specialty items. Fresh, bakery and deli are traffic drivers (around 1/4 of offline sales by one report; fresh 20% of SKUs / 35% of sales at one store).

### Cited Findings
- Estimated SKU count **10,000–15,000** vs Walmart >20,000 and Costco <4,000 (化妆品观察 estimate) — [品观网](https://m.pinguan.com/article/detail/id/22642)
- Jinsanjiao (金三角) store: ~10,000 SKUs in the supermarket floor. Fresh is **20% of SKUs and 35% of sales**. Grocery/general merchandise and fresh are the main categories — [知乎 (older article)](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/79090094)
- Yu Donglai: for the past three years the merchandise department has been required to delist poor products, especially third-tier brands. PDL now mainly sells first-tier brands and specialty products — search summary of [zggxsmlt](https://www.zggxsmlt.com/portal/article/index.html?id=761&cid=4) / [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368)
- In its Yonghui revamps, PDL cut ~70–80% of products. The restructured range overlaps PDL's own assortment by >90% — [界面新闻](https://www.jiemian.com/article/11231722.html); [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368)
- Fresh, bakery and deli are the main offline traffic drivers, at around **1/4 of sales** — search summary of [第一财经](https://www.yicai.com/news/102400617.html)
- Example of "good-better-best" price laddering: the towel bay at Times Square displays 30+ SKUs by price tier (RMB 20/50/100+) and colour — search summary of [腾讯新闻 胖东来的100个细节](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20241125A039P600). The towel range is split into basic, fashion, functional and value types across 5 brands — [新浪财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-05-02/doc-inhwnssi5742233.shtml?froms=ggmp)
- Store base: 13 stores (5 department stores, 7 community supermarkets, 1 apparel/footwear) in Xuchang and Xinxiang. Times Square did RMB 4.413 billion in 2024 sales. Average transaction was RMB 78 in 2024 — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368). Group sales were RMB 16.96 billion in 2024 and RMB 23.53 billion in 2025 — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368); [网易](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KOKVL4920556KO47.html)

### Inferences
- "Fewer but better" applies more to **quality filtering** (no white-label or third-tier goods) than to a radically small SKU count. PDL is not an Aldi/Costco-style limited-assortment format.
- The model for Indonesia: cover the categories broadly, but every SKU must pass a quality bar. Use price-tier ladders inside categories, fresh/bakery/deli as the reason to visit, and DL heroes as buzz.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 official SKU count per store. No category sales mix for the whole group. Imported goods share and seasonal programme details were not found.

## 5. Quality control and information labels

### Takeaway
PDL puts a lot of detail into shelf information: price tags with purchase price and margin, fruit tags with **measured sugar content (Brix %)** and taste grade (sweet / sweet-sour / sour), origin, and usage/selection guidance. It backs this with strict supplier filtering, third-party appraisal (jewelry) and a generous complaint-reward system.

### Cited Findings
- Fruit tags (reported Oct 2026) show price, origin and **sugar content measured in-store**, e.g. sugar mandarin 13.0%, rambutan 16.7%, pomelo 13.3%. Some also grade taste as sweet, sweet-sour or sour. The testing method is not disclosed — [新浪/微博](https://www.sina.cn/weibo/detail/5352828383789352.html); [什么值得买](https://post.smzdm.com/p/a03x6o20/)
- Jewelry/jade: customers may use third-party or government appraisal and get a full refund if doubts are confirmed (2025) — [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A08BK800)

## 6. Supply chain (direct sourcing, logistics park, central kitchen, supplier payment)

### Takeaway
PDL combines **regional joint procurement** (the "四方联采" alliance since 2001, with 洛阳大张, 南阳万德隆 and 信阳西亚), **direct sourcing for fresh and apparel**, a ~RMB 1.5 billion **industrial logistics park with a central kitchen** (opened Oct 2022; central kitchen in production since June 2023), and **fast supplier payment (reportedly within about 7 days)**. Several quantitative claims circulate only in secondary or blog sources.

### Cited Findings
- Joint procurement: since 2001 PDL has bought together with 洛阳大张, 信阳西亚 and 南阳万德隆 (四方联采) — [东方财富 broker report PDF (Jun 2024)](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202406061635672974_1.pdf) (via search summary). 36Kr describes a procurement alliance with 洛阳大张 and 南阳万德隆 with annual purchasing over RMB 30 billion, fresh costs cut 15–20% and shared cold chain. PDL also joined the International Voluntary Chain Procurement Alliance (IFA) — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368)
- Logistics park: in operation since Oct 2022, 150 mu, total investment ≈RMB 1.5 billion, covering logistics, central kitchen, office, R&D and processing. The central kitchen started production in June 2023 — [东方财富 broker report](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202406061635672974_1.pdf) (search summary). A Zhihu post gives RMB 1.25 billion and 135 mu, which conflicts — [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1937524559206155420). 36Kr says the park enables "field → central kitchen → store in 72 hours" — [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368) (unverified)
- Direct-sourcing bases: Yantai apples, Zhoukou vegetables, Inner Mongolia beef/lamb, with fresh self-sourcing over 60%. This comes from analysis/blog posts only and was not verified — [知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1937524559206155420)
- Direct-sourced apparel carries a 27% margin vs 22% for external suppliers, which implies direct sourcing is a meaningful share of apparel — [新浪科技 8 Oct 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/roll/2026-10-08/doc-iniupafi3776142.shtml)
- Supplier payment: reportedly **"payment within 7 days of delivery"**, with early payment before holidays and finance staff chasing invoices. A 17-year supplier says payments arrive within 7 days. Traditional supermarket terms are 60–90 days — [新浪 胖东来"现结"背后的生意经](https://k.sina.cn/article_7857201856_1d45362c0019033uz8.html). Self-media also reports that PDL holds RMB 4.1 billion in cash and has no bank loans — [东方财富财富号 Oct 2025](https://caifuhao.eastmoney.com/news/20251016115041788135660)
- Co-manufacturing for DL: beer by Hangzhou Qiandaohu Beer, baijiu with Wuliangye and Jiugui — [搜狐](https://www.sohu.com/a/795763404_120392110); [36氪](https://36kr.com/p/3224618307370368); [时代周报](https://time-weekly.com/post/318910)

### Inferences
- Fast payment with no or low slotting fees probably buys PDL **supplier priority on quality and allocation** (best fruit grades, first stock) rather than the lowest cost price. This is consistent with the "fair, not cheapest" price outcome.
- The scale needed for low-cost DL comes from pooled buying (alliance plus helped retailers such as Yonghui and Bubugao), not from PDL's 13 stores alone.

### Gaps
- The 7-day payment term is reported in one self-media article. No official PDL statement on supplier terms was found.
- Percentage of goods directly sourced (overall) and central kitchen output/SKUs are not verified.

## 7. Customer service features tied to merchandising

### Takeaway
Service guarantees reduce the risk of PDL's higher prices: "不满意就退货" (return if unsatisfied, since 1999), a 500 RMB complaint reward, a fee-free jewelry refund on third-party appraisal, purchase limits to curb scalpers, and online booking for scarce items.

### Cited Findings
- "不满意就退货" since 1999. Frontline staff can process returns directly without escalation when items are resaleable. Another report says no-reason returns within 3 days; the two sources give different time limits — [search summary: 知乎](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/26492858675); [网易](https://c.m.163.com/news/a/JPJOIK1005198DT6.html?spss=sps_sem)
- Complaint reward: **RMB 500** once verified by the duty manager. In one 2026 case PDL gave **RMB 10,000** to a reporter even though the complaint (alleged altered production date on cut-resistant gloves) was found unsubstantiated — [界面新闻](https://www.jiemian.com/article/15322615.html) (search summary)
- Jewelry returns: no handling fees or taxes deducted. Refund on third-party appraisal doubt (May 2025) — [腾讯新闻](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250508A08BK800)
- Rationing: purchase limits on beer, online-only same-city pickup with 2 mooncake sets per day (Sep 2026), jade booking at 50 slots per day (May 2025) — sources above.

### Gaps
- No source found on a formal price-match/price-guarantee policy, tasting programmes, or cut-fruit/small-pack options. These are likely present but unverified.

## 8. Customer perception (value for money, criticisms)

### Takeaway
Perception is overwhelmingly about **trust, quality and service ("品质、自由和爱")**, not low prices. Criticisms cluster on some items being expensive (deli, fresh, towels, jade), crowds and queues, sell-outs and scalpers, and limited availability outside Henan.

### Cited Findings
- During reporter visits, shoppers most often said "品质、自由和爱" (quality, freedom and love), not discounts. A common saying: "胖东来哪里都好，唯一不好的地方就是我们这里没有" ("PDL is great in every way; the only bad thing is it isn't here"). Also quoted: "质量好，但有点贵了" ("good quality, but a bit expensive") — [21经济网](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20250207/herald/2067c25173e6b4626d3fa7652e1113c7.html)
- Crowds: people queue from 6 a.m. and some wait 3 hours to get in — [网易](https://c.m.163.com/news/a/JPJOIK1005198DT6.html?spss=sps_sem). Resellers make the experience worse for customers — [新华网 Apr 2024](http://www.news.cn/fortune/20240408/17efe43906bf4b0c8cc7874d05a4939a/c.html)
- "Luxury towel" and "can't leave Henan" criticisms (blogger, Apr 2026) — [新浪财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-05-02/doc-inhwnssi5742233.shtml?froms=ggmp). Jade "profiteering" accusation (Apr 2025), which the regulator found unfounded — [新浪财经](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-05-07/doc-inevtimy9678251.shtml)
- Public opinion largely sided with PDL in the jade dispute — [search summary, 中国新闻周刊](https://news.inewsweek.cn/finance/2025-05-08/25211.shtml)

### Inferences
- For Indonesia, the lesson is that "value" is built from **transparency, quality assurance and service**, not low prices. A PDL-like concept can price at or above market on fresh, deli and premium goods if trust mechanisms (disclosed margins, no-questions returns, quality labels) are credible. A few hero private-label SKUs at striking prices then supply the "it's a bargain" headline.

### Gaps
- No quantitative Dianping/Xiaohongshu sentiment analysis was found. The perception evidence comes from media quotes.
