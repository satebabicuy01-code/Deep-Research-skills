Research note scope: PBoC/Chinese state gold accumulation beyond the generic "de-dollarization" story. Current date Oct 20, 2026. Many of the 2026 sources are secondary (Kitco, Fortune, Business Today, GoldSilver, ZeroHedge, Substack); primary documents (PBoC/SAFE releases, Goldman notes, the BRICS declaration text) could not be fetched directly in this session (several pages returned HTTP 403). ## 1. Data: official PBoC holdings, buying streak, hidden/unreported buying, Treasury decline

### Takeaway
Officially, the PBoC has added gold for 23 straight months (Nov 2024 to Sep 2026) and reached about 77.47m oz (~2,410 t) at end-September 2026. September's +740k oz (~23 t) was the largest monthly addition since 2023. Market-flow estimates (Goldman Sachs, Julius Baer, WGC) suggest true Chinese official buying runs at roughly 1.75 to 2 times the reported pace. Over the same period, China's US Treasury holdings fell to $633.4bn (June 2026), the lowest since September 2008.

### Cited Findings
**Official PBoC series (recent):**
- End-Sep 2026: reserves rose 740,000 oz to **77.47 million oz**. This is the **23rd consecutive month** of additions (streak began Nov 2024). It was the largest monthly gain since Sep 2023, and it came as prices weakened toward ~$4,000/oz. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/world/story/china-buys-23-tonnes-of-gold-in-september-highest-monthly-purchase-since-2023-560787-2026-10-10); [Bloomberg headline: "China's Central Bank Adds Gold in September as Bullion Weakens"](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-07/china-s-central-bank-adds-gold-in-september-as-bullion-weakens); [China Daily HK](https://www.chinadailyhk.com/hk/article/640689)
- Business Today gives ~23 t and a record **~2,410 t** total. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/world/story/china-buys-23-tonnes-of-gold-in-september-highest-monthly-purchase-since-2023-560787-2026-10-10)
  - **CONFLICT:** Kitco's headline says 21 t and a total of 2,196 t. — [Kitco](https://www.kitco.com/news/article/2026-10-07/chinas-central-bank-buys-21-tonnes-gold-september-largest-monthly-purchase). 77.47m oz ÷ 32,150.7 oz/t = 2,409.6 t, so the ounce-based ~2,410 t is internally consistent and 2,196 t looks like an error. Another outlet (BigGo) calls September the "strongest pace since March" rather than "since 2023". — [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/7ec1bcf4-a98b-41f3-9e92-6b875057f0db)
- August 2026: ~20 t officially, the 22nd consecutive month (WGC data). — [GoldSilver summary of WGC](https://goldsilver.com/industry-news/goldsilver-news/pboc-gold-reserves-august-2026-22-months/)
- Earlier cycle: the PBoC bought for **18 consecutive months** (Nov 2022 to Apr 2024), paused in May 2024 "amid surging gold prices", then resumed in Nov 2024 after a six-month halt. Holdings went from 72.80m oz to **72.96m oz** at end-Nov 2024. — [Kitco/Reuters, Dec 9 2024](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2024-12-09/chinas-central-bank-resumes-gold-purchases-after-six-month-hiatus); [BNN Bloomberg](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/business/international/2024/12/07/china-resumes-buying-gold-reserves-in-nov-after-6-month-halt)
- The PBoC was the world's largest official-sector buyer in 2023. — [Fortune, Dec 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/12/07/china-central-bank-gold-purchases-reserves-prices-record-high-dedollarization)

**Unreported / hidden buying estimates:**
- **Goldman Sachs** (Lina Thomas, Daan Struyven; note reported Sep 23 2026) estimated China bought **~35 t in July 2026**, "nearly double" the reported figure. Kitco's headline says "75% more than reported". Goldman's July nowcast for all central banks was 44 t, against a pre-2022 average of 17 t/month. Its 3-month seasonally adjusted trend was 91 t/month in July and 100 t/month in June. — [Kitco, Sep 23 2026](https://www.kitco.com/news/article/2026-09-23/gold-price-will-hit-4900oz-2026-strong-sovereign-demand-china-buys-75-more)

Bank of England holdings for foreign central banks rose 63 t in July, more than the decline in NY Fed vaults. The Kitco article does **not** cite UK/Swiss customs export data as the method. — [Kitco](https://www.kitco.com/news/article/2026-09-23/gold-price-will-hit-4900oz-2026-strong-sovereign-demand-china-buys-75-more)
- ZeroHedge (Sep 22 2026) headline: "China Is Buying Twice as Much Gold as It Reports" (relays Goldman). — [ZeroHedge](https://www.zerohedge.com/news/2026-09-22/china-buying-twice-much-gold-it-reports)
- **Julius Baer (Carsten Menke):** the PBoC bought ~**190 t in 2026 through end-August, of which ~110 t was unreported**. — [GoldSilver (search summary)](https://goldsilver.com/industry-news/article/central-bank-gold-purchases-2026-unreported-demand/) (page returned 403 on fetch; figure comes from search snippet)
- **World Gold Council:** estimated **actual Q1 2026 central-bank purchases at 244 t vs only 16 t in official net reports**, which shows a very large "unknown buyer" share. — [GoldSilver (search summary)](https://goldsilver.com/industry-news/article/central-bank-gold-purchases-2026-unreported-demand/)
- An unverified industry-sourced report says China bought ~40 t via the London OTC market in June 2026 (no named institution). — [Kaohoon International](https://www.kaohooninternational.com/markets/589249)

**US Treasury holdings (TIC):**
- China held **$633.4bn** at end-June 2026, down from $659.3bn in May. Reuters called it the lowest since **September 2008**. Earlier 2026 readings: $694.4bn (Jan), $682.6bn (Feb report), $651.1bn (Apr). — [PrimeRates](https://primerates.com/foreign-treasury-holdings-fall-72-billion-june-china-633-billion/); [Feedbagel](https://feedbagel.com/post/china-trims-us-treasury-holdings-to-6511-billion-hitting-an-18-year-low)
- China's share of total foreign holdings of USTs fell to **7.3%**, the lowest since 2001 (Feb 2026 report). — [PrimeRates](https://primerates.com/foreign-treasury-holdings-fall-72-billion-june-china-633-billion/)

### Inferences
- **Arithmetic:** 72.96m oz (Nov 2024) to 77.47m oz (Sep 2026) is +4.51m oz, or **~140 t officially over 23 months** (~6 t/month average). Julius Baer's ~80 t reported in Jan to Aug 2026 plus ~23 t in September would put ~103 t in 2026 alone. That implies the official pace **accelerated sharply in 2026**, after token 1 to 5 t/month prints in late 2024 and 2025. Late-2024/2025 monthly prints are not verified in this session.
- If Goldman's "nearly double" and Julius Baer's 110 t unreported (Jan to Aug) hold, true Chinese official-sector acquisition in 2026 could run at roughly **250 to 300 t/yr**, against ~120 to 140 t reported. This is an analyst-derived estimate, not fact.
- **Gold share of reserves (estimate):** 77.47m oz × ~$4,100 to 4,300/oz ≈ **$320 to 335bn**. Against roughly $3.3tn in FX reserves (a widely cited figure, not verified this session), gold is about **9 to 10%** of China's official reserves. That is far below the US, Germany, Italy or France (60 to 75%) and below the ~20 to 27% global share the ECB reports. The low share is the main structural argument that China has a long runway to keep buying.
- **Swap pattern:** the UST decline (~$1.3tn peak in 2013, from background knowledge, to $633bn) alongside gold accumulation is consistent with a deliberate change in reserve composition. Part of the UST decline is also valuation loss and possible re-custody (e.g., via Belgium/Luxembourg custodians). TIC records only by custodian location, so the "China sells Treasuries" reading is partly contested.

### Gaps
- Historical official series before 2022 could not be source-verified in this session. Widely reported figures from background knowledge, **unverified here**: 600 t (2003 to Apr 2009), **1,054 t announced Apr 2009**, **1,658 t revealed July 2015** (first update in 6 years), ~1,842 t by late 2016, 1,948 t by Sep 2019 after Dec 2018 to Sep 2019 buying, then a freeze until Nov 2022. The report writer should verify these against PBoC/SAFE or WGC tables.
- I found no separate 2026 Societe Generale estimate of unreported Chinese buying. No reliable data was found on gold held by **SAFE, CIC, the national team, or state banks** (e.g., ICBC, BOC, ABC gold-lease books) outside PBoC reserves.
- UK/Swiss customs export flows to China for 2026 were not retrieved.
- China's total FX reserves for Sep 2026 and the official gold share were not retrieved.

## 2. Sanctions-proofing logic: Russia's frozen reserves, legal mechanics, "no-counterparty" gold, Taiwan contingency

### Takeaway
The 2022 freezing of Russian central-bank assets has hardened into an indefinite EU freeze (Dec 2025). Most of the €210bn sits at Euroclear, and its windfall profits are already transferred to Ukraine. That turns "reserves held in Western custody" into a policy-contingent claim. Domestically vaulted physical gold is the one reserve asset with no issuer liability and no foreign custodian. This is the core structural motive, distinct from simple "dollar dislike".

### Cited Findings
- In **December 2025** EU countries agreed to **indefinitely freeze €210bn** of Russian state assets. — [TBS News explainer](https://www.tbsnews.net/explainer/how-will-west-use-russias-frozen-assets-1269291); [MarketScreener/Reuters](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/how-does-the-eu-want-to-use-russia-s-frozen-assets-for-ukraine-ce7d50d8da8af12c)
- At end-March 2026, Euroclear Bank's balance sheet was **€237bn, of which €200bn related to sanctioned Russian assets**. Euroclear has transferred ~**€6.6bn** in windfall contributions to the European Fund for Ukraine since Feb 15 2024, with a next tranche of ~€1.4bn due July 2026. — [Moscow Times, Aug 6 2026](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/08/06/russias-central-bank-seeks-230bln-from-euroclear-over-frozen-assets)
- Reparations-loan plan: Dec 2025 plans spoke of €165 to 185bn. By Aug 2026 the Commission had proposed ~**€90bn**, repayable by Ukraine only if Russia pays reparations, and talks were "increasingly difficult". Belgium (Euroclear's home) demands shared EU guarantees. A €45bn G7 loan may have to be repaid first. — [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/08/06/russias-central-bank-seeks-230bln-from-euroclear-over-frozen-assets); [Fitch via Ukrainska Pravda, Dec 2025](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/12/17/8012186)
- Russian legal retaliation: the Bank of Russia sued Euroclear in Moscow for **RUB 18.2tn (~$256bn)**. On May 30 2026 Euroclear appealed immediate enforcement of the ruling. — [bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/euroclear-appeals-immediate-enforcement-of-256bn-russian-central-bank-ruling-445735/)
- An ECB report (citing a WGC survey) found that over 60% of central banks that increased gold reserves cited **geopolitical risk** as the main factor. — [Mining.com on ECB](https://www.mining.com/?p=1207502)
- Goldman tracks central-bank gold moving from London OTC into **"domestic vaults"**. Bank of England custody for foreign central banks rose 63 t in July 2026 while NY Fed vault holdings fell. This is physical evidence of custody reshuffling. — [Kitco](https://www.kitco.com/news/article/2026-09-23/gold-price-will-hit-4900oz-2026-strong-sovereign-demand-china-buys-75-more)

### Inferences
- **Legal mechanics:** Western-held reserves (USTs at the NY Fed/DTC, euro bonds at Euroclear/Clearstream, deposits at correspondent banks) are book-entry claims on intermediaries inside sanctioning jurisdictions. Freezing them takes an executive or Council decision, not a court process. The move from "freeze" (2022) to "use profits" (2024) to "indefinite freeze plus loan collateral" (2025 to 26) shows the escalation ladder China would face.
- **Gold held onshore** in PBoC/SGE vaults has no issuer, no custodian, and no foreign settlement layer. It can be mobilized for trade settlement, swaps or collateral with friendly counterparties even under full SWIFT/USD cut-off. That makes it the natural "war chest" asset in a Taiwan-contingency or "fortress balance sheet" framework. The heavy, steady, price-insensitive buying (including adds as prices fell in Sep 2026) is consistent with strategic rather than return-driven motives. This is analyst inference; China does not publish its rationale.
- The **opacity** of hidden purchases has its own strategic logic. It avoids moving prices and denies adversaries visibility into the true size of sanction-proof reserves.

### Gaps
- Primary-source verification of the original ~$300bn Russian freeze figure, the US **REPO Act** (Rebuilding Economic Prosperity and Opportunity for Ukrainians Act, April 2024) mechanics, and G7 ERA $50bn loan details was not possible this session. These are background knowledge only.
- I found no official Chinese document explicitly linking gold to Taiwan contingency planning. That link is analyst/think-tank inference.
- The outcome of the EU's latest (post-August 2026) summit on the reparations loan was not found.

## 3. Market infrastructure China is building (SGE, yuan benchmark, offshore vaults, Hong Kong hub, insurers)

### Takeaway
China is building a parallel, yuan-denominated physical gold ecosystem. It includes SGE contracts, offshore SGE-approved vaults (Hong Kong first, June 2025), and a Hong Kong central clearing system co-governed with SGE (trial from July 2026, 2,000 t storage target). Institutional domestic buyers such as insurers (Feb 2025 pilot) are being added. Together these create a gold venue where yuan, not dollars, is the pricing and settlement unit. Claims of a Saudi SGE vault and an "oil to yuan to gold" loop remain unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- **April 2025:** Chinese regulators said they would "explore the internationalisation of physical delivery", i.e., offshore delivery and storage for SGE products. — [SCMP](https://scmp.com/business/banking-finance/article/3307555/china-boost-yuan-overseas-gold-storage-facilities-it-challenges-us-hegemony)
- **June 2025:** SGE's first offshore physical delivery vault opened in **Hong Kong**, run by Bank of China (Hong Kong). SGE listed two new yuan-denominated gold contracts settleable physically or in cash. — [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/banking-finance/article/3315978/shanghai-gold-exchange-launches-first-offshore-vault-hong-kong)
- Cities reported under consideration for China's vault network: **Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Dubai, Riyadh, Moscow**. — [SCMP](https://scmp.com/business/banking-finance/article/3307555/china-boost-yuan-overseas-gold-storage-facilities-it-challenges-us-hegemony); SCMP analysis "why Chinese global network vaults would bolster yuan's trade role" — [SCMP](https://scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3365092/gold-strategy-why-chinese-global-network-vaults-would-bolster-yuans-trade-role)
- **Hong Kong clearing system:** run by government-owned Hong Kong Precious Metals Central Clearing Company. **SGE holds a deputy chair seat** (Jan 2026 agreement). — [RTHK, Jan 26 2026](https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1841371-20260126.htm)
  - Trial operations from **July 7 2026**. It settles OTC spot gold via unallocated accounts and is designed as a central counterparty. — [Asia Asset Management](https://www.asiaasset.com/analysis/hong-kong-bets-on-gold/)
  - Reuters (via Emirates 24/7): Hong Kong launched central clearing, **revived gold futures trading**, and started a **"Delivery Connect"** scheme with SGE for cross-border settlement. The stated aim is to be a regional **reserve** hub. — [Emirates 24/7](https://www.emirates247.com/business/hong-kong-launches-gold-clearing-system-aims-to-be-regional-reserve-hub/3347)
  - Storage target of **>2,000 t**, by 2028 per one source and 2030 per another (CONFLICT). The Financial Services Secretary told Yicai "within three years". — [Yicai Global](https://www2.yicaiglobal.com/news/hong-kong-to-team-up-with-shanghai-gold-exchange-on-otc-gold-central-clearing-system-treasury-bureau-boss-says); [Money Metals, May 2026](https://www.moneymetals.com/news/2026/05/21/hong-kong-gold-clearing-house-set-to-begin-operation-004930); [Caixin, Oct 2025](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-10-01/hong-kong-accelerates-push-to-become-gold-trading-hub-102368287.html)
  - Hong Kong competes with **Singapore** for the Asian hub role. — [Asia Asset Management](https://www.asiaasset.com/analysis/hong-kong-bets-on-gold/)
- **Insurance pilot (Feb 7 2025):** 10 insurers (incl. PICC P&C and China Life) may allocate up to **1% of assets** to gold via SGE spot, deferred-delivery and gold-leasing contracts. Potential flow is ~**RMB 200bn (~$27.4bn)** per China Merchants Securities, or ~**183 t** per TD Securities, with no set timeline. The rationale cited is low interest rates and a shortage of investable assets. — [WGC press release](https://www.gold.org/news-and-events/press-releases/chinas-insurance-funds-inject-new-vitality-global-and-domestic-gold); [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/insurance/china-frees-possible-27-billion-from-insurers-to-invest-in-gold); [Yicai](https://yicaiglobal.com/news/china-launches-pilot-scheme-for-picc-nine-other-insurers-to-invest-in-gold); [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/gold-chinese-life-insurance-companies-become-members-of-the-exchange-tds-202503261403)
- SGE internationalization background (SGE International Board, launched 2014 in the Shanghai FTZ). — [LBMA Alchemist #95](https://www.lbma.org.uk/alchemist/issue-95/the-international-development-of-the-shanghai-gold-exchange); [BullionStar explainer](https://www.bullionstar.com/gold-university/the-mechanics-of-the-shanghai-international-gold-exchange)
- **Saudi vault and oil to gold (UNVERIFIED):** a Substack claims China plans an SGEI vault in Saudi Arabia and an "oil-gold-RMB" pilot in which Gulf exporters settle oil in RMB and convert to gold in local vaults. There is no official or major-outlet confirmation. — [VBL Gold Fix Substack](https://vblgoldfix.substack.com/p/brics-2025-china-builds-global-gold)

### Inferences
- **The petroyuan to gold loop (mechanism, partly speculative):** an exporter paid in yuan (e.g., via INE Shanghai crude futures, launched 2018) faces a capital-account-closed currency with limited investable assets. The ability to convert yuan into **physically deliverable gold at an SGE-approved vault in its own region** (HK today, possibly Gulf/ASEAN later) gives yuan receipts a convertibility "exit". Gold, not the yuan, then becomes the store of value, and China avoids having to open its capital account. Hong Kong's CCP plus Delivery Connect creates the offshore plumbing for this. The **Saudi/Gulf leg is not yet evidenced**.
- The SGE-governed HK clearing house also gives Beijing an **Asian alternative to LBMA/London unallocated clearing**, where Western custodians and English law dominate. That fits the sanctions-proofing logic in section 2.
- Insurer allocation (~183 t potential), domestic household demand and PBoC buying together make China's onshore demand structurally large and partly policy-directed.

- PBoC **gold import quotas** to commercial banks in 2025 to 26 were not found in this session (a CNBC Oct 2026 piece on record imports was paywalled/403).
- Whether the insurer pilot was expanded in 2026 was not found.

## 4. Gold as settlement asset for trade surpluses; BRICS Unit / BRICS Pay status

### Takeaway
Bilateral local-currency trade (e.g., China-Russia) leaves structural imbalances that one side must hold in an unwanted currency. Gold is a neutral clearing asset for those residuals. However, the 2026 New Delhi BRICS summit produced **no common currency**. The gold-referenced "Unit" remains at **feasibility stage**, and BRICS Pay is endorsed but not shown as operational.

### Cited Findings
- 18th BRICS summit, **New Delhi, Sep 12 to 13 2026**: a 140-paragraph declaration adopted by consensus, with **no common currency** created or formally proposed. — [EntrepreneurLoop](https://entrepreneurloop.com/brics-summit-2026-takeaways/); [BusinessDay, Sep 29 2026](https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-09-29-rufaro-mafinyani-brics-and-the-emerging-payments-landscape/)
- The declaration reportedly **endorsed BRICS Pay for local-currency cross-border settlement**. It instructs the BRICS Payment Task Force to continue interoperability work. A gold-referenced **"unit" is treated as still at feasibility stage**. — [BusinessDay](https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-09-29-rufaro-mafinyani-brics-and-the-emerging-payments-landscape/)
- BRICS Pay described as linking India's UPI, China's CIPS and Russia's SPFS outside SWIFT. Pre-summit coverage targeted "operational deployment" by September 2026, with no confirmation found. — [IOL/Business Report, Jul 10 2026](https://iol.co.za/news/brics/2026-07-10-brics-series-can-the-bloc-deliver-a-working-alternative-to-swift-by-september/); [IOL, Mar 23 2026](https://iol.co.za/business/economy/2026-03-23-brics-payment-system-what-experts-say-about-its-design-rollout-and-timeline/)
- Pozsar's thesis: in a commodity-centric order, the **renminbi and euro play larger roles in trade settlement while gold re-emerges as a neutral reserve asset**. — [Blockeden (TOKEN2049 summary)](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2025/09/19/goldman-sachs-and-zoltan-pozsar-at-token2049/)

### Inferences
- **Mechanism:** in China-Russia trade, mostly in RMB/RUB, imbalances accumulate as unwanted currency balances. Russia's oil sales to India produced the well-known "stuck rupee" problem in 2023. Settling net balances in gold, which is transportable, universally accepted and sanction-proof, solves the "what do I do with surplus local currency" problem without routing through USD. For China as the structural surplus country, holding gold rather than USTs lets it **recycle surpluses without creating claims on a potential adversary**.
- India, as 2026 host, is the most dollar-friendly BRICS member and has resisted a common currency. That likely explains why the Unit stayed at feasibility stage (inference).

### Gaps
- No primary evidence was found of **actual gold-settled trade** between China and Russia/Iran in 2026. Reports remain anecdotal.
- The BRICS 2026 declaration text and an official Indian MEA readout were not retrieved. Details of the "Unit" (40% gold / 60% basket proposal, from the 2024 Kazan-era IIER/Unit concept) were not verified this session.

## 5. Domestic angle: households, ETFs, yuan internationalization, "gold-backed yuan"?

### Takeaway
Policy actions (the insurer pilot) explicitly cite low interest rates and the lack of investable assets. That is consistent with households and institutions using gold as a property and deposit substitute. However, 2026 China ETF and household demand data could not be sourced. There is **no evidence** of any plan for a gold-backed yuan. It remains speculation.

### Cited Findings
- The insurer pilot rationale cites **low interest rates, shrinking investable assets and rising gold prices**. — [Insurance Business Asia](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/asia/news/breaking-news/china-opens-gold-market-to-insurers-524342.aspx); [Reinsurance Asia](https://reinasia.com/china-launches-pilot-programme-allowing-insurers-to-invest-in-gold/)
- CNBC (Oct 12 2026) reports China is "importing a record amount of gold" and links it to interest rates (headline/URL only; body not accessible). — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/12/china-gold-buying-interest-rate-.html); [Vietnam.vn relay](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/vi-sao-trung-quoc-nhap-khau-luong-vang-ky-luc)
- Analysts frame the offshore vault network as a way to fix the "yuan problem", i.e., the yuan's limited convertibility. — [SCMP](https://scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3365092/gold-strategy-why-chinese-global-network-vaults-would-bolster-yuans-trade-role); [GeoSirius (lower-quality)](https://geosirius.ifz.ru/china-building-global-gold-vault-network-fix-yuan-problem)

### Inferences
- **Gold supports yuan credibility indirectly, not via a peg.** A large, partly hidden gold stock plus an SGE yuan-gold market lets foreign holders of RMB convert into gold (a "convertibility substitute") while China keeps capital controls. That is closer to an "implicit gold anchor for trade yuan" than a gold-backed currency. A formal peg would conflict with the PBoC's need for monetary autonomy and would be inconsistent with capital-account management.
- A property crash plus a near-zero deposit-rate environment makes gold one of the few permitted, liquid stores of value for Chinese households. This helps explain high domestic premiums and imports (inference; no 2026 data in hand).

### Gaps
- **No 2026 data found** on China gold ETF AUM/inflows, bar and coin demand, SGE withdrawals, or the SGE premium over London. The report writer should note this as missing.
- No credible source found for an official "partially gold-backed yuan" plan. Claims circulating online are speculation.

## 6. Global central-bank context: WGC surveys, gold vs euro/Treasuries in reserves, Basel III

### Takeaway
China is part of a global regime shift. In 2026, 89% of reserve managers expect global official gold holdings to rise and a record 45% plan to add themselves. By ECB measures, gold passed the euro (end-2024, 20% vs 16%) and then US Treasuries (end-2025, 27% vs 22%) as the second-largest reserve asset after the dollar. Foreign official gold (~$4tn) exceeded foreign official UST holdings (~$3.9tn) for the first time since 1996.

### Cited Findings
- **WGC Central Bank Gold Reserves Survey 2026** (released Jun 16 2026; fielded Feb 5 to May 19 2026; **76 responses**, a record): **89%** expect global CB gold holdings to rise over 12 months (vs **95% in 2025**). A record **45%** expect their own institution to add gold. Only **1%** expect their own gold reserves to decline. **83%** expect gold's share of reserves to be higher in 5 years (vs 76% last year). — [WGC press release](https://www.gold.org/download/file/20916/Central%20Banks%20Survey%20Press%20Release%202026); [Anadolu](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/economy/central-banks-expect-gold-holdings-to-rise-as-dollars-reserve-role-weakens-survey-finds/3968472); [Tribune India](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/gold-rush-in-vaults-central-banks-pivot-to-bullion-amid-rising-geopolitical-turmoil-wgc-survey/)

## 8. What experts say this signals: Bretton Woods III, neutral reserve asset, US revaluation, counterarguments

### Takeaway
The "neutral reserve asset" thesis (Pozsar's Bretton Woods III: outside money over inside money) is strongly supported by observed official behaviour. However, the US gold-revaluation idea floated in February 2025 was walked back and never enacted. Counterarguments remain strong: the dollar is still ~42 to 46% of reserves, China's capital account is closed, and BRICS failed to launch a currency.

### Cited Findings
- **Pozsar (Mar 2022, Credit Suisse):** the system is shifting from "inside money" (fiat, government debt) to **"outside money" (commodities, gold)**, and gold benefits as a **neutral reserve asset**. Sources here are secondary. — [Scottsdale Mint](https://www.scottsdalemint.com/articles/2025/understanding-gold-reserves-and-dedollarization-a-comprehensive-guide-for-investors/); [Blockeden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2025/09/19/goldman-sachs-and-zoltan-pozsar-at-token2049/)
- **US gold revaluation:** in Feb 2025 Treasury Secretary **Bessent** said the government would "monetize the asset side" of the balance sheet, which prompted revaluation speculation. He later clarified he did not mean repricing gold. US gold is carried at the statutory **$42.22/oz** (set 1973), a book value of ~**$11bn** vs ~**$765bn** market value at ~$2,920/oz. Mechanically, the Fed holds gold certificates and credits the Treasury. A revaluation would likely require Congress. — [Fortune, Feb 23 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/02/23/us-gold-reserves-repricing-fort-knox-trump-musk-treasury-federal-reserve); [Fortune, Feb 11 2025](https://dc.fortune.com/2025/02/11/adjusting-bookkeeping-america-gold-reserves-add-750-billion-treasury-overnight); [Jupiter AM](https://jupiteram.com/ch/en/professional/insights/does-trump-have-a-golden-plan)
- Skepticism that a revaluation would have real effect. — [VBL Gold Fix (Bloomberg relay)](https://vblgoldfix.substack.com/p/wall-street-talk-of-revaluing-us); [FXStreet "Funny money" Aug 2025](https://www.fxstreet.com/amp/analysis/funny-money-gold-revaluation-bitcoin-reserve-and-other-shell-games-202508181710)
- Dollar still **42%** of global reserves (ECB, end-2025) vs gold 27%. — [Mining.com](https://www.mining.com/?p=1207502)
- BRICS 2026: no common currency, and the gold unit is only at feasibility stage. — [BusinessDay](https://www.businessday.co.za/opinion/2026-09-29-rufaro-mafinyani-brics-and-the-emerging-payments-landscape/)

### Inferences
- At ~$4,140/oz, US gold (~261.5m oz, background figure) would be worth ~**$1.08tn** vs an $11bn book value. That is why revaluation keeps resurfacing. China's own accumulation raises the strategic value of the US stockpile and the incentive for a "gold-anchored" US response (speculative). No US revaluation had been enacted as of the sources found (all dated 2025).
- **Counterarguments (strong):** (a) the total official gold stock (~$4tn) is small vs the global dollar system (USD debt, FX turnover, trade invoicing). (b) Gold yields nothing and is not a payments medium, so it is a reserve, not a vehicle currency. (c) China's closed capital account and small RMB share in SWIFT/COFER (not retrieved here, ~2 to 5% historically) limit yuan substitution. (d) A large share of the reserve share gain is price, not quantity. (e) Gold markets remain dominated by London/NY clearing. Hong Kong's CCP is only in trial.
- The best-supported reading is a **"fragmented/multipolar reserve system with gold as the neutral collateral layer"**, not a new gold standard or a yuan-centric order.

### Gaps
- Primary text of Pozsar's notes was not retrieved. No record was found of a formal "Mar-a-Lago Accord" (Stephen Miran's Nov 2024 paper is background knowledge only) that includes gold. Atlantic Council / CFR / Brad Setser 2026 commentary on Chinese reserves was not retrieved within the tool budget.

## 9. Projected multi-year sequence 2026-2035 (evidence vs analyst scenario vs speculation)

### Takeaway
On current evidence, the most likely path is gradual. China keeps buying (officially plus hidden) toward a 15 to 20% gold share of reserves. It builds out yuan-gold convertibility via Hong Kong and offshore SGE vaults, and gold entrenches as the neutral reserve layer of a fragmented system. A gold-backed yuan, a BRICS gold currency, or the dollar's displacement remain speculation.

### Cited Findings (evidence base for the projection)
- Streak and acceleration: 23 months, 2,410 t, a record September add into price weakness. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/world/story/china-buys-23-tonnes-of-gold-in-september-highest-monthly-purchase-since-2023-560787-2026-10-10)

- HK 2,000 t storage target by 2028 to 2030, SGE co-governance, Delivery Connect. — [Yicai](https://www2.yicaiglobal.com/news/hong-kong-to-team-up-with-shanghai-gold-exchange-on-otc-gold-central-clearing-system-treasury-bureau-boss-says); [Emirates 24/7](https://www.emirates247.com/business/hong-kong-launches-gold-clearing-system-aims-to-be-regional-reserve-hub/3347)

### Inferences (labelled sequence)
**[EVIDENCE-BASED TREND, 2026-2027]**
1. The PBoC streak continues, with official adds of ~5 to 25 t/month and hidden buying through London OTC/Swiss channels. Official holdings could reach ~2,500 to 2,600 t by end-2027 (extrapolation).
2. China's UST holdings drift toward or below ~$600bn. TIC custodial ambiguity remains.
3. The HK clearing system moves from trial to full operation. More SGE-approved offshore vaults follow (candidates per SCMP: Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Dubai, Riyadh, Moscow).
4. EU decisions on Russian assets (reparations loan) proceed. Each step reinforces central-bank repatriation and "domestic vault" preference, consistent with the Goldman/BoE flow data.

**[ANALYST SCENARIO, 2027-2030]**
5. Gold's share of China's reserves rises from ~9 to 10% to ~15 to 20%, implying a several-hundred-tonne disclosure jump. A large "catch-up" revelation like those in 2009 and 2015 is possible.
6. Gold passes ~30% of global official reserves at market value if prices hold at $4,000 to 5,400+. Goldman's $5,400 end-2027 target is a scenario.
7. Yuan-gold convertibility hubs let commodity exporters (Gulf, Russia, Africa, Latin America) accept RMB for a growing slice of China-bound sales and convert residuals into gold in regional vaults. The "petroyuan to gold" loop moves from speculation to partial practice. That needs confirmation such as a Saudi or UAE SGE vault opening.
8. BRICS Pay achieves limited interoperability (CIPS-SPFS-UPI links). The gold-referenced Unit likely remains a pilot or study.

**[SPECULATION, 2030-2035]**
9. A **two-tier reserve system**: USD stays dominant for transactions and liquidity (Treasury market depth), while gold becomes the core neutral reserve and collateral for geopolitically non-aligned states. The RMB is a regional trade and settlement currency backed by gold convertibility, but not gold-pegged.
10. US policy response: possible revaluation of US gold (the $42.22 statutory price) or using gold in a debt or currency accord. This has been floated but **never enacted**.
11. Tail scenarios: (a) a Taiwan crisis triggers full Western sanctions on China. China's gold, held onshore and partly undisclosed, becomes its main usable external reserve, and gold reprices sharply. (b) Alternatively, China partially opens its capital account with a gold-convertibility "anchor" for offshore RMB. **There is no evidence for either; both are pure speculation.**

### Gaps
- No institutional (IMF/BIS/think-tank) long-range forecast specific to China's gold share or a 2030+ reserve composition was found in this session.
- Projections depend heavily on the price path, Fed policy, and the true size of unreported Chinese holdings, which cannot be verified.
