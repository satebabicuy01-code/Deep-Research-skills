## 1. Global status: how many countries, who has launched, and where the major pilots stand

### Takeaway
As of the Atlantic Council tracker's May 2026 update, 146 countries/currency unions (98%+ of world GDP) are exploring CBDCs, 77 are in advanced stages, 41 are piloting, but only 3 have fully launched (Bahamas, Jamaica, Nigeria) — and those launches have low uptake. The big 2026 events are China turning the e-CNY into an interest-bearing deposit-like instrument (1 Jan 2026), Russia's digital ruble mass rollout (1 Sept 2026), and the European Parliament adopting its digital-euro negotiating position (July 2026); meanwhile Brazil shut its Drex blockchain platform (Nov 2025), the UK is leaning away from a retail digital pound, and Indonesia remains wholesale/PoC-stage.

### Cited Findings

**Global counts (Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker, last updated May 2026) [2026]**
- 146 countries and currency unions exploring a CBDC, representing over 98% of global GDP; 77 in an advanced phase (development, pilot or launch); 41 pilot projects; 3 fully launched (Bahamas, Jamaica, Nigeria); 13 cross-border wholesale CBDC projects — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)
- The US is "the only G20 member not exploring a CBDC" (retail), though the New York Fed continues wholesale cross-border research via Project Agorá — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)
- Cross-border wholesale CBDC projects have "more than doubled" since Russia's invasion of Ukraine and the G7 sanctions response — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)
- Note on conflicting totals: third-party summaries cite 134 or "over 145" countries depending on date of snapshot; use 146 / May 2026 as the latest — [eco.com summary](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15210561-cbdc-updates-2026-what-changed-this-year); [Axis Intelligence](https://axis-intelligence.com/cbdc-statistics/)

**Launched CBDCs**
- Bahamas Sand Dollar (launched Oct 2020, first nationwide retail CBDC), Nigeria eNaira (Oct 2021), Jamaica JAM-DEX (2022) — listed as the only 3 fully launched — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/) (launch dates [BACKGROUND])
- **eNaira — why it failed [2023 IMF, still the authoritative assessment]:** IMF "Nigeria's eNaira, One Year After" (May 2023) called adoption "disappointingly low"; 98.5% of wallets were unused in any given week; causes cited: CBN's phased approach (initially bank-account holders only, onshore use only), inability to enforce usage, limited merchant acceptance, competition from established incumbents (mobile money, bank transfers), and weak public trust in the monetary system and technical reliability — [Nairametrics on IMF report](https://nairametrics.com/2023/05/19/imf-scores-enaira-low-on-adoption-in-new-report/); [Ripples Nigeria](https://ripplesnigeria.com/imf-scores-cbns-enaira-disappointingly-low-reveals-challenges-in-new-report)
- Conflicting numbers: CBN claimed ~13 million users, while the IMF counted ~860,000 wallet downloads — [Techloy](https://techloy.com/nigerias-enaira-struggles-with-low-adoption)
- Context [BACKGROUND]: adoption only briefly spiked during the 2022-23 naira-note redesign cash shortage, when cash was physically scarce — i.e., uptake came from coercion/cash scarcity, not voluntary preference. (Not re-verified this session.)

**China e-CNY [2026]**
- Largest CBDC pilot globally. By December 2025, retail e-CNY had processed over 3.4 billion transactions worth roughly 16.7 trillion yuan (~US$2.3-2.4 trillion), an increase of more than 800% vs 2023 — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/); [The Block citing Reuters/PBoC, Jan 2026](https://www.theblock.co/post/386057/china-led-cross-border-cbdc-platform-mbridge-surges-past-55-billion-in-transaction-volume-reuters)
- **Confirmed: interest-bearing / deposit reclassification from 1 Jan 2026.** PBoC formally reclassified e-CNY from a "digital cash substitute" (M0-like) into an interest-bearing deposit; flagged by Deputy Governor Lu Lei in a 28 Dec 2025 article in Financial News (PBoC newspaper). Commercial banks can hold e-CNY as a liability subject to reserve requirements and use it for credit creation/balance-sheet management. The underlying action plan has not been published — [Trivium China, 14 Jan 2026](https://triviumchina.com/2026/01/14/pboc-reclassifies-e-cny-as-interest-bearing-deposit-in-major-overhaul/)
- Atlantic Council describes this as reclassifying e-CNY "as deposit liabilities", possibly signalling a shift away from its original digital-cash function; "future plans are uncertain" — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)
- Wallet-tier details (secondary sources): real-name wallets (tiers 1-3) earn the bank's demand-deposit rate; anonymous tier-4 wallets remain interest-free; balances reportedly covered by deposit insurance; a ~0.35% rate is cited by only one source — [CCN](https://www.ccn.com/news/crypto/china-cbdc-digital-yuan-enter-new-era-heres-whats-changing/); [Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/e-cny-interest-payments/) [UNVERIFIED on rate]
- Reason for the change: uptake had been poor because WeChat Pay and Alipay dominate retail payments; Trivium expects 2026 to be the first year e-CNY gains "meaningful traction" — [Trivium China](https://triviumchina.com/2026/01/14/pboc-reclassifies-e-cny-as-interest-bearing-deposit-in-major-overhaul/)
- **e-CNY International Operation Center, Shanghai:** began operations 24-25 Sept 2025; run by PBoC's Digital Currency Institute; three platforms — cross-border payment platform, blockchain services platform, digital asset platform; first announced by Governor Pan Gongsheng at the June 2025 Lujiazui Forum — [Shanghai government](https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-FinancialServices/20250926/0881cbbaba5a44f8b420339d47413399.html); [Caixin Global](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2025-09-29/china-launches-digital-yuan-international-hub-to-spur-cross-border-use-102367502.html); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/358691/chinas-central-bank-unveils-e-cny-center-to-boost-global-reach-cites-stablecoins-impact-on-cross-border-payments)
- The center later signed direct-participant agreements with 26 financial institutions able to join CBETS, a cross-border settlement platform with 24/7 links to foreign central banks and overseas institutions (Reuters, dated 16 June, year likely 2026) — [The Standard HK](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/334787/China-signs-up-26-financial-institutions-to-digital-yuan-cross-border-payment-platform)

**India e-rupee (e₹) [2026]**
- Retail e₹ circulation fell ~24% to ₹771.7 crore at 31 March 2026 from ₹1,016.5 crore a year earlier (RBI Annual Report 2025-26), even as pilots expanded (direct-benefit-transfer pilots; programmable use cases) — [Business Upturn](https://www.businessupturn.com/sectors/banking/digital-rupee-in-circulation-falls-24-to-rs-771-7-crore-even-as-rbi-expands-cbdc-pilots)
- RBI weekly table for the week ended 4 Sept 2026 lists total e₹ at ₹1,182.60 crore (official, but columns look internally inconsistent) — [RBI currency in circulation](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_CurrencyCirculationDetails.aspx)
- Planned cross-border trials with Singapore and UAE — [TechPillow](https://www.techpillow.co/blog/rbi-digital-rupee-cbdc-circulation-welfare-cross-border-2026)
- As 2026 BRICS chair, India reportedly proposed linking members' digital currencies for trade and tourism — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)
- Comparison: e₹ (~₹800-1,200 crore ≈ US$100-140m) is trivial versus UPI, which dominates Indian digital payments ([BACKGROUND]).

**Brazil Drex (pivot) [2025]**
- Early Nov 2025: Banco Central do Brasil announced it would switch off the Drex platform after four years; in Aug 2025 it had already said the Hyperledger Besu infrastructure did not meet privacy and security requirements for large-scale operations; new approach = "define use cases first, technology later"; undecided whether new infrastructure would use blockchain — [Exame](https://exame.com/future-of-money/banco-central-anuncia-desligamento-da-plataforma-do-drex-e-nova-infraestrutura/); [Forbes Brasil](https://forbes.com.br/escolhas-do-editor/2025/11/banco-central-abandona-o-blockchain-do-drex-o-projeto-do-real-digital/); [Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/pagamentos/drex/drex-abandona-blockchain-em-nova-fase-tokenizacao-continua/)
- Tokenization work continues independently; participants said no launch was ever expected before 2027 — [Exame](https://exame.com/future-of-money/drex-sera-desligado-mas-deixa-legado-de-tokenizacao-segundo-a-abbc/); [Gazeta do Povo](https://www.gazetadopovo.com.br/economia/banco-central-encerra-projeto-do-drex-o-real-digital/)
- Atlantic Council still lists a Drex tokenized-credit pilot as part of a wholesale infrastructure push — [Atlantic Council CBDC Tracker](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)

**Russia digital ruble [2026]**
- Mass rollout began 1 Sept 2026: largest systemically important banks must offer digital-ruble operations; retailers with revenue >₽120m that bank with them must accept it; other banks/merchants phased in by 1 Sept 2027 and 1 Sept 2028 — [Moscow Times, 2 Jul 2026](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/07/02/russia-says-digital-ruble-ready-for-sept-1-launch-despite-weak-public-demand-a93155); [Interfax](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/112257/); [Reform.news, 1 Sept 2026](https://reform.news/en/russia-begins-mass-rollout-of-digital-ruble/)
- Bank of Russia notice (31 Aug 2026): individuals may use it "if they wish"; accounts held on the Bank of Russia platform; individual top-ups capped at ₽300,000/month; no cap for businesses — [Bank of Russia](https://www.cbr.ru/eng/press/event/?id=32808)
- Nabiullina (July 2026): "Technologically, everything is ready"; coverage notes weak public demand; 130+ banks preparing to integrate — [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/07/02/russia-says-digital-ruble-ready-for-sept-1-launch-despite-weak-public-demand-a93155); [Sputnik India](https://sputniknews.in/20260821/russias-digital-ruble-goes-live-for-circulation-from-september-1-12011782.html)
- No post-launch adoption data found (gap).

**Digital euro [2025-2026]**
- European Parliament ECON committee adopted its position 23 June 2026 (43-14-1); plenary negotiating mandate adopted 9 July 2026 (416 for, 169 against, 22 abstentions). This sets Parliament's position for trilogues with Council and Commission — it is not yet law — [HowTheyVote.eu](https://howtheyvote.eu/votes/195719); [Andante](https://www.andante-invest.com/en/post/the-digital-euro-reaches-a-key-milestone-what-the-econ-committee-vote-changes); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/06/23/european-parliament-backs-long-awaited-digital-euro-to-reduce-us-dominance-in-payments)

- Timeline: aim to finish legislation by end-2026, pilot in 2027, possible first issuance ~2029 — [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/european-parliament-approves-digital-euro-legislation-paving-way-for-2026-finalization). [BACKGROUND] The ECB Governing Council concluded the preparation phase (Nov 2023-Oct 2025) on 30 Oct 2025 and moved to the next phase, conditioned on legislation being adopted in 2026 — [ECB digital euro page](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/digital_euro/html/index.en.html)

**UK digital pound [2026]**
- Design phase since 2023; a decision plus a blueprint and joint BoE-HM Treasury assessment due in 2026; primary legislation would be required before any launch — [PaymentExpert, 14 Oct 2026](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/10/14/bank-of-england-digital-pound-2/); [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/113626/bank-of-england-says-no-need-to-rush-digital-pound-focus-shifts-to-stablecoins)

**Indonesia Digital Rupiah (Project Garuda) [2022-2026]**
- [BACKGROUND] White paper published Nov 2022; roadmap: (1) "immediate state" wholesale cash ledger (issuance/redemption/interbank transfer), (2) "intermediate state" wholesale + DvP with securities, (3) "end state" integration with retail — [Bank Indonesia Digital Rupiah page](https://www.bi.go.id/en/rupiah/digital-rupiah/default.aspx)
- Immediate-state PoC completed Dec 2024 on R3 Corda and Hyperledger Besu; next phase = digital assets/DvP — [BI press release](https://www.bi.go.id/id/publikasi/ruang-media/news-release/Pages/sp_2627024.aspx); [Fintech Singapore](https://fintechnews.sg/105255/indonesia/wholesale-rupiah-digital/); [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/project-garuda-takes-flight-first-phase-of-indonesias-digital-rupiah-achieved/)
- Feb 2026: infrastructure vendor Kaleido says the project completed PoC and is advancing toward "production readiness and expanded securities use cases" — [Kaleido](https://www.kaleido.io/blockchain-blog/project-garuda-bank-indonesias-cbdc-infrastructure)
- Nov 2025: BI unveiled a plan for a rupiah "national stablecoin"/tokenised instrument backed by digital rupiah and government securities (SBN); Governor Perry Warjiyo stressed this is not a retail CBDC launch — [IDN Financials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/58561/bank-indonesia-develops-stablecoin-based-on-digital-rupiah); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/cbdc-stablecoin-mechanics-indonesia-digital-152911913.html)
- CSIS Indonesia criticism: BI has not moved beyond PoC or run a pilot; calls to "refocus the purpose" — [CSIS](https://csis.or.id/publication/refocusing-the-purpose-of-indonesias-cbdc/)

### Inferences
- Pattern across launches: retail CBDCs fail to gain voluntary adoption where fast private/instant-payment rails already exist (UPI in India, Alipay/WeChat in China, mobile money in Nigeria, Pix in Brazil). Central banks are responding either by (a) making CBDC more deposit-like/attractive (China interest), (b) mandating acceptance (Russia), (c) retreating to wholesale/tokenisation (Brazil, UK, Indonesia).
- China's reclassification blurs the line between CBDC and tokenised bank deposits: the interest-bearing e-CNY is effectively a commercial-bank liability on PBoC rails, which undercuts the "digital cash" narrative but deepens state visibility into deposits.
- Indonesia's trajectory points to wholesale/tokenised-SBN first; a retail digital rupiah before ~2028-2030 appears unlikely based on BI's own phasing.

### Gaps
- No official PBoC 2026 data on e-CNY wallet counts or transaction totals since Dec 2025; the actual e-CNY interest rate is only from secondary sources.
- No post-1-Sept-2026 data on digital-ruble adoption.
- Bahamas Sand Dollar and JAM-DEX 2026 circulation figures not found (both widely reported to be tiny; not re-verified).
- Exact trilogue status of digital euro in Sept-Oct 2026 (Council general approach, start of trilogues) not confirmed in this session.
- No 2026 BI official announcement of a Garuda pilot date found.

## 2. The US stance: CBDC ban and stablecoins as the private "digital dollar"

### Takeaway
The US has chosen the opposite path from China/EU: an executive ban on a CBDC (Jan 2025), a House-passed (not yet enacted) statutory ban, and a federal stablecoin law (GENIUS Act, July 2025) that positions dollar stablecoins as the private-sector "digital dollar" to extend dollar reach abroad.

### Cited Findings
- Executive Order 14178, "Strengthening American Leadership in Digital Financial Technology," signed 23 Jan 2025, prohibits agencies from establishing, issuing or promoting a CBDC; revoked Biden's EO 14067; created the President's Working Group on Digital Asset Markets — [LegalClarity summary](https://legalclarity.org/digital-assets-executive-order-what-the-policy-covers/); primary text [BACKGROUND]: [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/strengthening-american-leadership-in-digital-financial-technology/)
- Anti-CBDC Surveillance State Act (H.R. 1919) passed the House 17 July 2025 by 219-210; Senate companion S.1124 (Cruz) referred to Senate Banking in March 2025 with no further action; as of June 2026 not enacted — [Congress.gov S.1124](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1124/all-info); [Rep. Emmer press release](https://emmer.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/whip-emmer-s-cbdc-ban-legislation-sent-to-senate); [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-laws/anti-cbdc-surveillance-state-act/)
- April 2026: House attached the bill text to another measure (Emmer's office: Foreign Intelligence Accountability Act; CryptoSlate says S.1318 — sources disagree) awaiting Senate consideration — [Emmer](https://emmer.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/whip-emmer-s-cbdc-ban-legislation-sent-to-senate); [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-laws/anti-cbdc-surveillance-state-act/)
- A Senate housing bill amendment would block a US CBDC until 2030 (Atlantic Council in the news, 2 Mar 2026) — [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/insight-impact/in-the-news/cbdc-tracker-featured-in-cointelegraph-article-on-a-senate-housing-bill-that-would-block-us-cbdc-until-2030/). One source claims the Senate voted 85-5 on 22 June 2026 to add a four-year retail-CBDC ban to housing legislation — [InformedClearly](https://informedclearly.com/en/crypto/58045/us-cbdc-ban-stablecoin-boom-2026) [UNVERIFIED]
- A House CBDC-ban rider was reported in the FY2026 NDAA; no confirmation it survived into the enacted law — [XT.com](https://www.xt.com/en/blog/post/u-s-house-blocks-cbdc-in-2026-ndaa-shaping-digital-currencys-future-report) [UNVERIFIED]
- GENIUS Act passed Congress 17 July 2025, signed 18 July 2025; requires 1:1 high-quality reserve backing; effective date = earlier of 18 Jan 2027 or 120 days after final regulations (one source says possibly as early as 15 Nov 2026) — [Société Générale Securities Services](https://www.securities-services.societegenerale.com/de/insights/views/news/us-publication-genius-act-stablecoins/); [Motley Fool Research](https://www.fool.com/research/banks-payment-processors-stablecoins/); [Chicago Fed (DeCarlo)](https://www.chicagofed.org/-/media/others/people/documents/decarlo-stablecoins-under-genius-act.pdf?sc_lang=en)
- Stablecoin market size: estimates range ~US$250bn to projections of US$500bn by end-2026 (from ~$200bn in 2025 per Chainalysis); no verified October 2026 figure — [Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/q3-2026-stablecoin-trends-to-expect-genius-act-bank-stablecoins-and-the-1-trillion-race/) [figures provisional]
- China explicitly cites stablecoins' impact on cross-border payments as a motivation for the e-CNY international center; e-CNY interest move framed by commentators as competing with US stablecoins — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/358691/chinas-central-bank-unveils-e-cny-center-to-boost-global-reach-cites-stablecoins-impact-on-cross-border-payments); [Stablecoin Insider](https://stablecoininsider.org/e-cny-interest-payments/)
- Critics argue GENIUS effectively creates "multiple privately-issued CBDC-like" dollars (Spanish-language editorial: "the US legalised multiple CBDCs") — [CriptoNoticias](https://www.criptonoticias.com/editorial/estados-unidos-legalizo-multiples-cbdc/) (opinion)

### Inferences
- Stablecoins require reserves mostly in T-bills, so stablecoin growth creates captive demand for US government debt — a de facto financial-repression-adjacent channel and a tool to export dollarisation into emerging markets (including Indonesia) via phones.
- Regulated stablecoins are not anonymous: issuers can freeze addresses (Tether/Circle have done so for sanctions/law-enforcement) and GENIUS imposes BSA/AML obligations — so "no CBDC" ≠ "no surveillance"; it shifts control to private issuers under US jurisdiction.
- For EMs, dollar stablecoins are a bigger near-term threat to monetary sovereignty than foreign CBDCs, which explains BI's rupiah-stablecoin/SBN-tokenisation plan.

### Gaps
- Final enacted status of any statutory US CBDC ban (NDAA or housing bill) as of Oct 2026 not confirmed.
- Status of GENIUS implementing rules (OCC/Treasury) in Oct 2026 not confirmed.

## 3. Cross-border and wholesale platforms: mBridge, Agorá, Nexus, BRICS, Dunbar, Jura — and the SWIFT/sanctions question

### Takeaway
Cross-border CBDC work has split into blocs: China-led mBridge (now ~US$55bn cumulative, ~95% e-CNY) for the China-Gulf-ASEAN corridor, and the G7-aligned BIS Project Agorá (tokenised correspondent banking with commercial banks and SWIFT). BIS exited mBridge in Oct 2024. Multilateral interoperability is widely seen as stalled, producing parallel rails that can reduce—but not yet eliminate—dependence on the dollar/SWIFT.

### Cited Findings
- mBridge: >4,000 cross-border transactions worth ~US$55.5bn cumulative (Atlantic Council data via Reuters, Jan 2026), ~2,500x 2022 levels; members: PBoC, HKMA, Bank of Thailand, CB of UAE, Saudi Central Bank; e-CNY ~95% of settlement volume — [The Block / Reuters, 17 Jan 2026](https://www.theblock.co/post/386057/china-led-cross-border-cbdc-platform-mbridge-surges-past-55-billion-in-transaction-volume-reuters); [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)
- BIS exited mBridge Oct 2024; Agustín Carstens called it a "graduation" and said "mBridge is not the BRICS bridge," rejecting sanctions-evasion speculation — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/386057/china-led-cross-border-cbdc-platform-mbridge-surges-past-55-billion-in-transaction-volume-reuters)
- Atlantic Council analyst Alisha Chhangani: mBridge "is unlikely to challenge dollar dominance directly, but it may incrementally erode it" — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/386057/china-led-cross-border-cbdc-platform-mbridge-surges-past-55-billion-in-transaction-volume-reuters)
- Project Agorá: BIS with seven central banks (incl. NY Fed, BoE, BoJ, also Banque de France for Eurosystem, Bank of Korea, Bank of Mexico, SNB [BACKGROUND]) and 40+ private institutions incl. JPMorgan, Citi, HSBC and SWIFT; tokenised correspondent banking on a unified ledger; testing findings expected H1 2026; Reuters reported stepped-up testing — [Forbes, 12 May 2026](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/05/12/after-mbridge-and-agora-multilateral-cbdc-interoperability-is-dead/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/386057/china-led-cross-border-cbdc-platform-mbridge-surges-past-55-billion-in-transaction-volume-reuters); [BIS Agorá page](https://www.bis.org/about/bisih/topics/fmis/agora.htm) [BACKGROUND]
- Forbes (May 2026): "multilateral CBDC interoperability is dead" — treasurers will face Agorá-style rails in G7 corridors and mBridge or successors in China-Gulf corridors — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/05/12/after-mbridge-and-agora-multilateral-cbdc-interoperability-is-dead/)
- Academic analysis of the geopolitics of CBDC-based cross-border systems (2026) — [New Political Economy / T&F](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13563467.2026.2737138)
- e-CNY CBETS cross-border platform with 26 institutions signed (see section 1) — [The Standard HK](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/334787/China-signs-up-26-financial-institutions-to-digital-yuan-cross-border-payment-platform)
- BRICS: India as 2026 chair reportedly proposed linking members' CBDCs — [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/). [BACKGROUND] "BRICS Bridge" and "BRICS Pay" were floated in Russia's 2024 chairmanship (Kazan declaration) but remain concepts without a live multilateral platform.
- [BACKGROUND] Project Nexus (BIS, originated 2021): links domestic instant-payment systems (not CBDCs) — founding members India, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand; handed to industry entity Nexus Global Payments in 2025 — [BIS Nexus page](https://www.bis.org/about/bisih/topics/fmis/nexus.htm). No 2026 update found. Note: Indonesia's relevance is via ASEAN QR/instant-payment linkages (QRIS cross-border) rather than CBDC.

## 4. Technical design choices and their implications (risks, surveillance, control)

### Takeaway
The design levers—retail vs wholesale, account vs token, holding limits, tiered privacy, programmability, offline modes—determine whether a CBDC is "digital cash" or a tool for granular control. Most democracies are designing in limits (holding caps, no programmable money, privacy tiers), while China's and Russia's designs put the central bank (or state-licensed banks) closer to every transaction. Surveillance and "social credit" linkage claims are mostly speculative; documented features are tiered KYC and "controllable anonymity."

### Cited Findings
- Retail vs wholesale: the current momentum is wholesale/tokenised settlement (Drex pivot, UK tokenised deposits, Indonesia Garuda wholesale-first, Agorá) — [Exame](https://exame.com/future-of-money/banco-central-anuncia-desligamento-da-plataforma-do-drex-e-nova-infraestrutura/); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/401984/bank-of-england-tokenization-stablecoins); [Kaleido](https://www.kaleido.io/blockchain-blog/project-garuda-bank-indonesias-cbdc-infrastructure)
- Two-tier distribution: e-CNY distributed via commercial banks, now as deposit-like liabilities eligible for reserve requirements and credit creation — [Trivium](https://triviumchina.com/2026/01/14/pboc-reclassifies-e-cny-as-interest-bearing-deposit-in-major-overhaul/). Russia, by contrast, holds digital-ruble accounts directly on the Bank of Russia platform (banks are intermediaries/interfaces) — [Bank of Russia](https://www.cbr.ru/eng/press/event/?id=32808)
- Holding limits as anti-bank-run/disintermediation tool: ECB says no stability risk up to ~€3,000 per person — [Andante](https://www.andante-invest.com/en/post/the-digital-euro-reaches-a-key-milestone-what-the-econ-committee-vote-changes); Russia caps individual top-ups at ₽300,000/month — [Bank of Russia](https://www.cbr.ru/eng/press/event/?id=32808)
- Privacy tiers: e-CNY anonymous tier-4 wallets (low limits, no interest) vs real-name tiers 1-3 that earn interest — [CCN](https://www.ccn.com/news/crypto/china-cbdc-digital-yuan-enter-new-era-heres-whats-changing/) — i.e., the 2026 change financially rewards giving up anonymity.
- Brazil's Drex shut down partly because the privacy architecture on Besu did not meet requirements — showing privacy is a binding technical constraint, not just politics — [Exame](https://exame.com/future-of-money/banco-central-anuncia-desligamento-da-plataforma-do-drex-e-nova-infraestrutura/); [Kiara Capital analysis](https://insights.kiara.capital/articles/reg-drex-fim/)
- Programmability: India's e₹ pilots include programmable direct benefit transfers (welfare money restricted to purpose) — [Business Upturn](https://www.businessupturn.com/sectors/banking/digital-rupee-in-circulation-falls-24-to-rs-771-7-crore-even-as-rbi-expands-cbdc-pilots); [ecorpit](https://ecorpit.com/programmable-digital-rupee-cbdc-fintech-builder-guide-2026/)
- Expiry dates [BACKGROUND, 2020]: e-CNY pilot "red envelope" lotteries (e.g., Shenzhen Oct 2020: 10m yuan in 200-yuan packets) had spending expiry windows (~1 week) — a demonstration of time-limited money for stimulus; reported widely by Reuters at the time (not re-fetched).
- US political framing: legislation is literally titled "Anti-CBDC Surveillance State Act" — [Congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/senate-bill/1124/text); EU Parliament debate also centred on privacy and holding limits — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/06/23/european-parliament-backs-long-awaited-digital-euro-to-reduce-us-dominance-in-payments)

**Evidence vs speculation (surveillance/control)**
- VERIFIED: Tiered KYC wallets; central-bank/bank visibility of real-name wallet transactions; programmable conditional payments in pilots (India DBT); expiring stimulus vouchers (China 2020 pilots); holding/top-up caps (EU proposal, Russia); interest now paid on e-CNY, making interest-rate pass-through technically possible.
- NOT VERIFIED / SPECULATIVE: A documented technical link between e-CNY and China's social credit system — no primary source found in this session. Claims that CBDCs will be used to impose negative rates, block "disfavoured" purchases, or auto-confiscate funds are scenario-based concerns, not observed policy in any launched CBDC. Note however that existing bank and payment rails already permit account freezes (e.g., Canada 2022 trucker protest account freezes [BACKGROUND]); CBDCs would lower the cost and raise the granularity of such actions.

### Inferences
- Bank disintermediation/runs: an unlimited, risk-free central-bank liability lets depositors flee banks instantly in a crisis; hence holding caps (EU), waterfall links to bank accounts, and China's choice to make e-CNY a bank liability. In EMs with weaker banks (and in Indonesia, where LPS deposit insurance caps at IDR 2bn [BACKGROUND]), this is a real policy constraint.
- Negative rates: a remunerated CBDC (China now pays interest) technically enables negative remuneration too; holding caps plus cash removal would be preconditions. No central bank has announced this.
- Capital controls: token/account-level geofencing and per-wallet caps make capital controls easier to enforce than with cash or crypto; combined with offline/anonymous tiers being kept small.
- Programmable payments (conditional logic in the payment layer, e.g., escrow/DvP) are distinct from programmable money (restrictions embedded in the money itself, e.g., expiry). The ECB has publicly ruled out programmable money for the digital euro [BACKGROUND]; China has demonstrated both.

### Gaps
- No primary source located on e-CNY offline (hardware wallet) usage statistics or on digital-euro offline privacy rules in the July 2026 text.
- No primary source on any e-CNY/social-credit integration (absence of evidence noted).

## 5. Connection to the current geopolitical/monetary shift (sanctions, de-dollarisation, stablecoins, cash decline, financial repression, tokenisation)

### Takeaway
CBDCs sit at the intersection of three forces: (1) sanctions-driven demand for non-dollar settlement rails (mBridge, e-CNY CBETS, digital ruble, BRICS proposals); (2) the US counter-strategy of exporting dollars via regulated stablecoins; (3) the tokenisation of finance (tokenised deposits, tokenised T-bill funds, tokenised government bonds), where wholesale CBDC is the settlement asset. The world is fragmenting into payment blocs rather than converging on one global digital-money system.

### Cited Findings
- Cross-border wholesale CBDC projects more than doubled after Russia's 2022 invasion and G7 sanctions — [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)
- Digital euro explicitly framed as reducing US dominance in payments — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/06/23/european-parliament-backs-long-awaited-digital-euro-to-reduce-us-dominance-in-payments)
- e-CNY international center framed by PBoC around stablecoin competition in cross-border payments — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/358691/chinas-central-bank-unveils-e-cny-center-to-boost-global-reach-cites-stablecoins-impact-on-cross-border-payments)
- UK and BoE preferring tokenised deposits and regulated stablecoins over a retail CBDC — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/401984/bank-of-england-tokenization-stablecoins)
- Bank Indonesia's plan for a tokenised rupiah instrument backed by government securities (SBN) — [IDN Financials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/58561/bank-indonesia-develops-stablecoin-based-on-digital-rupiah)
- BIS Annual Economic Report 2026 highlights a "new fiscal-financial stability nexus" (rising sovereign debt intertwining with financial stability) — [BIS AER 2026](https://bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2026e.htm)
- [BACKGROUND] BlackRock's tokenised T-bill fund BUIDL launched March 2024 on Ethereum and grew into the largest tokenised treasury fund (multi-billion USD in 2025); it is used as collateral/reserve by crypto platforms. 2026 AUM not verified this session.

### Inferences
- Financial repression channel: when CBDCs/stablecoins/tokenised money must be backed by government debt (GENIUS reserves in T-bills; BI's SBN-backed rupiah token; e-CNY now feeding bank balance sheets), digital money becomes a structural buyer of sovereign debt. Combined with high global debt (BIS fiscal-financial nexus), this raises the likelihood of policies that keep real rates low and steer savings into state paper.
- Cash decline + CBDC: as cash use falls, the anonymous "exit" option shrinks; whether a CBDC preserves cash-like privacy (EU's stated aim) or not (tiered, interest-for-identity in China) is the key differentiator for citizens.
- De-dollarisation via CBDC is incremental: mBridge's ~$55bn cumulative is a rounding error vs. global FX turnover, but it builds sanctions-resilient plumbing that can scale quickly in a crisis (e.g., further secondary sanctions on China or Gulf states).

- No hard data on share of China-Russia or China-Saudi trade settled via e-CNY rails.

## 6. Projected path 2026-2035 with signposts, and practical implications for an individual in an emerging market (Indonesia)

### Takeaway
Base case: through 2030 the world gets bloc-based wholesale CBDC/tokenised-settlement rails plus a handful of retail CBDCs (China, Russia, possibly EU from ~2029, India) with modest voluntary use; dollar stablecoins grow fastest among EM retail users. For an Indonesian, the near-term change is not a retail digital rupiah but tokenised SBN/rupiah instruments, QRIS/instant-payment expansion, and stronger digital KYC/visibility over money; hard-to-control stores of value (physical gold, self-custodied Bitcoin, cash) keep their role as hedges, with regulatory risks. All projections below are inferences, not facts.

### Cited Findings (anchor dates for the timeline)
- Digital euro: legislation targeted end-2026, pilot 2027, possible issuance ~2029 — [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/european-parliament-approves-digital-euro-legislation-paving-way-for-2026-finalization); [HowTheyVote.eu](https://howtheyvote.eu/votes/195719)
- Digital ruble phased mandatory acceptance: Sept 2026 → Sept 2027 → Sept 2028 — [Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/07/02/russia-says-digital-ruble-ready-for-sept-1-launch-despite-weak-public-demand-a93155)
- UK decision on digital pound due by end-2026 — [PaymentExpert](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/10/14/bank-of-england-digital-pound-2/)
- GENIUS Act effective by 18 Jan 2027 at the latest — [Motley Fool Research](https://www.fool.com/research/banks-payment-processors-stablecoins/)
- US CBDC ban to 2030 proposed in Senate housing bill — [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/insight-impact/in-the-news/cbdc-tracker-featured-in-cointelegraph-article-on-a-senate-housing-bill-that-would-block-us-cbdc-until-2030/)
- Indonesia: Garuda moving from PoC to "production readiness" and securities use cases (wholesale) — [Kaleido](https://www.kaleido.io/blockchain-blog/project-garuda-bank-indonesias-cbdc-infrastructure); BI SBN-backed rupiah token plan — [IDN Financials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/58561/bank-indonesia-develops-stablecoin-based-on-digital-rupiah)
- 2026 BRICS chair India pushing CBDC linkages — [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/cbdctracker/)

### Inferences — step-by-step projection (speculative; label as scenario)
1. **Late 2026 – 2027 (rules year):** EU trilogue deal on digital euro + holding limit; UK announces "no retail CBDC for now" or a slow-track blueprint; GENIUS rules go live; Russia expands mandatory acceptance; e-CNY usage jumps on interest-bearing status. *Signposts:* trilogue agreement and final holding-limit figure; BoE/HMT decision; PBoC e-CNY balance data in 2026 annual report; stablecoin market cap crossing $400-500bn; Agorá phase results.
2. **2027 – 2028 (plumbing year):** Wholesale tokenised settlement (Agorá-like, Pontes, Garuda intermediate state with SBN DvP) moves to production; mBridge/CBETS expand membership in Gulf/ASEAN; BRICS link proposals become bilateral CBDC corridors (e.g., India-UAE). Indonesia: tokenised SBN/rupiah settlement token in wholesale use. *Signposts:* new central banks joining mBridge (esp. ASEAN incl. BI, or Russia — would be a major sanctions flag); Saudi/UAE oil settlement in e-CNY announcements; US secondary sanctions targeting CBDC platforms.
3. **2029 – 2030 (retail decision point):** Digital euro possible first issuance (with ~€3,000-class cap); Russia full merchant coverage (2028) — watch whether digital-ruble use becomes required for state payments/salaries; US ban possibly expires/renews (2030). Indonesia: decision whether to extend digital rupiah to retail ("end state"). *Signposts:* any central bank making CBDC mandatory for public-sector salaries/benefits; cash-acceptance laws (EU legal-tender-of-cash regulation) vs. cash restrictions; programmable welfare at scale (India).
4. **2030 – 2035 (equilibrium/stress-test):** Two scenarios. (a) *Benign:* CBDCs are a niche public payment option; money remains mostly bank deposits/tokenised deposits; stablecoins are the dominant cross-border retail rail. (b) *Repression/stress:* in a sovereign-debt or banking crisis, governments use digital rails for capital controls, holding caps, deposit-to-CBDC conversions, or rate floors below inflation. *Signposts:* holding limits being lowered/raised; negative or zero-remuneration decisions; restrictions on self-custody wallets; withdrawal limits on cash; mandatory KYC on all crypto transfers (travel rule) extended to self-hosted wallets.

### Inferences — practical implications for an individual in Indonesia
- **Bank deposits:** No near-term forced change. Watch BI's tokenised-rupiah/SBN plan—deposit-like digital instruments may appear inside bank apps. Deposit insurance (LPS) limits remain the key protection; a future retail digital rupiah would likely have holding caps to protect banks.
- **Cash:** Rupiah cash remains legal tender; but continued QRIS growth and any future CBDC reduce cash's practical role. Holding some physical cash preserves an offline, private option.
- **Privacy:** Expect more data visibility over time (tiered KYC, programmable welfare pilots modelled on India/China). The China precedent (interest only for real-name wallets) shows how privacy can be priced out rather than banned.
- **Gold:** Physical gold is outside all digital rails and is not programmable; it is the classic hedge against financial repression and capital controls (and is being accumulated by EM central banks, per the broader research set). Digital/tokenised gold products inherit platform and KYC risks.
- **Crypto/Bitcoin:** Bitcoin is a non-sovereign, censorship-resistant alternative if self-custodied; but on/off-ramps are KYC'd (in Indonesia regulated by OJK since 2025 [BACKGROUND]) and could be restricted in a capital-control scenario. Dollar stablecoins (USDT/USDC) offer a dollar hedge but are freezable by issuers and subject to US jurisdiction/GENIUS rules.
- **Foreign CBDCs:** e-CNY/digital euro access for Indonesian residents is unlikely to matter for individuals; relevance is via trade settlement (exporters to China/Gulf).
- **What to watch (Indonesia-specific):** BI announcements on Garuda "intermediate state" and SBN tokenisation; any BI retail pilot announcement; BI/OJK rules on rupiah stablecoins and foreign stablecoins; Indonesia joining mBridge or BRICS payment initiatives (Indonesia became a BRICS member in Jan 2025 [BACKGROUND]); changes in LPS coverage; cash withdrawal/transaction reporting thresholds; crypto tax and self-custody rules.

- No reliable quantitative forecasts (e.g., IMF/BIS projections of CBDC share of money supply by 2030) were retrieved.
- Indonesia's membership/participation status in mBridge, Nexus or BRICS Pay as of Oct 2026 not verified this session.
