# Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center: Services, Platforms and Programs

Researched 2026-11-11. Every fact below is dated. Short source names: GZGov = gz.gov.cn, 21J = 21jingji.com, SC = southcn.com, XKB = xkb.com.cn (New Express).

**Which entity this is.** Two candidates match the name.

- **Candidate A (most literal match): 广州人工智能融合赋能中心**, "Guangzhou AI Integration (Convergence) & Empowerment Center".
  - Unveiled on 8 June 2022 at Guangdian Pingyun Plaza (广电平云广场), 163 Pingyun Road, Tianhe District.
  - Built by Guangzhou Radio Group (广州无线电集团, GRG) with Huawei. GRG's parent is now Guangzhou Digital Technology Group (广州数字科技集团, "广州数科").
  - Its working platform is the **Guangzhou AI Public Computing Center** (广州人工智能公共算力中心). It is paired with the Guangzhou "Kunpeng + Ascend" (鲲鹏+昇腾) innovation center.
  - After 2022, most reporting names the Public Computing Center and the **Guangzhou Intelligent Computing Alliance** (广州智算联盟), not the "融合赋能中心" brand.
- **Candidate B (newer, similar name): 广州市粤港澳大湾区人工智能应用赋能中心**, "Guangzhou Greater Bay Area AI Application Empowerment Center".
  - Unveiled on 18 June 2026 at Yangcheng Creative Industry Park (羊城创意产业园).
  - Six co-founders: Guangzhou Digital Technology Group, China Telecom Guangzhou, PCI Technology (佳都科技), CloudWalk (云从科技), Guangzhou Yundie Technology (广州云蝶科技) and the Guangzhou AI Industry Development Promotion Association (广州市人工智能产业发展促进会).
  - Guangzhou's 2026 AI work plan and draft AI promotion regulation both call for a municipal "AI application empowerment center". Candidate B appears to be that center.
- **Both candidates are controlled or co-founded by Guangzhou Digital Technology Group (GRG's parent).** These notes cover both. Candidate A has much more operational data.

## What are the core service lines and platforms, and who provides them?

### Takeaway
Candidate A is essentially a public AI-compute and enabling platform run by state-owned GRG / Guangzhou Digital Technology Group, with Huawei Ascend as the core technology stack. Its services are:
- Ascend-based public compute: 100P planned in phase 1, about 99P delivered, completed in September 2022.
- A city-wide compute scheduling and trading platform (智算运行服务平台, later a "token-level" 一体化算力网监测调度平台).
- Hosted open-source model services, including DeepSeek.
- Solution co-development, supply–demand matchmaking, and an alliance (广州智算联盟) that includes the three telecom carriers.

Candidate B (June 2026) is a matchmaking, talent, standards and compliance service platform. Its announced services have no published metrics yet.

  - More than 11,000P of multi-vendor compute under management.
  - A model open-service platform compatible with more than 170 mainstream open-source models.
  - Source: [Baidu Baike: 广州人工智能公共算力中心](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%B9%BF%E5%B7%9E%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E5%85%AC%E5%85%B1%E7%AE%97%E5%8A%9B%E4%B8%AD%E5%BF%83/61369240)

  - The Guangzhou Government Services & Data Administration and Guangzhou Digital Technology Group launched the 广州市一体化算力网监测调度平台. It is billed as China's first city-level compute operation platform that schedules and bills by token (词元).
  - Billing is pay-per-use or by period. Architecture is "1 compute base + 1 monitoring center + 1 scheduling engine".
  - Managed compute is to expand to 70,000P. This is a target, not a reported actual.
  - The same event broke ground on four approved compute projects by Guangzhou Mobile, Guangzhou Unicom, 云下科技 and 连云大数据: RMB 4.839 billion total investment and more than 40,000P of new intelligent compute.

  - Hardware: NVIDIA A800/V100, Tiangai 100 (天垓100) and Huawei Ascend 910B.
  - Products: Atlas 800TA2 and Atlas 800 training servers (monthly or annual rental), a Moore Threads server, and an on-premises DeepSeek-671B appliance.
  - A 算力券 (compute voucher) application channel.
  - Coverage of 7 districts and 15 compute centers.
  - Model and data services marked "under construction" (筹建中).
  - Contact: aipcc-gz@grg.net.cn; 163 Pingyun Road, Tianhe.

**Model services**
- DeepSeek went live on 28 January 2025 (upgraded release):
  - Versions: V1–V3 and R1, the 671B full model, and 7B/14B distilled models.
  - Claimed as the first government-grade secure compute center in China to open a trial of the DeepSeek-R1 671B Ascend-adapted version.
  - Services: online inference, API, one-click data processing, fine-tuning and deployment, plus private model training on enterprise data.
  - Can support 1,000 enterprises at the same time.
  - More than 20 organisations had visited by mid-February 2025 (government bureaus, restaurant groups, tourism groups).

### Inferences
- In practice, the "Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center" is the Pingyun Plaza complex: the GRG/Huawei Public Computing Center, the Kunpeng+Ascend innovation center and the alliance secretariat.
  - The hardware is dominated by Huawei Ascend.
  - Carriers take part mainly through the alliance and new compute projects, not as center operators.
  - Most heterogeneous compute (NVIDIA, Moore Threads, Tiangai) is aggregated through the scheduling platform.
- Candidate B looks like the "AI+ application" upgrade or successor layer, with the same parent group (Guangzhou Digital Technology Group). It may share staff or venues with Candidate A. This is inference; no source confirms an organisational link.
- No source reports the founding partners Baidu, iFlytek, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent, SenseTime, Pony.ai, WeRide, XPeng or GAC as operators of either center. Baidu and Volcano Engine appear only as compute buyers at the April 2026 compute supply–demand event.

### Gaps
- Baidu Baike pages for both centers could not be fetched; their stats come from search snippets only.
- The fetch of Guangzhou's official 2026 AI work plan (fgw.gz.gov.cn) failed (HTTP 503). The search snippet says it calls for building a "Greater Bay Area (Guangzhou) AI application empowerment center" for industry services, technology transfer, cross-border/going-global services and talent.
- Current actual compute under management in 2026 was not found. The platform homepage shows "0 P", which looks like a placeholder.

## What concrete outcomes and case studies have been reported?

### Takeaway
Reported metrics grew from 70 service applications (June 2022) to more than 150 enterprises (April 2023) and about 230 enterprises (June 2024, Baike), plus more than 80 solutions and 2,068P matched (March 2025). Targets for 2025 were 300 enterprises, 100 matches and 100 cases. No audited 2025 year-end results were found.

### Cited Findings
- June 2022: more than 300 companies had visited for talks and more than 70 had applied for services. — [21J, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- April 2023: more than 150 enterprises connected, 45% of them "专精特新" (specialized, innovative SMEs); more than 80 solutions co-built. — [Times Weekly via Sina, 2023-04-13](https://finance.sina.cn/2023-04-13/detail-imyqezsn6906060.d.html)
- June 2024 (Baike search snippet): more than 230 enterprises served cumulatively, 45% of them 专精特新, and more than 80 industry solutions. — [Baidu Baike](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%B9%BF%E5%B7%9E%E4%BA%BA%E5%B7%A5%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD%E5%85%AC%E5%85%B1%E7%AE%97%E5%8A%9B%E4%B8%AD%E5%BF%83/61369240)
- 2025 targets (set 25 March 2025):
  - Serve 300 enterprises, achieve 100 supply–demand matches, select 100 typical cases.
  - Build 2–3 municipal "AI+" application promotion bases and 100 in-depth cases.
  - Eight focus sectors, including manufacturing, health, education and autonomous driving.
  - The center released its first batch of "AI+" typical cases.
  - Sources: [XKB, 2025-03-27](https://www.xkb.com.cn/articleDetail/390384); [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250327A097Z200)
- 3 April 2026 compute supply–demand event: 28 compute and data users signed with local compute builders, including Baidu, Volcano Engine, JD Technology, 37 Interactive and Turing Technology. — [GZGov, 2026-04-04](https://www.gz.gov.cn/szgzkfgx/zdgz/content/post_10756479.html)
- DeepSeek use cases offered (February 2025):
  - Government: policy Q&A and 12345 hotline dispatch.
  - Healthcare, industrial quality inspection, office content, customer service agents.
  - A private SOP model for restaurant chains.
  - Source: [GZGov, 2025-02-15](https://www.gz.gov.cn/zt/jj2025gzlh/lhgjc/zhcs/content/post_10116762.html)

### Inferences
- Growth in enterprises served is modest: about 230 cumulative by mid-2024 against a target of 300 in 2025. The center's scale lies more in compute aggregation (thousands of P) than in direct enterprise projects.

### Gaps
- No 2025 year-end or 2026 figures were found for enterprises served, deals, contract values or number of registered models.
- Candidate B has no outcome metrics yet.

## Are there membership, alliance, voucher or international programs?

### Takeaway
The main membership vehicle is the Guangzhou Intelligent Computing Alliance (more than 50 members). The platform website has a compute-voucher (算力券) channel, and Guangzhou said in 2025 it would issue annual "intelligent vouchers", but no published Guangzhou voucher amounts were found. International or foreign-company programs are only aspirational: Candidate B's "Bay Area collaboration and global value chain" module and the 2026 work plan's "cross-border/going-global services".

### Cited Findings
- Alliance: more than 50 members, including Guangzhou Digital Technology Group, Huawei and the three carriers. — [XKB, 2025-03-27](https://www.xkb.com.cn/articleDetail/390384)
- In March 2025 the Guangzhou MIIT bureau (with the Data Bureau) said it was drafting a compute-application policy to issue annual intelligent (compute) vouchers for SMEs. — [XKB, 2025-03-27](https://www.xkb.com.cn/articleDetail/390384); [Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20250327A097Z200)
- The platform website offers a "算力券" subsidy application entry. — [web.aipcc-gz.com](https://web.aipcc-gz.com/)
- Tianhe District 2025 guide: up to RMB 1 million per year for operators of recognised public intelligent compute centers that provide ecosystem services. This is operator support, not a user voucher. — [search result summary; gz.gov.cn policy library](https://www.gz.gov.cn/gzzcwjk/attachment/7/7781/7781781/10166411.pdf)
- Candidate B's module 6 is "湾区协同与全球价值链重构" (Bay Area collaboration and global value chain restructuring). Guangzhou says it will keep opening scenarios and welcome global developers and enterprises. — [21J, 2026-06-18](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260618/herald/9f1f54c690c02d85b13a6c4f97c8fa0b.html)

### Inferences
- Foreign companies would most plausibly engage through:
  - The alliance, or via Chinese partners.
  - Candidate B's matchmaking and compliance services.
  - Provincial matchmaking conferences, such as the 2nd Guangdong AI Application Matchmaking Conference on 22 September 2026 ([21J, 2026-09-24](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260924/herald/dcb47e823926eeb49681435c9611bece.html)).
- No formal foreign-membership track was found.

### Gaps
- Guangzhou voucher amounts, eligibility rules and any 模型券 (model voucher) program were not found. Shanghai, Beijing Yizhuang and Shenzhen have explicit voucher programs, but Guangzhou city-level details did not surface.
