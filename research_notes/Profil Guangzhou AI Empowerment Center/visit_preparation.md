# Visit Preparation: Indonesian Delegation to the Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center (广州人工智能融合赋能中心)

Research date: 11 November 2026. Every fact is dated where the source allows. "Inferences" are the researcher's own reasoning and are not sourced facts.

Naming note (important for logistics): at least two similarly named Guangzhou bodies exist. Confirm with the host which one you are visiting.
- **广州人工智能融合赋能中心 (Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center)**: unveiled 8 June 2022 at Guangzhou Radio Group and co-built with Huawei. It is linked to the Guangzhou AI Public Computing Center (100P of compute planned for Phase 1) — [cnbayarea.org.cn / Southern Daily, 9 Jun 2022](https://www.cnbayarea.org.cn/homepage/news/content/post_978471.html). In April 2026 it was relaunched as a Huawei "showcase site" (样板点) built on Ascend compute — [IT168, 29 Apr 2026](https://server.it168.com/a2026/0429/6926/000006926746.shtml).
- **广州市粤港澳大湾区人工智能应用赋能中心 (Guangzhou GBA AI Application Empowerment Center)**: a different, newer public-service platform unveiled 18 June 2026. Its six founders include Guangzhou Digital Technology Group, China Telecom Guangzhou, PCI Technology (佳都科技) and CloudWalk (云从科技) — [ScienceNet / China Science Daily, 22 Jun 2026](https://news.sciencenet.cn/htmlnews/2026/6/566952.shtm).

## 1. Which foreign delegations have visited this center (or similar Guangzhou AI venues), and what did they see or do? What does a visit usually look like?

### Takeaway
I found no public, dated report of a named foreign or Indonesian delegation touring the 广州人工智能融合赋能中心 itself. The center has been promoted as a Huawei "global showcase" open to visits since April 2026. The best-documented foreign visit to Guangzhou AI sites is the APEC SME Ministerial programme on 1 September 2026. It visited Xpeng, Fanto Digital and the Haixinsha Full-Space Intelligent Experience Center, and it combined guided showroom tours with hands-on robot and XR demos.

### Cited Findings
**About the center (what visitors are likely to be shown)**
- On 8 June 2022 the center was unveiled at Guangzhou Radio Group (广州无线电集团), co-built with Huawei. Huawei signed a deepened strategic cooperation agreement with the Guangzhou municipal government at the same ceremony. Phase 1 of the Guangzhou AI Public Computing Center planned 100P of AI compute. Target sectors are intelligent manufacturing, autonomous driving, biomedicine and smart cities. The first ecosystem partners to move in were Huawei, Guangzhou Industrial Investment Holding Group, China Academy of Industrial Internet, Unicom (Guangdong) Industrial Internet, CloudWalk Technology and Guangzhou University — [cnbayarea.org.cn, 9 Jun 2022](https://www.cnbayarea.org.cn/homepage/news/content/post_978471.html); also [gz.gov.cn listing](https://www.gz.gov.cn/zt/gzydyl/jsxd/content/post_8328334.html) (the page returned 404 when fetched on 11 Nov 2026).

### Inferences
- The likely visit format, inferred from the APEC programme and the center's profile: a welcome by a Guangzhou Digital Technology Group or municipal official, a briefing video or presentation on "Digital Guangzhou" and the compute platform, a walk through the machine room or exhibition area (Ascend compute, liquid cooling, intelligent O&M), application demos (government services, city governance, industrial AI), then a seated exchange or Q&A. An MoU or letter-of-intent signing is possible only if it was arranged in advance through the foreign affairs office.
- Because Huawei co-launched the showcase site, Huawei staff may co-host or present, and Huawei branding (Ascend/"昇腾") is likely to be prominent.
- If the host programme includes "AI experience" stops, Haixinsha and Xpeng are the venues already used for high-level foreign guests in 2026.

## 2. What Indonesia–Guangzhou/Guangdong or ASEAN–China AI cooperation exists as of 2026?

### Takeaway
Indonesia–China AI cooperation in 2026 is mainly national and multilateral. Indonesia joined the China-led WAICO in July 2026, a bilateral AI/semiconductor working group was launched in August 2026, and Nanning hosts ASEAN-China AI centres with an Indonesia agreement. Guangdong-specific ties are promotional: consulate events, investment salons, and the Surabaya–Guangzhou sister-city relationship. I found no signed Guangzhou-company–Indonesia AI MoU.

## 4. Smart questions, realistic collaboration angles, and etiquette and practical tips

### Takeaway
Realistic angles: compute-as-a-service and data-centre know-how (Indonesia pitches itself as an AI data-centre location), smart-city and e-government AI, talent training (vocational/university), manufacturing AI, and healthcare. For etiquette, observe hierarchy, exchange cards with both hands and keep them bilingual, give modest group-level gifts lead-to-lead, and use interpreters with simple slides.

### Cited Findings
**Collaboration hooks already voiced by officials**
- Indonesia's acting Consul General in Guangzhou (June 2026) named data centres/cloud/AI, pharma R&D and green energy as priorities — [China Daily HK](https://www.chinadailyhk.com/article/635012).
- IIPC (August 2026) offered free support on regulations, site selection and local partners for Guangdong investors in AI data centres and semiconductors — [Newsgd](https://www.newsgd.com/node_d36b0ef83f/f6e6fcdee0.shtml).

Its first Super Intelligent Agent Competition has AI+medical and AI+elderly-care tracks — [ScienceNet](https://news.sciencenet.cn/htmlnews/2026/6/566952.shtm).
- The center's original sector focus is intelligent manufacturing, autonomous driving, biomedicine and smart cities — [cnbayarea.org.cn](https://www.cnbayarea.org.cn/homepage/news/content/post_978471.html).

**Etiquette (reputable guides)**
- Gifts: typically one modest group gift from delegation lead to host lead. Avoid expensive gifts that could look like bribery, especially to officials — [Canadian Trade Commissioner Service](https://www.tradecommissioner.gc.ca/en/market-industry-info/search-country-region/country/canada-china-export/business-etiquette-china.html). One travel-agency source claims gifts are forbidden outright. This conflicts with the official guidance and looks overstated — [CIBT Visas blog](https://cibtvisas.com/blog/business-etiquette-china).
- Business cards: present and receive with both hands, senior person first. Read the card and do not write on or fold it. Bilingual (English/Chinese) cards are appreciated — [Canadian TCS](https://www.tradecommissioner.gc.ca/en/market-industry-info/search-country-region/country/canada-china-export/business-etiquette-china.html); [Aviation Week guide](https://aviationweek.com/air-transport/airports-networks/chinese-etiquette-guide-world-routes-2018-delegates).
- Hierarchy: send a delegation list in strict order of rank. Seating at meetings and banquets follows rank, and titles determine invitations and seating — [Canadian TCS](https://www.tradecommissioner.gc.ca/en/market-industry-info/search-country-region/country/canada-china-export/business-etiquette-china.html); [Australian Consulate-General Chengdu, formal meetings](https://chengdu.china.embassy.gov.au/cxdu/formalmeetingblog.html).
- Interpreters: arrange or request an interpreter even if counterparts understand English. Keep slides short (3–4 points per slide) so interpretation can follow — [Canadian TCS](https://www.tradecommissioner.gc.ca/en/market-industry-info/search-country-region/country/canada-china-export/business-etiquette-china.html).

**Suggested questions (researcher-drafted; not sourced, see Inferences)**: listed below.

### Inferences
- **Smart questions to ask the host:**
  1. What share of current compute capacity is used by government versus enterprises, and is there a pricing model for foreign or overseas users?
  2. Which AI applications have measurable outcomes in Guangzhou government services (e.g., processing-time reductions)?
  3. How is government data governed and classified before it is used to train models (link to PRC "important data" rules)?
  4. Which local-language (Bahasa Indonesia) LLM or speech capabilities exist?
  5. Does the center run training programmes for foreign officials or engineers, and in what language?
  6. How does the center cooperate with the Nanning China-ASEAN AI centres or with WAICO?
  7. What is the model for "AI+manufacturing" support to SMEs, and could it be replicated in Indonesian industrial estates?
  8. Which partners are on the platform, and on what hardware/software stack (Ascend/MindSpore)?
- **Realistic collaboration angles:** civil-servant AI training and study visits; smart-city or e-government pilot exchanges (fits the Surabaya–Guangzhou sister-city tie); university links (Guangzhou University was a 2022 ecosystem partner and is active in China-ASEAN AI forums); manufacturing/SME AI adoption programmes; healthcare/elderly-care AI. Large compute or data-centre deals are better handled via IIPC/BKPM channels and need compliance vetting (Section 5).
- **Practical tips (general knowledge, not sourced here):** most hosts communicate by WeChat, so install it and set up a WeChat Pay/Alipay wallet before travel. Ask before photographing machine rooms or screens; data-centre areas often restrict photography. Prepare a short Mandarin-translated delegation introduction. ## 5. Compliance and reputational risks

### Takeaway
The center runs on Huawei Ascend compute. Huawei is on the US Entity List, and BIS's May 2025 guidance warns that using Ascend chips anywhere "risks violating" US export controls. CloudWalk, a partner in both Guangzhou empowerment centers, is on the Entity List and the DoD 1260H list. SenseTime and iFlytek are also Entity-Listed (Xinjiang-related). Visiting is not prohibited. ### Inferences
- **For an Indonesian government delegation:** a site visit and briefing carries essentially no legal exposure. The EAR applies to US-origin items, technology and US persons, and the BIS guidance targets transactions and use, not visits.
- **Partnership caution:** exposure rises if an Indonesian ministry, university or SOE later (a) buys or rents Ascend-based compute, (b) runs workloads that also use US-origin chips or software, or (c) has US partners, US funding, or US-listed subsidiaries. Run Consolidated Screening List checks and seek export-control counsel before any MoU with technical scope.
- **Reputational:** CloudWalk, SenseTime and iFlytek are listed over Xinjiang surveillance concerns. Photos of officials with facial-recognition or surveillance demos may attract criticism in Western or human-rights media. Prefer framing around public services, manufacturing and training.
- **Data:** any pilot that sends Indonesian citizen data to China or receives Chinese data needs analysis under both China's PIPL/DSL and Indonesia's own Personal Data Protection Law (UU PDP 27/2022). The UU PDP was not researched in this pass. Keep MoUs non-binding on data transfer until compliance mapping is done.
- **Balance:** Indonesia's WAICO membership (July 2026) signals official political cover for AI cooperation with China. The delegation should still keep its messaging consistent with Indonesia's non-aligned stance (e.g., also referencing ASEAN, Korea, and US/EU partnerships).
