# Industry Context & Positioning: Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center (广州人工智能融合赋能中心) within Guangzhou / GBA AI Landscape

*Research date: 11 Nov 2026. All figures dated; older data flagged. Facts are listed under "Cited Findings", my own reasoning under "Inferences".*

**Entity disambiguation (important for the report writer).** The search turned up three different Guangzhou "AI empowerment centers". Keep them separate:
- **(A) 广州人工智能融合赋能中心 (Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center).** Unveiled 8 Jun 2022. Run by Guangzhou Radio Group (GRG, 广州无线电集团), a municipal SOE, together with Huawei. It is built on the Guangzhou AI Public Compute Center (Huawei Ascend) and the Guangzhou "Kunpeng+Ascend" Ecosystem Innovation Center. Phase-1 capacity is 100P, with a five-year goal of 1000P. — [gz.gov.cn](https://www.gz.gov.cn/ysgz/xwdt/ysdt/content/post_8327541.html); [21jingji, 9 Jun 2022](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- **(B) 粤港澳大湾区（广州）人工智能应用赋能中心 / 广州市粤港澳大湾区人工智能应用赋能中心 (GBA (Guangzhou) AI Application Empowerment Center).** Established and unveiled on 18 Jun 2026. Its six co-founders are CloudWalk (云从科技), Guangzhou Digital Technology Group (广州数字科技集团), China Telecom Guangzhou, PCI/Jiadu Technology (佳都科技), Guangzhou Yundie Technology (云蝶科技) and the Guangzhou AI Industry Development Promotion Association. — [Sina Finance, 19 Jun 2026](https://finance.sina.cn/2026-06-19/detail-inicwxpe3068067.d.html?vt=4&wm=2824_0024%3Fq%24q&cid=76729&node_id=76729). The municipal 2026 AI Work Points name it explicitly as the body that gathers industry services, tech transfer, going-overseas support and talent. — [Sina, 12 May 2026](https://finance.sina.cn/2026-05-12/detail-inhxqzkt0055327.d.html?vt=4)
- **(C) 广州市卫生健康AI赋能中心 (Guangzhou Health AI Empowerment Center, "穗医智擎").** A sector-specific center. — [21jingji, 22 Jul 2026](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260722/herald/38207bd8526651992f882d334413e892.html)

The Chinese variant "广州市人工智能赋能中心" in the brief could refer to (A) or (B). The rest of these notes cover the policy and industry context for all of them, with (A) as the primary subject.

## 1. What national / provincial / municipal policies created the mandate and funding for AI empowerment centers?

### Takeaway
The mandate is layered.
- **National.** The State Council's "AI+" Opinions (Guofa [2025] No. 11, Aug 2025) set penetration targets: above 70% by 2027 and above 90% by 2030.
- **Provincial.** Guangdong's 2024 "AI Empowering Thousands of Industries" measures and its 2025–2027 Digital Economy Pilot Zone plan name public compute platforms, including the Guangzhou AI Public Compute Center.
- **Municipal.** Guangzhou's Mar 2026 Implementation Plan (Suifubanhan [2026] No. 10) and the May 2026 Work Points explicitly count "empowerment centers" (赋能中心) toward the target of 10 common-technology service platforms by 2030. The Guangzhou AI Development Promotion Regulations (passed Oct 2026) give this a legal basis.
- **Center A's own origin** predates the "AI+" wave. It came out of the 2020–2022 Huawei Ascend "AI computing center" city rollout and the Guangzhou–Huawei strategic agreement of June 2022.

Targets: by 2027, broad, deep integration of AI with 6 key fields, and penetration of new-generation smart terminals and AI agents above 70%. By 2030, above 90%. By 2035, entry into a new stage of the intelligent economy and society. — [Huanqiu](https://finance.huanqiu.com/article/4O4qkn9G9C6); [10jqka summary, 27 Aug 2025](https://news.10jqka.com.cn/20250827/c670668652.shtml)

**District-level subsidies (compute and model vouchers)**
- Guangzhou Development District / Huangpu (Jun 2025), 15 measures:

### Inferences
- Center A was not created by the 2025 "AI+" Opinions. It was a 2022 compute-plus-ecosystem platform in Huawei's city Ascend-center model. The 2026 municipal plan, however, retroactively gives such centers a formal role, since "empowerment centers" count toward the 10-platform 2030 target. The center now sits under a clear national→provincial→municipal mandate chain.
- From mid-2026, the city's official "empowerment center" branding in policy documents points to Center B (CloudWalk-led, application/ecosystem services), not Center A (GRG/Huawei, compute-centric). The report should not conflate them. Center A's relevance in 2026 likely runs through the compute-scheduling and "city intelligent-computing center" provisions.

### Gaps
- No official document found that allocates a specific budget line to Center A. Its funding appears to come from GRG (a municipal SOE) and Huawei, but no amounts were found.
- No source confirmed whether city-level (as opposed to district-level) compute vouchers were issued in Guangzhou.
- Final text of the Oct 2026 Regulations not retrieved.
- The exact years Guangzhou received the MOST pilot-zone and MIIT pilot-zone designations were not confirmed from the original MOST/MIIT notices.

## 2. What are Guangzhou's quantitative AI targets and recent results (2024–2026)?

### Takeaway
Guangzhou's flagship identity is a "vertical model capital" (垂类模型之都).
- **Targets.** 40 new vertical models and 450 scenarios in 2026; 100 vertical models over 2026–2028 or by 2030; 10,000 AI+ firms by 2030.
- **Firm count.** AI-related firms rose from about 1,800 (Mar 2025) to above 2,200 (Jul 2026).
- **Models.** 96 large models filed cumulatively, ranked 3rd nationally (Jul 2026).
- **Value added.** RMB 31.4bn in 1H 2025 (+7.4%).
- **Compute.** Ranked top-5 nationally (IDC 2026), but not top-3.
- **Gap.** No official full-year 2025 AI core industry figure for Guangzhou was found.

## 3. Who are the anchor companies and research institutions in Guangzhou's AI ecosystem?

### Takeaway
Guangzhou's anchors fall into four groups:
- Autonomous driving and intelligent vehicles: Pony.ai, WeRide, XPeng, GAC/Aion.
- Vision AI: CloudWalk, Jiadu/PCI.
- Internet and platform firms in Pazhou: Tencent, Alibaba, Vipshop.
- Municipal SOEs and hardware: GRG Radio Group and GRG Banking, Guangdian Wuzhou (servers), Yuexin Semiconductor.

Academic anchors are SYSU, SCUT, HKUST-Guangzhou and Guangzhou University. Huawei is a strategic partner through Center A and its local supply chain.

## 4. How does Guangzhou's center compare with peer centers elsewhere in China?

### Takeaway
Center A belongs to the Huawei Ascend "AI computing center" generation, alongside Wuhan, Xi'an, Chengdu, Nanjing and Hangzhou. Guangzhou joined later than Wuhan (May 2021) and launched at a similar 100P phase-1 scale. By 2025–2026, peers had moved toward integrated "empowerment" or "model community" formats:
- Beijing Yizhuang: industrial AI empowerment center connected to 5,000P of public compute.
- Shanghai: Model Speed Space (模速空间), with more than 300 model firms.
- Hangzhou: AI Computing Center phase 3 plus the AI Industry Innovation Center.
- Shenzhen: RMB 500m per year in training-compute vouchers and more than 12,000P of compute.

Center A's compute scale (100P to a 1000P goal) is small next to these. Its strengths are SOE ownership, Huawei ties and a manufacturing/AV scenario base.

## 5. What are the main challenges (chip export controls, compute shortage, talent, competition with Shenzhen/Hangzhou), and how does Guangzhou position versus Shenzhen within the GBA?

### Takeaway
Shenzhen leads Guangzhou within the GBA on:
- AI firm count: 2,887 vs ~2,200.
- Industry scale: Shenzhen reported RMB 368.5bn in 2024.
- Compute: Shenzhen ranks #3 nationally versus Guangzhou's top-5.
- Hardware and chips, and subsidy firepower (RMB 500m per year in training vouchers).

Guangzhou's comparative advantages are:
- A larger SOE and municipal-platform base.
- Autos and AV (XPeng, GAC, Pony, WeRide).
- Healthcare and energy scenarios (two national pilot bases).
- A strong large-model filing count (96, 3rd nationally).

Key challenges are compute scale, reliance on Huawei Ascend under US export controls, AI growth trailing the province, and attracting talent against Shenzhen and Hangzhou.

### Cited Findings
- **Shenzhen scale:**
  - 2,887 AI firms (899 in Nanshan) and 2024 AI industry scale of RMB 368.5bn (12.7% of national), per the Shenzhen AI Industry Association white paper (Jun 2025). — [szlh.gov.cn](https://www.szlh.gov.cn/zmhd/wdzsk/content/post_12266361.html)
  - Above 2,600 above-scale AI firms and core-industry revenue of about RMB 220bn as of 2025 (different definition). — [Sina, 27 Apr 2026](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2026-04-27/doc-inhvxatq7891158.shtml)

### Inferences
- **Division of labor in the GBA.** Shenzhen is the hardware, chips, servers and frontier-model and robotics hub with heavier subsidies. Guangzhou is positioning as the applications and "vertical model" city, using SOE scenarios, health, energy, autos and trade/exhibitions (Pazhou). Empowerment centers are Guangzhou's main tool for turning its large scenario supply into model deployments.
- **Center A's exposure to export controls is mixed.** Being Ascend-based shields it from Nvidia supply cuts and aligns it with policy. But Ascend supply is itself constrained nationally and the software ecosystem is less mature, which caps how fast it can reach 1000P.
- **Compute.** Guangzhou's 10,000-card cluster project and city-level scheduling platform could either absorb Center A as a node or overshadow it. This is a strategic uncertainty for the center's positioning.
