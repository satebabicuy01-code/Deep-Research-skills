# Identity & Organization: "Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center" (Guangzhou, China)

Research date: 2026-11-11. The facts are dated below. Most of the founding material is from June 2022. The newest material is from March to August 2026.

## Q1. What are the exact official names (Chinese/English), abbreviations, and aliases? Are there multiple candidates?

### Takeaway
The English name maps most directly to **广州人工智能融合赋能中心** ("Guangzhou AI Integration/Fusion Empowerment Center"). It was unveiled (挂牌) on 8 June 2022 and is built by Guangzhou Radio Group, renamed **Guangzhou Digital Technology Group (广州数字科技集团)** in February 2024, together with Huawei. Three other Guangzhou "AI empowerment center" entities have similar names but are different organizations (see the candidate table). No official English name was found in an English-language primary source.

### Cited Findings
- Official Chinese name: "广州人工智能融合赋能中心". The plaque was unveiled on 8 June 2022 — [gz.gov.cn (Guangzhou municipal portal)](https://www.gz.gov.cn/ysgz/xwdt/ysdt/content/post_8327541.html); [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- Full name of the 2022 event: "广州人工智能融合赋能中心挂牌暨广州人工智能公共算力中心生态入驻仪式" (the center's unveiling, combined with the ecosystem move-in ceremony for the Guangzhou AI Public Computing Center) — [Southern Daily via cnbayarea.org.cn, 2022-06-09](https://www.cnbayarea.org.cn/homepage/news/content/post_978471.html)
- The center is built on two existing facilities. The first is **广州人工智能公共算力中心** (Guangzhou AI Public Computing Power Center). The second is **广州"鲲鹏+昇腾"生态创新中心** (Guangzhou "Kunpeng + Ascend" Ecosystem Innovation Center). Guangzhou Radio Group and Huawei co-founded both in 2020 — [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- The current parent group's official profile (August 2026) states it "承建数字广州创新实验室和广州人工智能融合赋能中心" (built the Digital Guangzhou Innovation Lab and the Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center) — [gz.gov.cn, Municipal SASAC, published 2026-08-11](https://www.gz.gov.cn/zwgk/zdly/gqxx/jbxx/content/post_7801239.html)
- A March 2026 article still uses the name "广州人工智能融合赋能中心" and describes the center as active — [21jingji, 2026-03-29](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260329/herald/492e8c734fa0acf7eda3bb5a78962e50.html)
- **Candidate 2: 广州市粤港澳大湾区人工智能应用赋能中心** (Guangzhou Greater Bay Area AI Application Empowerment Center).
  - Unveiled on 18 June 2026 at 羊城创意产业园 (Yangcheng Creative Industry Park).
  - Six organizations jointly initiated it: 广州数字科技集团, China Telecom Guangzhou Branch, PCI Technology (佳都科技), CloudWalk (云从科技), 广州云蝶科技, and 广州市人工智能产业发展促进会 (Guangzhou AI Industry Development Promotion Association).
  - It is described as a "公共服务平台" (public service platform) following "公益性、中立性、权威性" principles (public-interest, neutral, authoritative).
  - Source: [21jingji / 羊城派, 2026-06-18](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260618/herald/9f1f54c690c02d85b13a6c4f97c8fa0b.html)
- **Candidate 3: "Guangzhou global AI empowerment center"** in Huangpu District.
  - The district government planned it, with about 60,000 m² of floor area. The goal is to attract 10–20 hard-tech firms.
  - It was announced in August 2023 and no operator was named.
  - Source: [China Daily (govt.chinadaily.com.cn), 2023-08-07](https://govt.chinadaily.com.cn/s/202308/07/WS64e095c5498ed2d7b7e98bd2/huangpu-to-build-global-ai-empowerment-center.html)
- **Candidate 4: "AI Industry Empowerment and Innovation Center"** in Nansha.
  - It was announced at the 2024 Greater Bay Area AI Industry Conference.
  - It aims to provide training, research, exhibition, investment, and consulting services.
  - This comes from a search-result summary and the primary page was not fetched, so it is unverified. Search pointer: [China Daily search results, 2024](https://govt.chinadaily.com.cn/s/202308/07/WS64e095c5498ed2d7b7e98bd2/huangpu-to-build-global-ai-empowerment-center.html)
- The draft Guangzhou AI Development Promotion Regulation (《广州市人工智能发展促进条例》) includes building a unified compute-scheduling platform and "应用赋能中心" (application empowerment centers). The draft was scheduled for a third reading and vote in October 2026 — [search summary of gz.gov.cn pages, 2026](https://www.gz.gov.cn/szgzkfgx/zdgz/content/post_10968256.html) (not fetched directly)

### Inferences
- "Integration" is the standard translation of **融合** in 融合赋能. That makes 广州人工智能融合赋能中心 the closest literal match to "Guangzhou AI Integration & Empowerment Center". Candidate 2 uses 应用 ("Application") rather than 融合, and Candidates 3 and 4 have no 融合.
- **Most likely target of an Indonesian delegation: 广州人工智能融合赋能中心**, for these reasons:
  - Its name matches most closely.
  - It is a long-running facility (since 2022) with showrooms. In 2022, guests toured an "AI算力赋能行业运用展" (AI computing industry-application exhibition) and the Kunpeng+Ascend center.
  - Its parent group is the municipal state-owned flagship. The group says its products reach 120+ countries, and its subsidiaries GRGBanking (广电运通) and Hi-Target/Haige (海格通信) have international business.
- Candidate 2 (June 2026) is plausible if the visit took place after June 2026. Its English name would more likely be "...AI Application Empowerment Center". Because Guangzhou Digital Technology Group initiates both centers, the same host organization is likely either way.
- The Huangpu center (Candidate 3) was still a planned building in 2023. No evidence was found that it is operating.

### Gaps
- No official English name for 广州人工智能融合赋能中心 was found on any official English-language site (China Daily, GDToday, Newsgd). The English used here is a translation.
- No press report was found of an Indonesian delegation visiting any of these centers. The searches covered Chinese terms for Indonesian delegation, Guangzhou, and AI empowerment center.
- No abbreviation or alias (for example an "AI融合中心" short form) was found in the sources.

## Q2. Who owns, operates, and sponsors it, and what is its legal/organizational form?

### Takeaway
It is **not an independent legal entity** that could be found. It is a platform, or "center", that Guangzhou's municipal state-owned enterprise group builds and runs: Guangzhou Radio Group, now Guangzhou Digital Technology Group Co., Ltd. (广州数字科技集团有限公司). Huawei is its core technology partner, and it runs under the guidance of the Guangzhou municipal government. By 2026 its director was a deputy general manager of 广州数据集团 (Guangzhou Data Group), which is a member company of the Digital Technology Group.

### Cited Findings
- The center was co-built ("共建") by 广州无线电集团 (Guangzhou Radio Group), 华为技术有限公司 (Huawei), and other organizations. Guangzhou Radio Group builds and operates the Guangzhou AI Public Computing Center using "自主可控人工智能根技术" (self-controlled AI root technology) — [Southern Daily via cnbayarea.org.cn, 2022-06-09](https://www.cnbayarea.org.cn/homepage/news/content/post_978471.html)
- The Kunpeng+Ascend Ecosystem Innovation Center runs "在广州市人民政府指导下" (under the guidance of the Guangzhou Municipal People's Government). Guangzhou Radio Group and Huawei lead it, and **广电研究院** (GRG Research Institute) builds and operates it — [Guangzhou MIIT Bureau (gxj.gz.gov.cn) PDF](https://gxj.gz.gov.cn/attachment/7/7460/7460707/9253349.pdf), as quoted in a search summary
- Rename of the parent group:
  - Guangzhou Radio Group (founded 1995) traces back to the state-run 国营广州无线电装修厂 (1956). It was reorganized as **广州数字科技集团有限公司** in February 2024.
  - The group calls itself "广州市首家国有资本投资公司" (Guangzhou's first state-owned capital investment company) and a 国有企业集团 (state-owned enterprise group).
  - The Municipal SASAC (市国资委) publishes the group's profile.
  - Source: [gz.gov.cn, 2026-08-11](https://www.gz.gov.cn/zwgk/zdly/gqxx/jbxx/content/post_7801239.html)
- A Shenzhen Stock Exchange disclosure confirms the controlling-shareholder name change to 广州数字科技集团有限公司, with no change in actual controller or shareholding — [SZSE disclosure, 2024-02-04](https://disc.static.szse.cn/disc/disk03/finalpage/2024-02-04/52575f13-08f9-41d7-ab8b-49e6c61920b7.PDF), as summarized in a search result
- About the group:
  - It has 200+ companies.
  - It controls five A-share listed companies: 广电运通 (GRGBanking), 海格通信 (Haige Communications), 广电计量 (GRG Metrology & Test), 广哈通信, and 中科江南.
  - Its member companies include 广州数据集团 (Guangzhou Data Group), 数科产投, 数科产业园, 广州低空, and others.
  - Its core is the "1+4+N" digital industry system built around the **aiCore System** AI open innovation platform.
  - It reports R&D spending above 12% of revenue and products and services in 120+ countries and regions.
  - Source: [gz.gov.cn, 2026-08-11](https://www.gz.gov.cn/zwgk/zdly/gqxx/jbxx/content/post_7801239.html)
- Group Deputy GM 程欧 also serves as the group's Chief Data Officer and as director and GM of 广州数据集团有限公司 — [search summary of personnel notice](https://www.gz.gov.cn/zwgk/zdly/gqxx/jbxx/content/post_7801239.html) (summary only; this may be out of date, because the August 2026 leadership list names 李瑜 as Chief Data Officer)
- In March 2026, the center's director 李学军 was identified as "广州数据集团副总经理" (deputy general manager of Guangzhou Data Group) — [21jingji, 2026-03-29](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260329/herald/492e8c734fa0acf7eda3bb5a78962e50.html)
- Ecosystem partners that moved into the Public Computing Center at the June 2022 launch:
  - 广州工业投资控股集团 (Guangzhou Industrial Investment Holding Group)
  - 中国工业互联网研究院 (China Academy of Industrial Internet)
  - 联通（广东）产业互联网 (Unicom Guangdong Industrial Internet)
  - 云从科技 (CloudWalk)
  - 广州大学 (Guangzhou University)
  - 安必平 (Anbiping)
  - 暗物智能 (DMAI)
  - 轩辕网络 (Xuanyuan Network)
  - Huawei
  - Reports say "8 partners", but the list names 9 including Huawei. Sources: [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html); [Southern Daily, 2022](https://news.southcn.com/node_54a44f01a2/419b7af56d.shtml)
- At the same 8 June 2022 event, the Guangzhou municipal government and Huawei signed a comprehensive strategic cooperation agreement. The **广州数字技术创新中心** (Guangzhou Digital Technology Innovation Center) was also unveiled, with 200+ companies and 7,700+ R&D staff involved — [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- Tianhe District policy (2025): operators of public intelligent-computing centers recognized at national, provincial, or municipal level can receive up to RMB 1 million per year — [gz.gov.cn PDF](https://www.gz.gov.cn/gzzcwjk/attachment/7/7781/7781781/10166411.pdf), as summarized in a search result

### Inferences
- The legal form is most likely an **SOE-run platform or brand** inside the Guangzhou Digital Technology Group system, not a 事业单位 (public institution) or a government agency. No business registration for an entity named "广州人工智能融合赋能中心" was found.
- Day-to-day operation appears to have moved from GRG Research Institute in 2022 to Guangzhou Data Group by 2026. This is based on the director's affiliation and is not confirmed by an official document.
- The ultimate owner is Guangzhou Municipal SASAC, because the group is a municipal SOE.

### Gaps
- No registration record, funding figure, or budget was found for the center itself.
- No staff headcount was found. One source mentions only a "technical team".
- No governance documents (board, steering committee) were found.

## Q3. When was it launched, what were the milestones, and what goals or KPIs were announced?

### Takeaway
Timeline: the predecessor computing centers were founded in 2020, the center's plaque was unveiled on 8 June 2022, and in 2025–2026 it repositioned as a "six-in-one" platform. In December 2025 it coordinated an Embodied AI Joint Lab. The launch KPIs were 100P of AI compute in phase one and 1,000P within five years.

### Cited Findings
- **2020:** Guangzhou Radio Group and Huawei co-founded the Kunpeng+Ascend Ecosystem Innovation Center and the Guangzhou AI Public Computing Center — [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- **8 June 2022:** The plaque was unveiled at 广电平云广场 (GRG Pingyun Plaza). Before the ceremony, guests toured an "AI算力赋能行业运用展" (AI computing industry-application exhibition) and the Kunpeng+Ascend center — [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html); [gz.gov.cn](https://www.gz.gov.cn/zt/gzydyl/jsxd/content/post_8328334.html)
- **Launch KPIs:**
  - Phase one plans **100P** of AI computing power. The five-year target is **1,000P**, where 1P means "运算速度每秒百亿亿次" (10^18 operations per second).
  - In the early stage, 300+ companies had visited to discuss cooperation and 70+ had applied for services.
  - Source: [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- The computing center follows a "一中心、多节点" (one center, multiple nodes) layout — [search summary of gz.gov.cn and 21jingji coverage](https://www.gz.gov.cn/ysgz/xwdt/ysdt/content/post_8327541.html)
- **December 2025:** The center coordinated the formation of an **具身智能联合实验室** (Embodied AI Joint Laboratory) with 广电计量, 广电运通, and 海格通信. The lab focuses on industrial manufacturing, healthcare, and security inspection — [21jingji, 2026-03-29](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260329/herald/492e8c734fa0acf7eda3bb5a78962e50.html)
- **By March 2026:** The center was described as upgraded from a public computing center providing affordable compute to a "六位一体的融合赋能中心" (six-in-one integration and empowerment center) covering compute, data, models, applications, security, and ecosystem — [21jingji, 2026-03-29](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260329/herald/492e8c734fa0acf7eda3bb5a78962e50.html)
- Case study (2026):
  - The technical team moved a client's audio processing from online to offline inference and optimized it for Ascend.
  - Quote: "在完全不新增算力硬件的前提下，单条音频处理耗时从超5秒压缩至近3秒，整体效率提升近40%" ("without adding any compute hardware, processing time per audio clip fell from over 5 seconds to nearly 3 seconds, an overall efficiency gain of nearly 40%").
  - Source: [21jingji, 2026-03-29](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260329/herald/492e8c734fa0acf7eda3bb5a78962e50.html)

### Inferences
- The 2026 repositioning matches the city's 2026 "数智融合" (digital-intelligence integration) agenda. The center appears to be moving from selling compute toward model and application services.

### Gaps
- No source confirms whether the 100P phase-one or 1,000P five-year targets were reached.
- The exact inauguration date of the 2020 computing center was not found.
- No 2024 milestones were found.

## Q6. What is its mission and positioning statement?

### Takeaway
At launch, the mission was to be a public service platform for AI application scenarios and for commercializing research, offering affordable compute to government, industry, and academia. By 2026 it described itself as a "six-in-one" platform covering compute, data, models, applications, security, and ecosystem.

### Cited Findings
- 2022 positioning:
  - The center was to be a "应用场景建设和创新成果产业化的公共服务平台" (public service platform for building application scenarios and commercializing innovations).
  - Target sectors were intelligent manufacturing, autonomous driving, biomedicine, and smart cities.
  - It was to focus on the compute needs of Guangzhou's key industrial chains and represent Guangzhou in global industrial cooperation.
  - It was to develop industry interface standards, data circulation, and a Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao digital asset base.
  - Source: [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- 2022 goals:
  - "为政府、企业和科研院校提供普惠的人工智能算力服务" (provide affordable AI compute to government, enterprises, and research institutions).
  - Help Guangzhou become a "数字经济引领型城市和国际一流智慧城市" (a digital-economy-leading city and a world-class smart city).
  - Promote the Greater Bay Area AI ecosystem.
  - Source: [Southern Daily via cnbayarea.org.cn](https://www.cnbayarea.org.cn/homepage/news/content/post_978471.html)
- The Public Computing Center is called "广州市的新型信息基础设施和人工智能的数字底座" (Guangzhou's new information infrastructure and AI digital foundation) — [21jingji, 2022-06-09](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20220609/herald/a816a1ad610574380bee2fb291611a56.html)
- 2026 positioning: "六位一体的融合赋能中心" (算力、数据、模型、应用、安全、生态), a six-in-one integration and empowerment center covering compute, data, models, applications, security, and ecosystem — [21jingji, 2026-03-29](https://m.21jingji.com/article/20260329/herald/492e8c734fa0acf7eda3bb5a78962e50.html)
- For comparison, Candidate 2's positioning is a hub integrating "供需对接、产研协同、成果孵化、人才培养" (supply-demand matching, industry-research collaboration, incubation, talent training). Its six modules are supply-demand matching, full-chain talent training, joint industry standards, one-stop compliance, frontier R&D, and GBA collaboration and global value-chain restructuring — [21jingji, 2026-06-18](https://www.21jingji.com/article/20260618/herald/9f1f54c690c02d85b13a6c4f97c8fa0b.html)

### Inferences
- Indonesian visitors would probably be briefed on the center as a showcase of China's domestic AI stack (Huawei Kunpeng/Ascend), affordable compute, and industry applications from the group's subsidiaries (banking AI from GRGBanking, metrology, communications).
