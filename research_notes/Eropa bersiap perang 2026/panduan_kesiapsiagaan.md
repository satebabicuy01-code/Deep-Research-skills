# What European governments and the EU actually tell citizens to do to prepare for war or crisis (fact-check, as of 2 Nov 2026)

Research date: 2 November 2026. Method: web search and page fetches (16 tool calls). Source quality varies and is flagged inline. Items marked "(secondary)" come from aggregators or tabloids and were not checked against the official page.

## Q1. The EU level: Preparedness Union Strategy (Mar 2025), the 72-hour kit, Lahbib's video, the Stockpiling Strategy (Jul 2025) and 2026 follow-ups

### Takeaway
The EU advice to households is non-binding: keep enough essentials to last at least 72 hours. It comes from the Preparedness Union Strategy of 26 March 2025, which has 63 actions. The July 2025 stockpiling strategy is about government and EU-level reserves, not household hoarding. I found no new EU household advice in 2026. The 2026 activity is implementation: a citizens' panel, a Special Eurobarometer on 30 Oct 2026, and the European Civil Protection Forum on 3–4 Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 26 Mar 2025: the Commission adopted the EU Preparedness Union Strategy "to prevent and react to emerging threats and crises" — [EC DG ECHO](https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/eu-preparedness-union-strategy-prevent-and-react-emerging-threats-and-crises-2025-03-26_en)
- The strategy encourages the public to keep essential supplies for at least 72 hours. This is a non-binding target, not an EU-wide legal requirement. The strategy also covers minimum preparedness criteria for essential services, stockpiling of critical equipment, an EU Crisis Hub and an EU Preparedness Day — [EC DG ECHO](https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/eu-preparedness-union-strategy-prevent-and-react-emerging-threats-and-crises-2025-03-26_en); [UNEP LEAP summary](https://leap.unep.org/en/countries/eu/national-legislation/european-preparedness-union-strategy)
- 26–27 Mar 2025: media headlines read "EU urges citizens to stockpile 72 hours' worth of supplies amid war risk" (CNN) and "EU tells public to stockpile 72 hours of supplies in case of disaster or attack" (ITV). These headlines are the source of the "stockpile" framing — [CNN, 26 Mar 2025](https://www.cnn.com/2025/03/26/europe/european-union-stockpile-member-states-intl-latam); [ITV, 27 Mar 2025](https://www.itv.com/news/2025-03-27/eu-tells-public-to-stockpile-72-hours-of-supplies-in-case-of-disaster-or-attack)
- The EU's recommended items are food, water, a torch, ID papers, medicine and a shortwave or battery radio. The EU's "strategic reserves" are government-held items such as firefighting planes and medical equipment, not household weapons (summary of CNN and other coverage) — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/03/26/europe/european-union-stockpile-member-states-intl-latam)
- Commissioner Hadja Lahbib (crisis management) made an official video that parodies beauty ads and walks through the contents of a survival kit. It was viewed almost 4 million times — [TSA Algérie](https://www.tsa-algerie.com/hadja-lahbib-commissaire-europeenne-parle-de-sa-maman-algerienne/) (secondary; the exact publication date is not in this source and was March 2025, when the strategy was launched)
- July 2025, EU Stockpiling Strategy: it sets up an EU stockpiling network, identifies gaps in reserves, expands EU-level stockpiles and strengthens transport and logistics for crisis response — [Eastflank](https://eastflank.eu/eastern-flank/eastflank-report/72-hours-or-two-weeks-europe-takes-different-approaches-to-how-prepared-people-should-be-for-a-crisis) (secondary analysis)
- 30 Oct 2026, Special Eurobarometer on EU civil protection: 9 in 10 Europeans say it is important that the EU helps coordinate disaster response, 94% say Member States should help one another, 91% want coordinated EU action strengthened, and 91% support EU help for countries outside the EU. Lahbib: "Europeans want a Europe that stands by them when disaster strikes." — [EC DG ECHO, 30 Oct 2026](https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/europeans-overwhelmingly-support-stronger-eu-civil-protection-cooperation-new-eurobarometer-shows-2026-10-30_en)
- 2026: the European Citizens' Panel on Population Preparedness brought together 150 citizens, who produced 20 recommendations. These will be presented at the European Civil Protection Forum on 3–4 Nov 2026. The Commission also runs a "We Act as One" preparedness campaign — [EC DG ECHO, 30 Oct 2026](https://civil-protection-humanitarian-aid.ec.europa.eu/news-stories/news/europeans-overwhelmingly-support-stronger-eu-civil-protection-cooperation-new-eurobarometer-shows-2026-10-30_en); [Citizens' Panel page](https://citizens.ec.europa.eu/european-citizens-panels/citizens-panel-preparedness_en)

### Inferences
- The wording that went viral ("EU tells 450 million to stockpile") makes a non-binding 72-hour recommendation sound like an order. "At least 72 hours" is a minimum kit, not hoarding.
- The Civil Protection Forum on 3–4 Nov 2026 is the next likely news hook: the citizens' recommendations will be presented there the day after this cut-off.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the Stockpiling Strategy's official Commission press release (July 2025), so I could not confirm the exact date or details on the primary source. Its exact date (about 9 July 2025) and any medical-countermeasures component are unverified here.
- The Lahbib video's exact date and any criticism of it: none of the sources I found mention a backlash.
- The Eurobarometer page did not give the share of citizens who hold an emergency kit or who fear war.

## Q2. Country by country: national guidance

### Takeaway
Most national guidance asks for about 72 hours of supplies: the EU, France, the UK, Poland, the Netherlands, Lithuania and Finland. Norway asks for one week and Germany for 10 days. Sweden's brochure (Nov 2024) is the most war-explicit: "an armed attack against Sweden cannot be ruled out." In 2026 the new items are: Poland's household handbook (Jan 2026) and air-attack guidance (Sept 2026); the UK "Be Ready" campaign (22 Oct 2026); Germany's military service law in force from 1 Jan 2026 and its shelter cataloguing plan (May 2026); and France's "Tous responsables" guide (published 20 Nov 2025).

### Cited Findings

**Sweden**
- Nov 2024 (from week 47): the new edition of "Om krisen eller kriget kommer / In case of crisis or war" was sent to about 5 million households, at the government's request. It says an armed attack against Sweden "cannot be ruled out" — [MCF (official)](https://www.mcf.se/en/advice-for-individuals/the-brochure-in-case-of-crisis-or-war); [MCF download page](https://www.mcf.se/en/advice-for-individuals/the-brochure-in-case-of-crisis-or-war/download-and-order-the-brochure-in-case-of-crisis-or-war/)
- The civil-defence agency MSB was replaced by MCF (Myndigheten för civilt försvar, the Swedish Civil Defence and Resilience Agency), with a focus on war rather than crisis — [Sveriges Radio](https://www.sverigesradio.se/artikel/swedish-agency-gets-a-rebrand-focus-on-war-rather-than-crisis)
- The brochure also says most people have an obligation to help in a war or threat of war, and warns about terrorism, cyberattacks and disinformation — [Daily Caller, 27 Oct 2026](https://dailycaller.com/2026/10/27/europe-distributes-preparedness-plans-case-crisis-war-uk-german-sweden/) (secondary)
- I found no new 2026 edition of the Swedish brochure. The Oct 2026 articles recycle the 2024 edition — [MCF](https://www.mcf.se/en/advice-for-individuals/the-brochure-in-case-of-crisis-or-war)

**Germany**
- Official guidance (website of the Federal Ministry of Food, Agriculture and Homeland) is to keep supplies for 10 days, with about 2 litres of liquid per person per day. It cites war as well as peacetime crises such as animal diseases and accidents at industrial plants — [Daily Caller, 27 Oct 2026](https://dailycaller.com/2026/10/27/europe-distributes-preparedness-plans-case-crisis-war-uk-german-sweden/) (secondary)
- Shelters: BBK head Ralph Tiesler (2025) said only 580 shelters remained, with room for about 480,000 people, and proposed converting underground car parks, metro tunnels and basements into shelter for about 1 million people — [The Defense Post, 19 May 2026](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/05/19/germany-civil-defense-bunkers-plan/); [Euronews, 26 Nov 2024](https://euronews.com/my-europe/2024/11/26/germany-plans-to-turn-buildings-into-bomb-shelters)
- May 2026: a proposed civil-defence package of about €10 billion (reported as $11.6 billion), backed by Interior Minister Alexander Dobrindt, would catalogue public shelters nationwide. The data would feed into the NINA warning app so people can find the nearest shelter and evacuation routes — [The Defense Post, 19 May 2026](https://thedefensepost.com/2026/05/19/germany-civil-defense-bunkers-plan/)
- Military service: the Bundestag passed the new military service law on 5 Dec 2025. From 2026, male citizens who turn 18 must fill in a questionnaire about their willingness to serve; for women it is voluntary. From 1 Jul 2027, a medical examination is mandatory for men born in 2008 or later. A lottery-based compulsory draft is possible only if the volunteer quotas are not met, or if the defence situation requires it, and only with a further decision by the Bundestag — [deutschland.de](https://www.deutschland.de/en/news/bundestag-approves-law-for-new-military-service); [OSW, 10 Dec 2025](https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-12-10/germanys-military-service-reform-voluntarism-its-core); [Bundesregierung](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-en/news/new-draft-military-service-law-2397962); [Berlin Herald](https://berlinherald.com/germany-mandates-bundeswehr-screening-for-18-year-old-men-under-2026-law/)
- There is conflicting reporting on the Bundesrat step: one government release said the Bundesrat still had to approve the law, while later reports describe it as in force from 1 Jan 2026 — [Bundesregierung](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-en/news/new-draft-military-service-law-2397962) vs [Berlin Herald](https://berlinherald.com/germany-mandates-bundeswehr-screening-for-18-year-old-men-under-2026-law/)

**France**
- 20 Nov 2025: the government published "Tous responsables", a free survival guide for households of about 27 pages, downloadable from info.gouv.fr/risques in accessible, easy-to-read and print versions. It covers natural disasters, terrorism, industrial and nuclear accidents, health crises, cyberattacks, conflict and information chaos — [The Local France, 20 Nov 2025](https://www.thelocal.fr/20251120/world-in-crisis-france-publishes-new-emergency-survival-guide); [franceinfo](https://www.franceinfo.fr/environnement/catastrophes-naturelles/le-gouvernement-publie-tous-responsables-un-guide-de-survie-destine-aux-francais-en-cas-de-crise_7628807.html); [info.gouv.fr (official)](https://www.info.gouv.fr/risques/se-preparer-a-une-situation-durgence)
- Kit contents: a 72-hour kit with drinking water (6 litres per person), a first aid kit, copies of documents, warm clothing, a survival blanket, a torch and medication. Check it every 6 months — [The Local France](https://www.thelocal.fr/20251120/world-in-crisis-france-publishes-new-emergency-survival-guide); [SDIS49](https://www.sdis49.fr/actualites/tous-responsable-le-guide-de-servie-publie-par-le-gouvernement/)
- Context: in March 2025 there were reports that the Bayrou government planned to send a survival manual to every household — [Yahoo/AFP](https://www.yahoo.com/news/security-fears-grip-europe-france-130054715.html)
- Sources disagree on distribution: early reports (2025) said it would be sent to every household, while the version actually published in Nov 2025 is mainly a free download — [Yahoo](https://www.yahoo.com/news/security-fears-grip-europe-france-130054715.html) vs [The Local](https://www.thelocal.fr/20251120/world-in-crisis-france-publishes-new-emergency-survival-guide)

**Poland**
- Jan 2026: the government began mailing a printed safety handbook to about 17 million households. It covers threats from cyberattacks to military situations and ends with safety "commandments", including keeping 3 days of supplies and knowing your nearest shelter — [Notes from Poland, 12 Jan 2026](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/01/12/government-sends-safety-handbook-to-every-household-in-poland/) (one secondary source puts it at 54 pages: [Streamline Feed](https://streamlinefeed.co.ke/news/poland-issues-comprehensive-air-attack-guidance-and-expands-shelter-network-amid-rising-russian-threats))
- Sept 2026: the Interior Ministry (MSWiA) issued guidance on air attacks: go to a shelter point with an evacuation backpack and medication, use the stairs not the lift, and if the shelter is far, stay in a windowless central room. There are separate guidelines for schools — [Notes from Poland, 25 Sep 2026](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/09/25/poland-issues-guidance-on-responding-to-air-attacks-and-warns-of-russia-threat/); [Ukrainska Pravda, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/09/24/8054948/)
- Shelters: about 85,000 shelter points with room for 23.7 million people (64% of the population). A draft regulation requires them to be marked by the end of 2026 (blue triangle on an orange background, plus a green "Punkt schronienia" sign). A shelter-locator app has existed since 2025. A national evacuation plan is in preparation. The ministry denied social media claims that the plan is meant to permanently displace Poles — [Notes from Poland, 25 Sep 2026](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/09/25/poland-issues-guidance-on-responding-to-air-attacks-and-warns-of-russia-threat/)
- Triggers for the Sept 2026 guidance: a Russian drone strike on a Kyiv–Warsaw passenger train about 2 km from the border, about 20 Russian drones entering Polish airspace in one night in 2025, and a Russian missile exploding in a field in eastern Poland in 2026. Tusk and Sikorski warned that Russia may be planning a major action this year — [Notes from Poland, 25 Sep 2026](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/09/25/poland-issues-guidance-on-responding-to-air-attacks-and-warns-of-russia-threat/)
- The civilian "Always Ready" programme teaches survival skills such as building shelter and making fire, not combat. Up to 400,000 participants are expected in 2026 (about 1% of the population) — [TVP World, 29 Mar 2026](https://tvpworld.com/92317848/are-poles-ready-for-war-and-crisis); [France 24, 11 Jun 2026](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260611-poland-prepares-stand-ground-against-potential-threats)

**United Kingdom**
- 22 Oct 2026: the Cabinet Office launched "Be Ready", the first phase of a three-year programme. It asks households to be able to cope with 3 days of disruption to power, water, phones and the internet. The checklist covers 9 categories: water (3 litres per person per day for drinking, plus more for cooking and washing), non-perishable food, hygiene products, a torch and batteries, blankets and warm clothing, a battery or wind-up FM radio, a power bank, cash, copies of ID, a first aid kit and medication, baby formula and nappies, and pet food. More than 500 organisations are sharing the materials. Security Minister Dan Jarvis: "Those who can follow this advice should follow this advice." — [UK Defence Journal, 22 Oct 2026](https://ukdefencejournal.org.uk/households-asked-to-prepare-for-three-days-of-disruption/)
- The stated rationale is everyday emergencies (storms, wildfires, cyberattacks, supply chain disruption), bringing the UK in line with France, Ireland and the Netherlands. It is not about war. Separately, ministers confirmed a Defence Readiness Bill giving government powers "during escalation towards war" — [UK Defence Journal](https://ukdefencejournal.org.uk/households-asked-to-prepare-for-three-days-of-disruption/)
- The government stressed it was "not a warning of an imminent threat" and urged people to be "alert but not alarmed" — [LBC, 27 Oct 2026](https://www.lbc.co.uk/news/uk/baked-beans-water-batteries-supermarket-shelves-stockpile/)
- Ministerial titles: LBC and the Daily Caller name "Defence Secretary Wes Streeting", while UK Defence Journal quotes Dan Jarvis as Security Minister. I did not check either title against gov.uk — [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/news/uk/baked-beans-water-batteries-supermarket-shelves-stockpile/); [Daily Caller](https://dailycaller.com/2026/10/27/europe-distributes-preparedness-plans-case-crisis-war-uk-german-sweden/)

**Norway, Finland, Denmark, Netherlands, Lithuania** (secondary and aggregator sources only)
- Norway (DSB) raised its advice from 72 hours to one week in May 2024 and mailed a brochure to every household in autumn 2024 — summary from search results ([Radar.ph fact check](https://radar.ph/fact-check-countries-are-stockpiling-food-and-water-but-not-for-the-reason-you-think/); [Eastflank](https://eastflank.eu/eastern-flank/eastflank-report/72-hours-or-two-weeks-europe-takes-different-approaches-to-how-prepared-people-should-be-for-a-crisis))
- Finland: at least 72 hours, covering power cuts, water outages, failed telecoms and military threats. One outlet claims 3.5 million copies of a guide would be mailed to every household and business in January; this is a single-source claim and unverified — [Eastflank](https://eastflank.eu/eastern-flank/eastflank-report/72-hours-or-two-weeks-europe-takes-different-approaches-to-how-prepared-people-should-be-for-a-crisis); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/map-shows-countries-urging-residents-to-stockpile-food-12629524)
- Denmark: sources disagree. One says the advice stays at "three days" of independent living; another says new guidance was issued without stating a duration — [LADbible, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.ladbible.com/news/world-news/eu-countries-stockpiling-advice-conflict-power-outage-weather-349905-20260922) (secondary)
- Netherlands: a 72-hour emergency kit. An Oct 2026 campaign reportedly focused on scenarios such as childcare during emergencies and helping neighbours — [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/news/world-news/eu-countries-stockpiling-advice-conflict-power-outage-weather-349905-20260922); [UK Defence Journal](https://ukdefencejournal.org.uk/households-asked-to-prepare-for-three-days-of-disruption/) (the latter confirms only the 3-day figure)
- Lithuania: the "LT72" programme, named for the first 72 hours of an emergency — [Eastflank](https://eastflank.eu/eastern-flank/eastflank-report/72-hours-or-two-weeks-europe-takes-different-approaches-to-how-prepared-people-should-be-for-a-crisis)
- One fact-check summary: most official recommendations cover 3–7 days, some up to 10 days (Germany), not "months" as viral claims say — [Radar.ph fact check](https://radar.ph/fact-check-countries-are-stockpiling-food-and-water-but-not-for-the-reason-you-think/)

### Inferences
- There is a clear spectrum. At the "war-explicit" end are Sweden, Poland and Finland, which are geographically close to Russia. At the "all-hazards / everyday emergencies" end are the UK, the Netherlands and the EU in its official wording.
- Germany is the outlier on duration (10 days). That advice is decades old, not a new 2025–2026 escalation (the BBK has recommended 10 days since well before 2022, though this was not re-verified on bbk.bund.de in this session).

### Gaps
- Finland (72tuntia.fi / suomi.fi, shelter capacity of about 4.8 million), Norway (DSB), Denmark (the 2025 brochure), Belgium, Estonia, Latvia and Switzerland (universal shelter-place law): I did not fetch the official pages because of the tool budget. The figures above for these countries are secondary only. Switzerland and Belgium were not covered at all.
- Germany's "Operationsplan Deutschland" (OPLAN DEU) was not covered in this session.
- Macron's latest statements (2025–2026) on preparedness or service were not covered.
- I did not verify whether Germany's BBK released a new brochure in 2025–2026.

## Q3. Weapons: does any government tell civilians to stockpile weapons?

### Takeaway
No. I found no official source in the EU or any member state that tells civilians to stockpile or buy weapons. What exists is voluntary military training (Poland), a military service questionnaire for 18-year-old men (Germany), civilian survival programmes that do not teach combat (Poland, "Always Ready"), and long-standing reservist and volunteer-defence-league structures.

### Cited Findings
- The EU kit list contains no weapons. The EU's "strategic reserves" are government-held items such as firefighting planes and medical equipment — [CNN, 26 Mar 2025](https://www.cnn.com/2025/03/26/europe/european-union-stockpile-member-states-intl-latam)
- Fact-check summary: "I found no source saying citizens are told to stockpile weapons." The "prepare for World War III" framing is described as a misreading of routine civil-defence advice — [Radar.ph fact check](https://radar.ph/fact-check-countries-are-stockpiling-food-and-water-but-not-for-the-reason-you-think/); [Amuse newsletter, "No, Europe Did Not Just Tell Its Citizens to Prepare for World War III"](https://newsletter.amuseonx.com/p/no-europe-did-not-just-tell-its-citizens) (commentary)
- Poland, 7–11 Mar 2025: Tusk announced voluntary military training for adults, with a target of 100,000 people a year from 2027 (about 35,000 a year previously). Sources disagree on scope: some say men aged 18–60, others men and women. It is voluntary with incentives (for example a truck licence and drone training). Ministers would also take part — [AP via Washington Times, 7 Mar 2025](https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2025/mar/7/military-training-adult-males-boost-reserves-planned-polands-tusk/); [Stars and Stripes, 11 Mar 2025](https://stripes.com/theaters/europe/2025-03-11/poland-military-training-100,000-volunteers-conscription-nato-17109144.html); [TVP World](https://tvpworld.com/85538886/polish-pm-tells-govt-ministers-they-will-undergo-voluntary-military-training)
- The headline "military training for every adult man in Poland" is inaccurate. The programme is voluntary, with a target of 100,000 a year — [Stars and Stripes](https://stripes.com/theaters/europe/2025-03-11/poland-military-training-100,000-volunteers-conscription-nato-17109144.html)
- Poland's civilian "Always Ready" programme teaches survival skills, not combat — [TVP World, 2026](https://tvpworld.com/92317848/are-poles-ready-for-war-and-crisis)
- Germany: from 2026 the obligation is a questionnaire for 18-year-old men, not a draft. A draft would require a further decision by the Bundestag — [OSW](https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-12-10/germanys-military-service-reform-voluntarism-its-core)
- The UK Be Ready checklist contains no weapons — [UK Defence Journal](https://ukdefencejournal.org.uk/households-asked-to-prepare-for-three-days-of-disruption/)

### Inferences
- The "hoard weapons" claim looks like a conflation of three things: (a) the 72-hour supply advice, (b) military training and service programmes, and (c) rising civilian gun-licence numbers in Poland. Point (c) was not verified in this session.

### Gaps
- I did not verify data on civilian gun-ownership changes (for example, rising firearm licences in Poland, the Baltics or Finland), Finland's reservist culture, or Baltic volunteer leagues (Kaitseliit, the Lithuanian Riflemen's Union, Zemessardze) in this session.
- I did not find whether AFP Fact Check has a specific article on the "weapons" claim.

## Q4. What triggered the new wave of coverage (Sept–Nov 2026), and new guidance in 2026

### Takeaway
The main trigger in late Oct 2026 was the launch of the UK "Be Ready" campaign on 22 Oct 2026, followed by panic buying in UK supermarkets and a wave of articles (Daily Caller, 27 Oct; LADbible; aggregators) that lumped it together with the old guidance from Germany and Sweden. Before that, Poland's air-attack guidance (25 Sep 2026), amid Russian drone and missile incidents, raised the "war" tone. The Eurobarometer (30 Oct) and the Civil Protection Forum (3–4 Nov 2026) added EU-level coverage.

### Cited Findings
- 22 Oct 2026: launch of UK "Be Ready" — [UK Defence Journal](https://ukdefencejournal.org.uk/households-asked-to-prepare-for-three-days-of-disruption/)
- 23 Oct 2026: a "Clash Report" post on X bundled the UK, Germany and Sweden together. On 27 Oct 2026 the Daily Caller ran "Europe distributes preparedness plans in case of 'crisis or war'" (republished by AOL and BizPacReview), presenting the 2024 Swedish brochure and Germany's old 10-day advice as if they were new — [Daily Caller, 27 Oct 2026](https://dailycaller.com/2026/10/27/europe-distributes-preparedness-plans-case-crisis-war-uk-german-sweden/); [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/europe-distributes-preparedness-plans-case-145949000.html); [BizPacReview](https://www.bizpacreview.com/2026/10/27/europe-distributes-preparedness-plans-in-case-of-crisis-or-war-1665037/)
- The Daily Caller article also linked the US "Fortress America" programme (Department of War directive, 30 Sep 2026: military bases self-reliant for 90 days), which widened the "war" framing — [Daily Caller](https://dailycaller.com/2026/10/27/europe-distributes-preparedness-plans-case-crisis-war-uk-german-sweden/)
- 22 Sep 2026: LADbible, "What EU countries are telling citizens to stock up on as global WW3 fears rise" — an example of sensational framing — [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/news/world-news/eu-countries-stockpiling-advice-conflict-power-outage-weather-349905-20260922)
- An X trending topic: "Europe and Asia Urge Households to Stock 72-Hour Emergency Kits" (it also covers Japan) — [X trending](https://x.com/i/trending/2115139302183063657) (low reliability)
- 24–25 Sep 2026: Poland's air-attack guidance after a drone strike on a Kyiv–Warsaw train near the border and a Russian missile falling in eastern Poland in 2026 — [Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2026/09/25/poland-issues-guidance-on-responding-to-air-attacks-and-warns-of-russia-threat/)
- New in 2026: Poland's household handbook (Jan), Poland's air-attack guidance (Sept), the UK Be Ready campaign (Oct), the Netherlands Oct campaign (secondary), Germany's shelter catalogue plan (May), Germany's military service law (Jan), and EU Eurobarometer and citizens' panel recommendations (Oct–Nov) — see the citations in Q2 and Q1

### Inferences
- Much of the "Europe is preparing for war" coverage in Oct 2026 is recycled: Sweden's brochure is from Nov 2024 and Germany's 10-day advice is long-standing. The genuinely new item, the UK campaign, is explicitly framed around everyday emergencies rather than war.

### Gaps
- The search summary mentions a Reuters report that an "internet outage" showed the importance of the Be Ready campaign. I could not verify this, and the LBC article (27 Oct) mentions no outage. Treat it as unverified.
- I found no source confirming a new Swedish or Finnish booklet in autumn 2026.

## Q5. Public reaction: panic buying, survival-kit sales, surveys

### Takeaway
There was measurable panic buying in the UK after Be Ready (late Oct 2026): canned food, bottled water, radios and torches. The EU Eurobarometer of 30 Oct 2026 shows very high support for EU coordination, but the public page does not give figures on fear or kit ownership.

### Cited Findings
- UK, after 22 Oct 2026: Iceland's sales of tinned products rose 40% over the weekend. Robert Dyas's radio sales rose 160% week on week. At Maplin, torch sales nearly quadrupled and radio sales almost doubled within 3 days (MD Ollie Marshall). Ocado ran out of bottled water (Volvic, Highland Spring, Brecon Carreg), batteries, baked beans and some soups. ### Inferences
- The UK panic buying was short-lived and concentrated in a few product categories (water, canned food, radios), which is consistent with the 3-day checklist and not with long-term "hoarding".

### Gaps
- No numerical surveys of fear of war (for example national polls in Germany, France or Poland, 2025–2026) were found in this session.
- I found no sales data for survival kits in France or Germany, and no 2026 data on panic buying outside the UK.
