# Europe's security situation, 2025 to 2 November 2026: the events behind the "Europe preparing for war" coverage

Research note. Every finding has a source. Coverage runs from 2025 to 2 November 2026, with most weight on 2026. Search results for 2026 events were mostly secondary reporting (Wikipedia summaries, wire-based outlets). Where a claim rests on a single or weak source, the bullet says so.

## 1. Russia–Ukraine war in 2026: front lines, US-brokered talks, whether a deal exists, Russian strength

### Takeaway
As of 2 November 2026 there is **no peace deal and no general ceasefire**. There have been several short truces, and the latest is a local truce in Oleshky (Kherson oblast) brokered by Melania Trump and started on 27 October 2026. US-brokered trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi are expected to resume "after 4 November", but no date has been fixed. Russia still demands all of Donetsk oblast. Ukraine wants to freeze the current front line. The front is moving slowly: Russia took Huliaipole and reportedly Pokrovsk/Myrnohrad around the turn of 2025–26, and Ukraine made small local gains in the south.

### Cited Findings
**Peace talks timeline**
- **23–24 Jan 2026:** the first trilateral US–Russia–Ukraine talks in Abu Dhabi. It was the first known meeting of all three sides since the full-scale invasion. Territory was the main issue. — [ABC News (AU)](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-01-24/ukraine-russia-us-meeting-in-abu-dhabi-peace-deal/106264648); [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/23/ukraine-russia-us-talks-open-in-abu-dhabi-with-territory-as-key-issue)
- **4–5 Feb 2026:** second round in Abu Dhabi. US envoy Steve Witkoff said the sides agreed to exchange 314 prisoners of war, the first swap in five months. Russia demanded that Ukraine withdraw from all of Donetsk oblast, and Kyiv refused. Ukraine proposed freezing the current front lines. Peskov called the Donbas demand "a very important condition". Russia occupies about 20% of Ukraine. — [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/2/4/russia-ukraine-war-second-round-of-peace-talks-set-to-begin-in-abu-dhabi); [News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/russia-ukraine-us-conclude-second-round-of-peace-talks-in-abu-dhabi)

- **Early Oct 2026:** Russia stepped up its attacks while the US pushed for talks. The US effort to broker a ceasefire had been paused since Washington began its war with Iran. — [CNBC, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/ukraine-war-russia-kyiv-putin-zelenskyy-trump.html)
- **4 Oct 2026:** Zelensky said Ukraine was ready for US-backed talks with Russia. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/4/ukraine-ready-for-us-backed-talks-with-russia-zelenskyy)
- **24 Oct 2026:** Ukraine and Russia were looking at new talks, but "steep obstacles remain". - **27 Oct 2026:** a six-day local ceasefire, brokered by Melania Trump, began at 03:00 in occupied Oleshky (Kherson oblast). Its purpose is humanitarian aid and evacuation for about 2,000 remaining residents. Russia kept attacking Ukrainian cities elsewhere. The timing depends "in part on battlefield conditions and the broader situation". Zelensky had suggested 10 November. — [Meduza](https://meduza.io/amp/en/news/2026/10/29/kremlin-says-u-s-russia-ukraine-peace-talks-will-take-place-in-abu-dhabi-after-november-4-depending-on-battlefield-conditions)
- **30 Oct 2026:** speaking at a Russia–Africa summit in Moscow, Putin said Russia is "ready" for war with Europe but does not want one, and that whoever wants such a conflict should prepare for it. — [Daily Post UK, low-reliability aggregator](https://www.dailypost-uk.com/2026/11/putin-says-russia-is-ready-for-war-with.html). Confirm this against Reuters or AP.

**Front line**
- Russia took Huliaipole (Zaporizhzhia oblast) in January 2026. — [Wikipedia: Huliaipole offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huliaipole_offensive)
- Russia was reported to have captured Pokrovsk and Myrnohrad in early 2026. Since December 2025 it has made no significant advances west of the two towns. — [Wikipedia: Pokrovsk offensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pokrovsk_offensive)
- In February 2026 Ukraine began a local counteroffensive in the south. ISW assessed that Ukraine controlled Ostapivske, Nechaivka and Radisne, and later Pryvillia and Zlahoda. Zelensky claimed 300 km² retaken. — [Wikipedia: 2026 Southern Ukraine counteroffensive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Southern_Ukraine_counteroffensive)
- **11 Aug 2026 (ISW):** Russian attacks toward Pokrovsk continued, with no confirmed advances on 10–11 August. A Russian milblogger said progress was only "tens of meters" because of the density of Ukrainian drones. ISW saw signs of Russian infiltration in Plavni (western Zaporizhzhia) but no confirmed advances. — [ISW/Critical Threats, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.criticalthreats.org/analysis/russian-offensive-campaign-assessment-august-11-2026)
- In March 2026 a front-mapping analysis said Russian advances were slow enough that Zaporizhzhia city's defensive ring was unlikely to be tested for more than a year. — [WarMapper, 11 Mar 2026](https://warmapper.substack.com/p/ukraine-update-11-march-2026) (secondary source)

### Inferences
- The war is still a war of attrition with a slow-moving front. The diplomatic track is active (Abu Dhabi), but the core territorial dispute over Donetsk is unresolved. US attention has also been split by the Iran war since March 2026.
- Putin's 30 October remarks and the delayed talks feed directly into the "Europe preparing for war" coverage in late October and early November 2026.

### Gaps

The ISW finding of "no comparable signs of preparation against the Baltic states" comes from a search summary only and was not checked against the original. — [search summary referring to ISW]

## 2. Russian hybrid attacks on Europe, 2025–2026

### Takeaway
2025 set the pattern: drones over Poland, Russian jets in Estonian airspace, cable sabotage, arson and a railway explosion. In 2026 it escalated. The key event was an attempted attack with an **explosive-laden drone on Leipzig/Halle airport, a NATO airlift hub, on 4 August 2026**. Germany formally blamed Russia on 1 September. A wave of sabotage followed in August and September. In October 2026 Romania attributed the arson at ROMARM Brașov to Russia, and the Bundeswehr raised its sabotage threat assessment to the second-highest level.

### Cited Findings
**2025**
- **January 2025:** Finland said the crew of the seized tanker (Eagle S, December 2024 incident) were ready to cut more cables when they were caught. Finland later charged the crew with sabotaging undersea cables (reported August 2025). — [Wikipedia: Russian sabotage operations in Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_sabotage_operations_in_Europe)
- **17 Mar 2025:** Lithuanian prosecutors said Russia was behind the May 2024 IKEA arson in Vilnius. **May 2025:** Poland said the GRU ordered the May 2024 Marywilska 44 mall fire in Warsaw, and closed the Russian consulate in Kraków. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_sabotage_operations_in_Europe)
- **26 Jun 2025:** Rheinmetall military trucks were set on fire in Erfurt, Germany, with three destroyed. Russian direction is suspected. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_sabotage_operations_in_Europe)
- **Late Aug–Sep 2025:** GPS jamming hit the aircraft carrying Ursula von der Leyen over Bulgaria. NATO officials blamed Russian electronic warfare, though Bulgarian authorities disputed this. **4 Sep 2025:** Sweden attributed a surge in GPS jamming over the Baltic to Russia. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_sabotage_operations_in_Europe)
- **9–10 Sep 2025:** about 19–20+ Russian drones entered Polish airspace and NATO aircraft shot some down. Poland invoked **NATO Article 4**. — [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11446135/nato-warns-russia-airspace-violations/amp); [KUTV](https://kutv.com/news/nation-world/nato-surges-military-forces-to-eastern-europe-in-response-to-russian-drone-incursions-sentry-defenes-polish-airspace-mark-rutte-president-donald-trump). Al Jazeera reports that four airports were closed and **only two** drones were shot down. — [Al Jazeera, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/11/new-level-of-danger-drone-incidents-scare-europe-amid-russia-ukraine-war)
- **12 Sep 2025:** NATO launched **Operation Eastern Sentry** in response, a deployment of allied assets on the eastern flank that includes counter-drone measures. — [KUTV](https://kutv.com/news/nation-world/nato-surges-military-forces-to-eastern-europe-in-response-to-russian-drone-incursions-sentry-defenes-polish-airspace-mark-rutte-president-donald-trump)
- **19 Sep 2025:** three Russian MiG-31s entered Estonian airspace over the Gulf of Finland for 12 minutes. Estonia requested Article 4 consultations, and Russia denied any violation. Accounts differ on which aircraft intercepted them: Italian jets, or F-35s with Swedish and Finnish support. — [Jakarta Post/Reuters](https://www.thejakartapost.com/world/2025/09/20/russian-jets-enter-estonias-airspace-in-latest-test-for-nato); [The War Zone](https://www.twz.com/air/russian-mig-31s-busting-estonian-airspace-has-eastern-europe-on-edge); [ESD](https://euro-sd.com/2025/09/major-news/46819/migs-in-estonian-airspace/)
- **16–18 Nov 2025:** sabotage on the Warsaw–Lublin railway (near Mika and Puławy). Tusk called it sabotage and alleged that Ukrainians working for Russia carried it out. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_sabotage_operations_in_Europe)
- AP has counted about **200 incidents** of sabotage and other hostile activity in Europe that Western officials blame on Russia since the invasion. — [search summary citing AP; see NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/02/g-s1-141502/germany-russia-attempted-drone-attack)

**2026**
- Wikipedia has articles titled "2026 Ukrainian drone incursions into the Baltic states" and "Øresund drone incident". The Baltic incursions were attributed to Ukrainian drones diverted, probably by Russian electronic interference. Details were not read. — [Wikipedia: 2026 drone incursions into the Baltic states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Ukrainian_drone_incursions_into_the_Baltic_states); [Wikipedia: Øresund drone incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%98resund_drone_incident)
- **15 Jun 2026:** Russian-born satirist Semyon Skrepetsky was shot dead in Biała Podlaska, Poland. No perpetrator has been named. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_sabotage_operations_in_Europe)
- **4 Aug 2026, Leipzig/Halle:** a quadcopter carrying plastic explosives got past the air defences of the NATO airlift hub and hit a Ukrainian cargo plane, but did not explode. It was found four hours later. Interior Minister Dobrindt called it "a new level of danger". The WSJ reported that US intelligence believes the drone was Russian. — [Al Jazeera, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/11/new-level-of-danger-drone-incidents-scare-europe-amid-russia-ukraine-war); [Wikipedia: 2026 Leipzig Airport drone attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Leipzig_Airport_drone_incidents)
- **Early Aug 2026:** Lithuanian Defence Minister Robertas Kaunas alleged that Moscow is planning a false-flag attack on Baltic critical infrastructure using captured Ukrainian drones. Poland and the Baltic states tightened security at energy sites. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/11/new-level-of-danger-drone-incidents-scare-europe-amid-russia-ukraine-war)
- **10 Aug–3 Sep 2026:** a six-week cluster of sabotage hit drone makers, munitions plants and defence electronics firms in five NATO states. — [Discovery Alert, single low-reliability source](https://discoveryalert.com/analysis/russia-european-sabotage-nato-supply-chain/). In the same period, according to the search summary, Tusk said Poland had been hit by new sabotage including arson, and a NATO jet in Lithuania shot down a drone that probably carried explosives and came from Belarus. — [search summary; not verified against a primary source]
- **1 Sep 2026:** Germany formally blamed Russia for the attempted Leipzig attack and announced it would close a Russian consulate. Rutte expressed "full solidarity". The Russian embassy denied involvement. — [NPR, 2 Sep 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/02/g-s1-141502/germany-russia-attempted-drone-attack)
- A German federal police document reportedly counts about 160 possible sabotage incidents and more than 700 suspicious drone sightings in 2026. — [search summary; original source not identified]
- **14 Sep 2026:** fire at the ROMARM Carfil industrial site in Brașov, Romania. **26 Oct 2026:** Romanian intelligence (SRI) and prosecutors (DIICOT) said a sabotage network coordinated from Russia carried out the arson. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_sabotage_operations_in_Europe)
- **5 Oct 2026:** BND chief Martin Jäger told the Bundestag that Germany risks being drawn into a "violent conflict" with Russia, and called the threat "new, more dangerous". — [Washington Times](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/oct/5/germany-warns-war-russia/); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/german-spy-chief-sees-new-more-dangerous-threat-from-russia); [ABC AU](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-06/ukraine-germany-russia-violent-conflict-/107231422)
- **About 21 Oct 2026:** a classified Bundeswehr analysis, leaked to WDR and Süddeutsche Zeitung, raised the Russian sabotage threat to "substantial", the second-highest level and the first time it has reached that level. It judges that Russia now likely has greater intent, expects short-term escalation, and calls Russian drone use a crossed "red line". — [Defense News, 21 Oct 2026](https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2026/10/21/german-military-raises-sabotage-threat-level-amid-warning-of-russian-attacks-report/); [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/87790)
- **21 Oct 2026:** Chancellor Merz named Russia and its hybrid threats as Germany's biggest security threat. — [Dig Watch](https://dig.watch/updates/chancellor-merz-russia-germany-security-threat)
- Russia dismisses the claims about drone incursions as "scare stories". — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/11/new-level-of-danger-drone-incidents-scare-europe-amid-russia-ukraine-war)

### Inferences
- The Leipzig attack in August 2026 was a qualitative jump: a drone with explosives against NATO military logistics, not just surveillance or disruption. The chain that followed (attribution on 1 Sep, BND on 5 Oct, Bundeswehr level raised on 21 Oct, Romanian attribution on 26 Oct) explains why coverage in September and October 2026 intensified.

### Gaps
- Not verified: drones over Copenhagen, Oslo, Munich, Brussels and Liège airports (September–November 2025), and Baltic cable incidents in 2026. Searches returned no source for these.
- No confirmed major cyberattack in 2026 was found.
- The Lithuania drone shoot-down and the Polish arson of August–September 2026 come only from search summaries.

## 3. The 2026 US–Iran war and the closure of the Strait of Hormuz

### Takeaway
The war began on **28 February 2026** with US and Israeli strikes; Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei was killed. Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz. A ceasefire on 8 April and the Islamabad MoU on 17 June did not last: fighting resumed on **8 July 2026**, and the US blockade was reinstated. Trackers say the strait is still effectively closed in early November 2026 (day about 246). European states declined to join militarily in March ("not our war", Pistorius) but backed reopening after a truce. The impact on Europe is energy prices and shipping, plus diverted US attention and munitions.

### Cited Findings
- **28 Feb 2026:** US–Israeli strikes began and Khamenei was killed. Iran closed the strait. **2 Mar:** the IRGC said the strait was closed to "unfriendly nations". **4 Mar:** the IRGC claimed "complete control". — [Wikipedia: 2026 Strait of Hormuz campaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign)
- **16 Mar 2026:** Germany, Spain, Italy, Estonia, the UK and the EU declined Trump's request to secure the strait. Pistorius said: "this is not our war, we have not started it." — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign)
- **19 Mar 2026:** the US began an air campaign to reopen the strait. On the same day five large European states and Japan said they would help secure it "after first establishing a truce". **20 Mar:** 22 countries, including the UK, France and Germany, said they were willing to contribute to "safe passage". — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign)
- **7 Apr 2026:** Russia and China vetoed a UNSC resolution (sponsored by Bahrain) authorising "all necessary means". France also opposed authorising force. **8 Apr:** a ceasefire was declared, though attacks on ships continued. **12–13 Apr:** talks in Islamabad failed and Trump announced a naval blockade of Iranian ports, fully in place by 15 April. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign)
- **11 Jun 2026:** the IMO counted 46 attacks on ships, 14 seafarers killed, and about 1,000 ships and 20,000 crew stranded in the Gulf. **17 Jun:** Trump and Pezeshkian signed the Islamabad MoU. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign)
- **8 Jul 2026:** the truce collapsed after Iran allegedly struck commercial ships. The US hit Iranian territory and Iran hit US bases in the Gulf. **14–15 Jul:** the US blockade was reinstated. **25 Aug:** more than 100 mines were cleared from the shipping lanes. **27 Sep:** Iran claimed it had captured a US REMUS 600 underwater drone. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_campaign)
- **Early Nov 2026:** a live tracker reports the strait "closed, day 246". The IRGC says two tankers exploded after hitting mines on an unauthorised route, and UKMTO reported a vessel hit by an unknown projectile. — [straits.live, low-reliability tracker](https://straits.live/)
- Energy: Brent rose to about USD 80–82 in early March. Traffic stayed far below pre-war levels even after the ceasefire. Europe was expected to be hit hard over the medium term, with the UK the worst-hit major economy. The Red Sea route to Europe was at 49% of pre-crisis capacity and was disrupted again. — [search summary of CRS R45281](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R45281); [Carra Globe](https://carraglobe.com/strait-of-hormuz-closure-2026/); see also [Wikipedia: 2026 Iran war fuel crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_fuel_crisis)
- The Iran war paused the US effort to broker a ceasefire in Ukraine. — [CNBC, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/ukraine-war-russia-kyiv-putin-zelenskyy-trump.html). Washington is waiting on developments in the Middle East before setting a date for Ukraine talks. — [Washington Times/AP, 28 Oct 2026](https://www.washingtontimes.com/news/2026/oct/28/six-day-ceasefire-ukrainian-city-holding-officials-say-moscow-kyiv/)
- Wikipedia has an article on "Russia in the 2026 Iran war", suggesting a Russian role. Not read. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_in_the_2026_Iran_war)

### Inferences
- For Europe the Iran war matters in three ways: higher energy and shipping costs; US military and diplomatic attention pulled away from Europe and Ukraine; and Russia benefiting from higher oil revenue and US distraction. This last point is inference only; no source checked.

### Gaps
- Specific 2026 data on European gas and oil prices, and on EU missions (Aspides) or European naval deployments to Hormuz, was not found.
- The status of the strait in October and November is confirmed only by a low-reliability tracker. The claim of an Iran–Oman revenue-sharing deal is unconfirmed.

## 4. US commitment to NATO under Trump: troop cuts, the December 2025 NSS, Greenland, and the Hague and Ankara summits

### Takeaway
The US is staying in NATO and signed the Ankara declaration in July 2026. But it is shrinking its presence: a brigade left Romania in October 2025, and in May 2026 it ordered about 5,000 troops out of Germany. Politically, the December 2025 NSS warned of Europe's "civilizational erasure", and the Greenland crisis of January–September 2026 included threats of force and tariffs against Denmark and other allies. This pushes Europe toward self-reliance ("a stronger Europe in a weaker NATO").

### Cited Findings
- **October 2025:** the Pentagon pulled one brigade combat team out of Romania; about 1,000 troops left, with a similar number remaining at three bases. — [Euronews, 3 Nov 2025](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/11/03/us-troop-drawdown-in-romania-signals-wider-us-pivot-away-from-europe); [GlobalSecurity/RFE-RL](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2025/10/mil-251029-rferl04.htm)
- **Early December 2025 (NSS):** the National Security Strategy said Europe's economic decline "is eclipsed by the real and more stark prospect of civilizational erasure". It questioned whether some European countries will stay "reliable allies", sought to end "the perception… of NATO as a perpetually expanding alliance", and called an "expeditious cessation of hostilities in Ukraine" a core US interest. German FM Wadephul rejected the "outside advice". — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/europe-will-unrecognizable-20-years-due-immigration-white-house-strate-rcna247534); [Kyiv Post](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/65687); [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/11563636/trump-national-security-strategy-europe-world-middle-east)
- The FY2026 NDAA bars cutting US troops in Europe below 76,000 for more than 45 days unless certain conditions are met. About 85,000 are currently in Europe. — [Euronews, 5 May 2026](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/05/05/can-us-law-stop-trump-from-withdrawing-troops-from-europe)
- **1 May 2026:** Hegseth ordered about 5,000 troops out of Germany (one brigade combat team) within 6–12 months. A planned long-range fires battalion deployment was cancelled. The Pentagon said this returns levels to roughly pre-2022. — [Stars and Stripes, 1 May 2026](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/europe/2026-05-01/trump-withdraw-5000-troops-germany-21550153.html); [ABC News](https://abcnews.com/International/us-withdraw-5000-troops-germany/story?id=132587562); [Stars and Stripes, 3 May 2026](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/europe/2026-05-03/germany-trump-troop-cuts-21561959.html)
- **28 Jul 2026 (single source):** the Pentagon began a six-month review of US forces in Europe. A rotational armoured brigade of about 4,000 troops bound for Poland and Eastern Europe was reportedly cancelled or delayed. — [ORF](https://www.orfonline.org/expert-speak/us-troop-withdrawal-from-europe-a-blessing-in-disguise)
- **Greenland crisis:** in January 2026 Trump would not rule out force. **17 Jan 2026:** he announced an extra 10% tariff on Denmark, the UK and six other European countries, with a 25% threat unless Greenland was ceded. **21 Jan 2026 (Davos):** he appeared to rule out force. Protests in Greenland were the largest in its history. **18 Sep 2026:** Trump announced a deal he said gives the US "permanent control" over Greenland's security, while Greenland stays Danish. **22 Sep 2026:** the US, Denmark and Greenland signed a new defence agreement supplementing the 1951 agreement. — [Wikipedia: Greenland crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland_crisis); [Al Jazeera, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/18/trump-claims-deal-with-denmark-gives-us-permanent-control-over-greenland); [CNN, 18 Sep 2026](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/trump-us-greenland-security); [House of Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-12214/)
- **Hague summit 2025:** its outcome was a target of **5% of GDP for defence by 2035**, which the Ankara summit reaffirmed. — [NATO, 8 Jul 2026](https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2026/07/08/secretary-general-on-the-ankara-summit-nato-delivers); [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/dispatches/eleven-takeaways-from-the-nato-summit-in-ankara/)
- **Ankara NATO summit, 7–8 Jul 2026:** reaffirmed Article 5 and the Hague 5% target. Other outcomes, per the search summary of NATO, the Atlantic Council and CSIS pages:
  - more than USD 50bn in new procurement
  - the "Drone Edge" initiative, about USD 40bn for counter-drone systems over five years
  - EUR 27bn to modernise fuel storage and pipelines toward the eastern flank
  - at least EUR 70bn in military aid to Ukraine this year and next
  - 11 allies jointly buying Saab GlobalEye to replace part of the AWACS fleet
  - a "Front Door for Industry"
