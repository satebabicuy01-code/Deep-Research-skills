## 1. JCI/IHSG performance 2024, 2025 and YTD 2026 (local and USD) vs EM/ASEAN; major drawdowns and triggers

### Takeaway
After a flat-to-down 2024 and a strong rupiah-terms 2025 (+22%, record highs driven by domestic investors while foreigners sold), Indonesia became the world's worst-performing equity market in 2026. The JCI is down about 40% YTD in rupiah and about 48% in USD (EIDO proxy), and it is about 43% below its 20 Jan 2026 record of 9,134. The 2026 collapse came from a market-governance and investability shock (MSCI free float, ownership opacity, "goreng-goreng" coordinated trading), layered on top of earlier policy shocks: Danantara/fiscal worries in March 2025, the removal of Sri Mulyani in September 2025, and the resignation of BI Governor Perry Warjiyo in July 2026.

### Inferences
- The two largest 2026 drawdowns came from index-provider and market-governance events (free float, opaque ownership, coordinated trading), not from nationalisation or expropriation. But investor commentary consistently bundles them with "growing state control of key industries", BI independence and fiscal credibility ([Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/10/17/2003866114)). Markets are pricing a broad institutional-credibility discount, of which the "state capitalism" thesis is one component.
- The 2025 rally (+22%) happened alongside foreign selling. It was a domestic-liquidity rally concentrated in a few tightly held stocks, which is the very "free-float" problem MSCI later targeted (see sections 3 and 6).

## 2. Foreign flows (equities, SBN ownership), 10-year yield, CDS, rupiah

### Takeaway
Foreigners have been net sellers of Indonesian equities in 2024 (disputed), 2025 and 2026. 2026 is on track to be the worst year on record (about $4.9–6bn out by October). Foreign ownership of rupiah government bonds fell to about 12.6% in April 2026, a roughly 19-year low, versus nearly 40% in 2019. The 10-year yield rose about 145bp YTD to about 7.64% (29 Oct 2026). The rupiah is near its lifetime low of 18,190/USD.

### Inferences
- The share of SBN held by foreigners dropped from about 40% to about 13%, so Indonesia's rates market is now mainly domestically held (banks, BI, insurers). This lowers its sensitivity to foreign sudden stops, but it also means foreigners have already voted with their feet. BI's 2026 hiking cycle, running against a pro-growth government, is a key signal investors watch for BI independence.

## 3. MSCI/FTSE index treatment and reclassification risk

### Takeaway
MSCI has escalated in steps since January 2026: a freeze on increases and additions (Jan), deletion of six names (May), a negative "information flow" assessment (Jun), deferral of its review (Jul), and an Oct 2026 consultation on reclassifying Indonesia from Emerging to **Standalone** (a "double downgrade" that skips Frontier). The decision is expected by end-June 2027, though reports conflict on the date. FTSE Russell deleted high-concentration stocks in June 2026. Indonesia's weight in MSCI EM has shrunk to about 0.4%.

### Cited Findings
- **27 Jan 2026 MSCI announcement:** "Fundamental investability issues persist due to ongoing opacity in shareholding structures." MSCI also cited possible coordinated trading. Interim measures took effect immediately: a freeze on all increases to FIF and NOS, no additions to MSCI IMI, and no upward size-segment migration. MSCI would reassess market accessibility if progress was insufficient by May 2026. Possible outcomes included a reduced weighting for all Indonesian securities in MSCI EM, or reclassification to Frontier — [MSCI](https://app2.msci.com/webapp/index_ann/DocGet?pub_key=4YgVKowBJiE%3D&lang=en&format=html).
- **May 2026:** MSCI removed six companies from its Indonesia index. FTSE Russell said it would delete Indonesian shares with high shareholding concentration, effective 22 Jun 2026 — [Reuters factbox](https://lufkindailynews.com/news_reuters/business/factbox-indonesia-suffers-a-series-of-market-sentiment-setbacks-in-2026/article_d3b4edca-baa8-5685-b4c0-8e7803f8e9cc.html).
- **June 2026:** MSCI's annual market accessibility review downgraded Indonesia's "information flow" assessment to negative and left other criteria unchanged. MSCI had designated Indonesia an EM in 1989 — [Fortune, 22 Jun 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/06/22/indonesia-awaits-msci-verdict-13-billion-capital-outflow/).
- **July 2026:** MSCI deferred its Indonesia review from July to November, saying a downgrade was still possible — [Reuters factbox](https://lufkindailynews.com/news_reuters/business/factbox-indonesia-suffers-a-series-of-market-sentiment-setbacks-in-2026/article_d3b4edca-baa8-5685-b4c0-8e7803f8e9cc.html).
- **15–16 Oct 2026:** MSCI said Indonesia's EM classification "is no longer appropriate". Feedback suggested that Frontier "would not properly recognise the depth of the market problems", so it opened a consultation on Standalone status. Concerns cited were opaque shareholding, suspected coordinated trading, and a lack of meaningful enforcement — [Reuters factbox](https://lufkindailynews.com/news_reuters/business/factbox-indonesia-suffers-a-series-of-market-sentiment-setbacks-in-2026/article_d3b4edca-baa8-5685-b4c0-8e7803f8e9cc.html); [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/10/17/2003866114). The consultation was launched on 15 Oct, with the final decision expected by end-June 2027 — [The Standard HK](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/345674/Indonesia-stocks-sink-most-since-1998-after-MSCI-flags-standalone-downgrade). The Taipei Times text says "June" and also quotes an analyst saying "June 2027" (an internal inconsistency). Minister Airlangga said MSCI had promised a November announcement but released the warning early — [Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/business/indonesian-stocks-end-at-2026-low-after-losing-1157-on-msci-warning). As a Standalone market, Indonesia would sit alongside markets such as Palestine and Lebanon — [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/10/17/2003866114).
- **Weight and size:** Indonesia is about 0.4% of MSCI EM (Mirae Asset, Oct 2026) — [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/10/17/2003866114). The free-float market cap of the MSCI Indonesia Index fell from $119.14bn (31 Dec 2025) to $53.36bn (30 Sep 2026) — [l0g.fr analysis](https://l0g.fr/en/analysis/msci-indonesia-free-float-index-risk/) [Second-hand].
- **Passive outflow estimates:** Goldman (Jan 2026) estimated more than $13bn: $7.8bn MSCI-linked plus $5.6bn if FTSE also changed its free-float methodology — [IDNFinancials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/60860/goldman-and-ubs-cut-rating-on-indonesian-equities). Goldman (Oct 2026) estimated about $5.6bn of passive outflows from full EM removal — [Kucoin/flash summary of Goldman note] [Second-hand](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/indonesian-stocks-hit-six-year-low-amid-msci-downgrade-fears). Mirae estimated about $3bn and Citi "mid-single-digit billions" — [Taipei Times](https://www.taipeitimes.com/News/front/archives/2026/10/17/2003866114).
- **Reforms announced:** Minimum free float raised from 7.5% to 15%. The shareholder disclosure threshold was cut from 5% to 1%. There is more scrutiny of affiliated holdings — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/22/indonesia-awaits-msci-verdict-13-billion-capital-outflow/); [Glass Lewis](https://www.glasslewis.com/article/free-float-ownership-transparency-governance-quality-what-indonesia-market-stress-revealed). Peer free-float minimums: HK 25%, India 25%, Malaysia 25%, Australia about 20%, Singapore 12–15% — [Glass Lewis](https://www.glasslewis.com/article/free-float-ownership-transparency-governance-quality-what-indonesia-market-stress-revealed).

### Inferences
- MSCI's grievance is about market integrity (who really owns the free float, related-party "stock frying"), not about state ownership as such. SOE stocks with clear majority state holdings are, if anything, more transparent than opaque conglomerate structures. For the thesis test, the index risk is driven more by a crony and conglomerate governance problem than by "socialism".

## 4. Valuations and house views (banks, asset managers, rating agencies)

### Takeaway
Global sell-side houses moved underweight early and stayed there. Morgan Stanley went UW in June 2024 and again in February 2025. JPMorgan went UW in November 2024 and later moved to neutral. Goldman cut to market weight in early 2025, then to UW in January 2026 (maintained in October 2026). UBS also cut in January 2026. The stated reasons evolved from fiscal uncertainty and FX, through weak domestic growth and ROE, to Danantara/Sri Mulyani credibility, and finally to MSCI investability, BI independence and state control. Moody's and Fitch both moved to negative outlooks in 2026. SOE bank valuations have de-rated sharply (BBRI P/B about 2.0x in 2024 to about 1.3x now).

- Sell-side reasoning moved from macro (fiscal/FX, 2024) to institutional (Danantara, Sri Mulyani, BI independence, market integrity, 2025–26). This is the clearest market evidence that investors price "state direction" as a governance and credibility risk premium.

## 6. Macro: GDP growth, forecasts, credit, consumer confidence

### Takeaway
Official growth has held at about 5% (Q1 2026 5.61% y/y), but its credibility is questioned (LPEM estimates 4.6–4.9%). The IMF (5.1%) and World Bank (5.2%) forecasts for 2026 sit below the government's 5.4% target. Market stress has come despite headline growth, not because of a growth collapse.

### Cited Findings
- **BPS:** Q1 2026 GDP +5.61% y/y, versus 5.4% in Q4 2025, led by household consumption — [BPS, 5 May 2026](https://www.bps.go.id/en/pressrelease/2026/05/05/2575/indonesia-s-economic-growth-in-q1-2026-was-5-61-percent--y-on-y-.html); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/2026/05/05/indonesia-crushes-forecasts-with-56pc-gdp-surge-despite-us-iran-war/218783).
- **LPEM-UI:** The Q1 2026 headline is likely overstated. Its corrected estimate is about 4.6–4.9%, based on conflicting manufacturing data — [LPEM](https://lpem.org/?sdm_process_download=1&download_id=21117).
- **IMF:** 5.1% for 2026, raised 0.1pp in the Oct 2026 WEO vs July — [Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/business/imf-upgrades-indonesias-2026-growth-forecast-to-51).
- **World Bank:** 5.2% for 2026 and 2027 (Oct 2026 EAP update), up from 4.7%, because the energy-price drag was smaller than expected — [Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2121029/world-bank-sees-5-2-growth-for-indonesia-whats-behind-the-revision). An earlier WB call was 5.0% for 2026 because of fiscal pressures — [Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/world-bank-says-indonesias-gdp-to-slow-to-5-in-2026-as-fiscal-pressures-rise-4736621).
- **Government target:** 5.4% for 2026, with potential of 5.6% (Airlangga) — [search summary of Jakarta Globe/Tempo coverage].

### Inferences
- The disconnect between steady reported GDP and a 40% equity crash suggests markets are pricing governance, institutional and policy risk (including state-directed allocation and data credibility) rather than near-term growth.
