# BUMN / Danantara: size and trajectory of Indonesia's state corporate footprint (as of 30 Oct 2026)

Scope note: research was capped at ~17 tool calls. Several requested series (full 2014–2026 assets/revenue tables, PMN by year, IDX SOE market-cap share, OECD cross-country SOE/GDP for Indonesia) were only partly found; these are listed under Gaps. All figures are dated. "Official" = Danantara/BP BUMN/Ministry statements; "independent" = media calculations, analysts, think tanks.

## 1. How big is the SOE sector (count, assets, revenue, profit, dividends, % of GDP) and is it growing?

### Takeaway
SOE consolidated assets more than doubled in nominal terms from ~Rp4,577T (2014, 119 SOEs) to ~Rp10,400–10,950T (2023–2024), roughly half of GDP; Danantara now claims ~US$900bn of assets across ~1,068–1,074 entities (holdings, subsidiaries, sub-subsidiaries). Reported profits are volatile and repeatedly restated (2024 profit cut from ~Rp304T to ~Rp186T; 2025 profit quoted anywhere from Rp276T to Rp332T), so official profit claims should be treated cautiously.

### Inferences
- Nominal consolidated SOE assets grew ~2.3x in 2014–2023 (Rp4.6T-thousand → Rp10.4T-thousand), faster than the parent count suggests; with assets ~50% of GDP in 2023, the footprint is large and at least stable relative to GDP. I could not verify a 2014 assets/GDP ratio from a source (2014 nominal GDP not retrieved), so "rising as % of GDP" is unconfirmed.
- Since 2025, the dividend stream (~Rp85–140T/yr historically, Rp200T potential for 2026) is retained by Danantara rather than flowing to the budget, increasing the state's off-budget investment capacity.
- Profit restatements (2024: −Rp118T; 2025: Rp326T claim vs ~Rp276T booked) weaken the reliability of official performance claims.

### Gaps
- No complete annual series of SOE revenue for 2014–2025, nor assets for 2015–2019, 2021, 2025; no revenue/GDP ratio found.
- No official FY2025 consolidated total assets/revenue from Danantara was located.
- IMF/World Bank/OECD independent estimates of Indonesia's SOE share of GDP not retrieved.

## 2. SOE share of the stock market and of banking

### Takeaway
State banks (Himbara) hold roughly half of commercial bank assets (50.2% in 2025), and SOE stocks dominate index movement; I found no reliable published figure for the SOE share of IDX market cap. In Oct 2026 the IDX crashed (market cap Rp16,014T on 2 Jan 2026 → Rp8,863T on 19 Oct 2026) amid an MSCI downgrade threat, and Danantara signalled it would buy SOE shares.

- Through Danantara, the state directly or indirectly controls ~half of the banking system by assets; with BSI's re-designation (Dec 2025), state control of banking slightly widened.

## 3. Danantara: legal basis, governance, mandate, dividends, deals, consolidation; status of the SOE Ministry

### Takeaway
Danantara (BPI Danantara) launched 24 Feb 2025 with seven anchor SOEs; in Oct 2025 the SOE Ministry was abolished and replaced by a regulatory agency (BP BUMN) under Law 16/2025, making Danantara the operational owner of ~1,070 entities and retaining SOE dividends outside the state budget. Governance concentrates power in presidential appointees and has drawn audit-access criticism (BPK/KPK).

- Launch: 24 Feb 2025; projected initial funding Rp320T (~US$20bn); first SOEs transferred: Bank Mandiri, BNI, BRI, MIND ID, Pertamina, PLN, Telkom — [Wikipedia: Danantara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danantara). (Underlying law, UU 1/2025 = 3rd amendment of BUMN Law 19/2003, is not named on that page; not independently verified in this session.)
- 4th amendment: Law No. 16/2025 converts the Ministry of SOEs into Badan Pengaturan BUMN (BP BUMN); DPR passed it 2 Oct 2025, Prabowo signed 6 Oct 2025; Perpres 105/2025 effective 7 Oct 2025; BP BUMN regulates and reports to the president, operational functions go to Danantara — [ANTARA Jambi](https://jambi.antaranews.com/berita/634413/prabowo-tandatangani-uu-16-2025-ubah-kementerian-bumn-jadi-bp-bumn); [ANTARA](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5148357/dpr-setujui-ruu-bumn-menjadi-uu-ganti-kementerian-bumn-jadi-bp-bumn); [DDTC News](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1815980/kementerian-bumn-berubah-jadi-bp-bumn-ini-sederet-tugasnya); [id.wikipedia BP BUMN](https://id.wikipedia.org/wiki/Badan_Pengaturan_Badan_Usaha_Milik_Negara). - Consolidation: 1,068 entities → 221 within 3–4 yrs (Jan 2026) — [ANTARA](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5381860/danantara-bidik-laba-bumn-tembus-rp350-triliun-pada-2026); 23 industrial-estate SOEs to merge into one (headline) — [Okezone, 8 Oct 2026](https://economy.okezone.com/read/2026/10/08/320/3246776/danantara-umumkan-laba-bumn-rp326-triliun-di-2025-potensi-dividen-tembus-rp200-triliun); PTPP + ADHI prioritized for merger, BUMN Karya merger mooted for end-2026 — [Kontan](https://investasi.kontan.co.id/news/menakar-prospek-kinerja-emiten-bumn-karya-jelang-wacana-merger-akhir-2026).

### Inferences
- The institutional trajectory is toward centralisation: ministry → regulator; operating ownership, dividends and capital injections consolidated in a presidentially-controlled body with weaker parliamentary/budget oversight. "Fewer SOEs" (1,068 → ~221) is consolidation of the same footprint, not privatisation.
- Patriot Bonds plus legal immunity effectively channel private elite capital into state vehicles at sub-market rates — a quasi-fiscal tool.

### Gaps
- Exact text of UU 1/2025 (3rd amendment) provisions on BPK audit and "kerugian BUMN bukan kerugian negara" not verified in-session.
- No complete list/count of Danantara deals 2025–2026 or final size of Patriot Bond tranche 1 in rupiah.

## 5. Benchmarks vs other countries

### Takeaway
Older OECD data show Indonesia among the most SOE-dominated large-firm universes (69% of top-10 firms, 2011), behind China (96%) and similar to Malaysia (68%); no current like-for-like SOE/GDP estimate for Indonesia was found.

### Cited Findings
- SOE share of top-10 firms (2011 data): China 96%, UAE 88%, Russia 81%, Indonesia 69%, Malaysia 68% — [OECD 2016, SOEs as Global Competitors](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2016/12/state-owned-enterprises-as-global-competitors_g1g6c657/9789264262096-en.pdf).
- SOE share of GDP (older OECD estimates): China ~30%, Viet Nam ~38%, India and Thailand ~25%, Malaysia and Singapore ~15% — [OECD 2015, SOEs in the Development Process](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2015/04/state-owned-enterprises-in-the-development-process_g1g507d4/9789264229617-en.pdf); Viet Nam still ~one-third of GDP — [OECD 2022 Viet Nam review](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2022/10/oecd-review-of-the-corporate-governance-of-state-owned-enterprises-in-viet-nam_431cb009/a22345d0-en.pdf).
- Companies with >25% public ownership = 2% of market cap in OECD countries vs 47% in China — [OECD Ownership and Governance of SOEs 2024](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/ownership-and-governance-of-state-owned-enterprises-2024_395c9956-en/full-report/component-4.html).
- New OECD global SOE dataset (May 2026) — [OECD Corporate Governance WP No. 29](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2026/05/building-a-global-dataset-on-state-owned-enterprises_c0fe3605/41a26c09-en.pdf) (not read in detail).

### Inferences
- Indonesia's SOE assets ≈50% of GDP (2023) is an assets measure, not value-added; not directly comparable to the OECD GDP-share figures above (which are output-based).

## 6. SOE performance and fiscal risk (debt, restructurings, PMN)

### Takeaway
Fiscal risk is concentrated in construction SOEs (BUMN Karya), Garuda and Krakatau Steel; the Garuda rescue in 2025 was funded by Danantara rather than budget PMN, shifting contingent liabilities off-budget.

### Cited Findings
- Waskita Karya: PMN Rp3.5T (2015) + Rp7.9T (2021) = Rp11.4T total to Q1 2024; MRA with 21 banks in 2024; debt down 20.8% since end-2023 — [Bisnis.com, 5 Jul 2024](https://market.bisnis.com/read/20240705/192/1779594/pmn-jumbo-dan-kinerja-melempem-bumn-konstruksi).
- Wijaya Karya: MRA with 11 lenders covering Rp20.58T; 2023 loss Rp7.12T — [Tempo](https://www.tempo.co/ekonomi/restrukturisasi-massal-utang-bumn-karya-821918); [Kumparan](https://kumparan.com/kumparanbisnis/kinerja-bumn-karya-disorot-wskt-and-wika-pastikan-restrukturisasi-tetap-jalan-21z4YaHKt8S).
- Krakatau Steel: ~Rp35T restructuring 2019–2020 (record); MRA in Q3 2024 — [Investor.id](https://investor.id/market/372732/ramairamai-emiten-bumn-gapai-restrukturisasi).
