# Sector-by-sector state control in Indonesia's strategic sectors (as of October 2026)

Scope note: this covers mining and minerals, land and plantations, energy, food, finance, and infrastructure, telecom and transport. Each figure is dated. Throughout, the notes separate two things:
- **OWNERSHIP**: the state holds equity or assets (SOEs, Danantara, MIND ID, Agrinas).
- **DIRECTION**: the state regulates or directs privately owned activity (export bans, DMO, quotas, price caps, retention rules, permit allocation).

The research budget was about 16 tool calls, so several sub-questions are listed under Gaps for the report writer.

## Mining and minerals: state share of output and policy moves, 2019–2026

### Takeaway
State **ownership** is strong in copper and gold through MIND ID's majority stake in Freeport Indonesia, which is due to rise by 12 percentage points from 2041. It is now controlling but minority in Vale Indonesia (nickel). It is small in coal: Bukit Asam produces only about 6% of national output. Nickel processing is mostly owned by Chinese private capital, around 75% of refining capacity in 2023. The big 2019–2026 shift is therefore mainly **direction**, not ownership. The tools are the export ban and downstreaming push, progressive royalties (PP 19/2025), DMO, mass permit suspensions and revocations, and a 2025 mining law that steers concessions to cooperatives, SMEs and religious mass organisations rather than to SOEs.

**Coal**
- Bukit Asam (PTBA), 65.93% owned by MIND ID: 2025 production was **47.2 Mt** (+9% YoY) and sales 45.4 Mt. **Royalties, permits and enforcement (direction)**
- PP 19/2025 took effect in April 2025. It introduced progressive royalties for nickel, copper and tin, and tiered coal royalties linked to the reference price (HBA). — [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103688604-decoding-indonesia%E2%80%99s-2025-mining-reform-from-regulatory-overhaul-to-global-industry-reshaping)
- 8 May 2026: ESDM held a public consultation on amending PP 19/2025. Nickel tiers would go from 5 to 6, and rates would rise 1–2 percentage points in the US$16,000–26,000/t range. Finalisation was unconfirmed at the time of writing. — [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103891952-smm-nickel-flash-news-indonesia-reviews-pp-192025-amendment-nickel-related-royalties-may-rise)
- June 2025: four nickel IUPs in Raja Ampat were revoked. — [The Diplomat](https://thediplomat.com/2025/06/indonesia-cancels-4-nickel-mining-licenses-after-environmental-outcry/); [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/asiapacific/20250610/f1784dc4268c4887a1813d94f3966059/c.html)
- Sept 2025: the DG Minerba suspended **190 IUPs**, about 4% of roughly 4,600 active permits. A government task force also took over part of the Weda Bay nickel operational area for alleged operation beyond its permit. — [Kamoa Capital](https://kamoacap.com/indonesiapermitssuspended); [SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/103688604-decoding-indonesia%E2%80%99s-2025-mining-reform-from-regulatory-overhaul-to-global-industry-reshaping)
- Satgas PKH had "around 200" mining firms under verification and had reclaimed "tens of thousands of hectares" of unauthorised mining sites (Antara, Oct 2026). No exact figure was given. — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/news/435082/two-years-of-satgas-pkh-reclaiming-state-control-over-forests)

**Mining Law 2/2025 (4th amendment of the 2009 Minerba law, in force 19 Mar 2025)**
- The law adds a "priority" (non-auction) allocation route. Cooperatives, SMEs and companies of religious mass organisations can receive WIUP/WIUPK. PP 39/2025 caps priority areas at 2,500 ha. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/1600530/indonesia-mining-law-bill-amendments-to-mineral-and-coal-mining-law); [AGI Legal](https://www.agilegal.id/perspectives/articles/30725/idkh-introduces-significant-changes-to-mineral-and-coal-mining-regulations); [Brigitta](https://brigitta.co.id/amendment-to-the-mineral-and-coal-mining-law-2/)
- Universities: the plan to let universities manage mines directly was dropped a day before the plenary. Universities now get research and scholarship funding, and SOEs, regional or private firms that share profit with a university can get priority. — [VOI](https://voi.id/en/economy/461119); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/mining/1600530/indonesia-mining-law-bill-amendments-to-mineral-and-coal-mining-law)
- Pre-2025 IUPs with overlap problems, as found by central government evaluation, must be revoked and returned to the state. — [Brigitta](https://brigitta.co.id/amendment-to-the-mineral-and-coal-mining-law-2/)
- PWYP Indonesia warns that priority permits for religious mass organisations risk permit brokering. — [PWYP](https://pwypindonesia.org/en/mining-permit-concerns-for-mass-organizations/)
- A Constitutional Court reading on these routes was reported for 16 Jul 2026, but the outcome was not found. See Gaps.

### Inferences
- **Coal**: the state owns about 6% of output. PTBA's 47.2 Mt divided by 790–817 Mt gives 5.8–6.0% in 2025. Even counting other SOE or MIND ID coal, state ownership of coal is a small minority. Control here is regulatory: DMO volumes, DMO price cap for PLN, HBA-linked royalties, and the 2026 production cut to about 600 Mt.
- **Copper**: Indonesia's copper concentrate and cathode output is dominated by PTFI (Grasberg/Manyar) and Amman Mineral (private, Batu Hijau). With MIND ID holding a majority of PTFI, state-controlled copper output is likely well above half. This is inferred, not a sourced production-share figure. The increment to about 61–63% only takes effect in 2041.
- **Nickel**: mine ore output is fragmented across many private IUPs. Processing (NPI, matte, MHP) is about 75% Chinese-controlled (2023). State presence comes through Antam and Vale Indonesia (34% MIND ID) plus minority stakes in Chinese smelters. Nickel is the clearest case of **dirigisme with private, mostly foreign, ownership**.
- **The 2025 mining law** widens the non-state concession base (cooperatives, SMEs, religious organisations), so it does not nationalise. It moves rent allocation toward politically aligned non-SOE actors, which cuts against a pure "state ownership" reading.
- **Direction of change, 2019–2026**: state ownership has risen modestly (PTFI 2018 majority, Vale 2024 control, PTFI 2041 step-up). State direction has risen strongly (2020 ore export ban, PP 19/2025 royalties, permit suspensions, Satgas PKH mining seizures, coal output caps).

### Gaps
- No sourced national production shares by owner type for nickel ore, gold or copper in 2024–2025. Antam's 2025 output was not retrieved.
- PT Timah: the 2024–2025 seizures of private tin smelters from the Rp300 trillion corruption case and any transfer to PT Timah were not retrieved. PT Timah's share of tin output was also not retrieved.
- The outcome of the 16 Jul 2026 Constitutional Court decisions on Law 2/2025 priority routes, and any count of permits actually awarded to cooperatives or religious organisations.
- The final status of the PP 19/2025 amendment, and the coal DMO price cap level (US$70/t for PLN, from background knowledge, not verified this session).
- Background knowledge not verified this session: MIND ID first took 51.23% of PTFI in Dec 2018; Indonesia banned nickel ore exports from 1 Jan 2020 and bauxite from June 2023.

## Land and plantations: Satgas PKH seizures and the state share of palm oil area

### Takeaway
This is the single largest **ownership** shift of 2025–2026. Under Perpres 5/2025 (signed 21 Jan 2025), Satgas PKH reclaimed more than **6.2 million ha** of plantation-sector forest area by early Oct 2026. About **4.1 million ha** is now managed by state-owned PT Agrinas Palma Nusantara, up from roughly 0.83 million ha in mid-2025. Against about 16.38 million ha of national oil palm area, that is about a quarter. Agrinas, created in 2025, is likely now the world's largest palm oil landholder by area.

### Cited Findings
- Perpres 5/2025 was signed 21 Jan 2025. Two-year review (Antara, 12 Oct 2026): more than 6 million ha of illegal oil palm plantations reclaimed; about **4.1 million ha** managed by PT Agrinas Palma Nusantara; about Rp52 trillion deposited to the state (fines, non-tax revenue, back taxes); 28 companies had operating licences revoked covering about 1 million ha of concessions; and the reclaimed land's economic value is put at nearly Rp400 trillion. — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/news/435082/two-years-of-satgas-pkh-reclaiming-state-control-over-forests)
- The Attorney General put plantation-sector forest area reclaimed at **6,206,212.14 ha** as of early Oct 2026. The 8th handover on 6 Oct 2026 covered 259,230.73 ha to the Forestry Ministry, of which 140,762.04 ha is going to Agrinas. — [Palm Oil Magazine, 13 Oct 2026](https://www.palmoilmagazine.com/forest-task-force-satgas-pkh/2026/10/13/indonesia-reclaims-6-2-million-hectares-of-forest-area-used-by-palm-oil-and-mining/)
- **13 May 2026**: the largest single handover, about **2.37 million ha**, took Agrinas to about **4.12 million ha**. — [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/803394); [OFI Magazine](https://www.ofimagazine.com/news/indonesia-task-force-hands-over-2-37m-ha-of-seized-land-to-state-palm-oil-firm-agrinas)
- Time series of Agrinas area:
  - Apr 2025: about 1 million ha reclaimed from 369 companies. — [Palm Oil Magazine, 4 Apr 2025](https://www.palmoilmagazine.com/agrinas/2025/04/04/indonesia-reclaims-1-million-hectares-of-palm-oil-plantations-from-369-companies/)
  - Mid-2025: 833,413.46 ha handed to Agrinas. — [IDNFinancials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/55814/pkh-task-force-hands-over-833-413-hectares-of-oil-palm-land-to-agrinas)
  - May 2026: about 4.12 million ha.
  - Oct 2026: about 4.1 million ha, plus about 0.14 million ha in the pipeline.
- National context: about 3.3 million ha of Indonesia's 16.38 million ha of oil palm sit illegally inside forest zones (Forestry Ministry data via Antara). Nearly 900,000 ha are designated for ecological restoration. — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/news/435082/two-years-of-satgas-pkh-reclaiming-state-control-over-forests)
- Analysts warn palm oil output may fall during the ownership transition to Agrinas. — [UkrAgroConsult](https://ukragroconsult.com/en/news/indonesias-palm-oil-output-may-decline-amid-ownership-transition-to-agrinas-palma/)
- Investigative criticism describes Satgas PKH as a military and attorney-general-led "palm oil business command under the guise of forest restoration." — [Pulitzer Center / Tempo-linked reporting](https://pulitzercenter.org/stories/pkh-task-force-and-palm-oil-business-command-under-guise-forest-restoration-bahasa)

### Inferences
- The state share of planted oil palm area went from roughly 5–6% to about 25–30%. Agrinas holds about 4.1 million ha, about 25% of 16.38 million ha. If PTPN's estates (roughly 0.5–0.6 million ha of palm, background knowledge, not verified) are added, total state-held palm area is plausibly about 28–30%. This is the clearest evidence for the investor's thesis, and it happened in under 18 months.
- The "reclaimed" total of about 6.2 million ha is larger than the Agrinas figure of about 4.1 million ha. The difference goes to restoration (about 0.9 million ha), national parks such as Tesso Nilo, and pending handovers.
- Legally this is framed as enforcing forest-zone law, not nationalisation. Compensation is not paid, and fines are collected. For investors it works like expropriation risk for any plantation with unclear forest-area status.

### Gaps
- No official Satgas PKH or Agrinas dataset was found. The 4.1 and 4.12 million ha figures differ slightly.
- No Agrinas production (FFB/CPO) data, and no share of national CPO output.
- No exact hectares of mining areas seized; only "tens of thousands of ha" is reported.
- GAPKI's assessment and current national planted area for 2025–2026 were not retrieved.

## Energy: Pertamina, PLN, subsidies and curbs on private fuel retailers

### Takeaway
Energy is the most state-dominated sector by design, but the picture is mixed. Pertamina produces about **69% of national oil** but only **37% of gas** (2024). PLN keeps a legal monopoly on transmission, distribution and retail, while IPPs own about **38.5% of installed capacity** (Sept 2025) and are slated to build about **73% of new capacity** under RUPTL 2025–2034, which is greater private participation. The 2025 forced sourcing of private retailers' fuel through Pertamina was a clear increase in state control of downstream fuel. It was partly reversed with separate 2026 import quotas allocated semi-annually.

### Cited Findings
- Pertamina produced **69% of national oil and 37% of national gas** in 2024 and manages 24% of oil and gas blocks. Its 2025 targets were 416,000 bpd oil and 2,536 mmscfd gas, up from 400,000 bpd and 2,454 mmscfd in 2024. — [Jakarta Post, Mar 2025](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2025/03/12/pertamina-eyes-oil-and-gas-production-to-increase-by-4-this-year); [VOI](https://voi.id/en/economy/538015); [NRGI profile](https://resourcegovernance.org/publications/national-oil-company-profile-pertamina)
- National oil lifting in Q1 2025 was 580,000 bpd, 96% of the APBN target of 605,000 bpd. — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/amp/news/356873/ris-q1-2025-oil-lifting-hits-96-percent-gas-surpasses-goal)
- **PLN**: IPPs held **30,788 MW, or 38.49%** of installed generation capacity as of 30 Sep 2025 (PLN global bond prospectus). RUPTL 2025–2034 plans about 69.5 GW of new capacity, about 73% via IPPs, and allocates 60% of renewable projects to IPPs. — [Petromindo](https://www.petromindo.com/news/article/pln-s-ipp-pipeline-signals-long-term-bet-on-geothermal-and-renewables); [Petromindo RUPTL](https://www.petromindo.com/news/article/govt-allocates-60-of-renewable-projects-to-ipps-in-new-ruptl); [Petromindo](https://www.petromindo.com/news/article/esdm-minister-announces-pln-s-2025-2034-electricity-supply-business-plan)
  - A social-media claim that "70–75% of generation is IPP-owned" conflicts with the prospectus and is unverified. — [Threads @apluswire](https://www.threads.com/@apluswire/post/DYzGR90FJaL/pln-mengonfirmasi-pembangkit-listrik-nasional-kini-dimiliki-ipp-namun-menurut/)
- **Private fuel retailers**: in 2025 Shell, BP-AKR and Vivo exhausted their import quotas and stations ran dry. The Energy Minister told them to source base fuel from Pertamina. Pertamina still had 34% of its 2025 quota (7.52 million kl) left. — [Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/business/energy-minister-tells-shell-bp-vivo-to-rely-on-pertamina-for-fuel-supply); [Jakarta Globe – incl. Exxon](https://jakartaglobe.id/business/shell-bp-vivo-exxon-agree-to-source-fuel-through-pertamina-bahlil)
  - Outcomes: BP-AKR restored BP 92 sales in late Oct 2025. Shell's first 100,000-barrel purchase from Pertamina Patra Niaga came in Nov 2025, and Vivo (44 stations) also bought 100,000 barrels. Vivo cancelled one cargo after finding about 3.5% ethanol in it. — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/amp/news/390553/bp-orders-another-100000-barrels-from-pertamina-to-ease-shortages); [Indonesia Business Post](https://indonesiabusinesspost.com/5700/energy-and-resources/shell-vivo-and-bp-akr-resume-fuel-sales-after-securing-imports-via-pertamina); [Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/business/shell-reaches-first-fuel-purchase-deal-with-pertamina-since-import-restriction); [Petromindo](https://www.petromindo.com/news/article/shell-bp-and-vivo-delay-fuel-purchase-from-pertamina)
- **2026**: private retailers get their own import quotas again, granted every six months, with officials open to about 10% above 2025. Final 2026 volumes were not found. — [Jakarta Post, 26 Jan 2026](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2026/01/26/shell-bp-to-receive-fuel-import-quotas-every-six-months-this-year.html); [Katadata](https://auto.katadata.co.id/news/shell-bp-akr-and-vivos-fuel-import-quotas-to-be-determined-soon-22200); [Digivestasi](https://www.digivestasi.com/news/detail/berita_terkini/energy-ministry-confirms-private-gas-stations-can-import-fuel-again-from-2026?lang=eng)

### Inferences
- **Upstream oil and gas**: state ownership is high and rising for oil, mainly because Pertamina took over expiring PSCs such as Rokan (2021) and Mahakam (2018), which is background knowledge, not verified this session. It is much lower for gas, where foreign operators dominate (BP Tangguh, Eni, ExxonMobil Cepu operator). The 2024 figure of 69% oil and 37% gas is the best current anchor.
- **Downstream fuel**: Pertamina has de facto dominance of retail and imports. Private retailers serve a niche (premium RON 92+) with a few hundred stations. The 2025 quota squeeze shows the state uses **import licensing** (direction) to entrench the SOE.
- **Power**: there is a single-buyer monopoly (PLN) with rising private generation. That makes electricity "state-controlled" through market structure and tariffs, not through generation ownership. The trend in generation ownership is toward more private IPPs.

### Gaps
- Pertamina's share of fuel retail (stations or volume) and the count of private stations in 2025–2026.
- 2025 and 2026 fuel and electricity subsidy plus compensation amounts in the APBN, and PLN's government receivables. A Rp110.74 trillion receivables figure appeared only on social media and is unverified.
- HGBT (gas price cap, US$6/MMBtu for 7 industries) extension status in 2025–2026 was not retrieved.
- The energy self-sufficiency targets (Prabowo's 2028–2029 aims) were not retrieved this session.

## Food: Bulog, rice imports, village cooperatives and free meals

### Takeaway
State **direction** of food rose sharply, and some state **ownership** is being built. The state stopped rice imports in 2025 and 2026, holds record Bulog reserves, and channels a large share of the supply chain through state vehicles: Agrinas Pangan's role in the Koperasi Desa Merah Putih build-out, MBG, and Bulog. Rice production and trade remain mostly private (farmers and millers). The state controls the import gate, the reserve, and the HPP/HET price floors and ceilings.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 Commodity Balance: no general rice import quota and no industrial rice imports for 2026. Rice and sugar imports for consumption are excluded. — [Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2073886/indonesia-confirms-no-industrial-rice-imports-in-2026); [Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2077454/indonesia-confirms-no-rice-or-sugar-imports-for-consumption-in-2026)
- USDA: the government did not authorise Bulog rice imports because of high 2024/25 ending stocks. — [USDA FAS GAIN, Apr 2026](https://www.fas.usda.gov/data/gain-report/2026/04/Grain%20and%20Feed%20Annual_Jakarta_Indonesia_ID2026-0010.pdf)
- Bulog and government rice reserve over time (figures conflict, see the GAIN report and VOI):
  - End-2025: about 3.2–3.24 Mt.
  - 18 Jun 2026: 5.2 Mt.
  - About 20 Oct 2026: about 4.55 Mt.
  - 7.7 Mt of reserve rice was distributed in 2023–2025. — [VOI](https://voi.id/en/amp/549535); [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/news/419863/indonesia-distributes-77-mln-tons-of-rice-reserves-in-2023-2025)
- The government claims sufficiency in 8 commodities at its two-year mark (Oct 2026). — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/amp/news/437181/ri-secures-sufficiency-in-8-commodities-as-prabowo-govt-marks-2-years)

  - Kemenkeu put **Rp37 trillion** in escrow at Himbara to cover installments due 25 Sep 2026, with verification delays.

### Inferences
- KDMP is quasi-state: it is legally a cooperative, but it is financed by state banks, guaranteed by village funds and the APBN, and built through Agrinas Pangan (an SOE). ## Finance: state banks, directed liquidity and export proceeds

### Takeaway
The state already owned the largest banks (Himbara). The 2025–2026 change is **direction of liquidity**:
- Rp200 trillion of government cash (SAL) was placed in five state banks from 12 Sep 2025 and extended to as late as July 2027.
- State-bank credit lines were extended to village cooperatives.
- The export proceeds retention rule (DHE SDA 100% for 12 months, PP 8/2025) was not verified this session.

### Cited Findings
- The Rp200 trillion SAL placement started 12 Sep 2025:
  - Mandiri, BRI and BNI got Rp55 trillion each, BTN Rp25 trillion, and BSI Rp10 trillion.
  - No tenor was set.
  - Credit growth was 7.70% in Sept 2025 versus 7.56% in August.
  - Purbaya admitted the impact was "not yet optimal."
  - Sources: [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/amp/news/379537/indonesia-injects-rp200-trillion-into-5-state-banks-to-spur-growth); [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/news/389713/rp200-trillion-in-state-banks-boosts-liquidity-finance-minister); [Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2077344/purbaya-admits-rp200tn-bank-injection-not-yet-optimal); [Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/business/purbaya-proceeds-with-rp-200-t-himbara-liquidity-plan-despite-bank-doubts)

## Infrastructure, telecom, aviation, ports and toll roads

### Takeaway
The direction is consolidation of ownership under Danantara, not nationalisation. The plan cuts about 1,074 SOEs and subsidiaries to about 200 by end-2026, while divesting non-core assets such as toll roads, water and fibre. That is asset recycling, which is a partial **decrease** in state ownership at the asset level.

### Cited Findings
- Danantara targets cutting SOEs and subsidiaries from 1,074 to about 200 by end-2026 through consolidation, mergers, liquidation, divestment and restructuring. As of early Oct 2026, 354 companies were listed for cuts. — [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/business/economy/danantara-targetkan-pangkas-750-perusahaan-bumn-hingga-akhir-2026-00-gshdq-lbhjjl); [Indonesia Business Post](https://indonesiabusinesspost.com/6559/corporate-affairs/danantara-centralises-control-over-state-assets-the-strategy-behind-167-soe-cuts)
- Consolidation follows divestment of non-core assets such as **toll roads, water systems and fibre optic** businesses. Hutama Karya's merger is delayed pending divestment of Waskita (WSKT) assets, including Waskita toll roads. — [Indonesia Business Post](https://indonesiabusinesspost.com/6559/corporate-affairs/danantara-centralises-control-over-state-assets-the-strategy-behind-167-soe-cuts)
- Seven construction SOEs (Hutama Karya, Waskita, PP, WIKA, Brantas Abipraya, Adhi Karya, Nindya Karya) are to merge into three groups in 2026, slipping from a June 2026 target. — [Tempo](https://en.tempo.co/read/2100275/danantara-indonesia-aims-to-finalize-soe-merger-by-2026); [Antara Yogyakarta](https://jogja.antaranews.com/berita/824615/danantara-pastikan-merger-bumn-karya-tuntas-pada-2026); [CNBC Indonesia opinion, 29 Oct 2026](https://www.cnbcindonesia.com/opini/20261029175524-14-780706/perampingan-bumn-dan-urgensi-penguatan-mandat-pembangunan-indonesia)
- Garuda and Pelita Air consolidation via acquisition was targeted for as early as 2026 (Dec 2025 report). Danantara also links SOE consolidation to "governance of natural resource exports." — [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/business/economy/2-tahun-prabowo-danantara-genjot-konsolidasi-bumn-kontrol-ekspor-sda-00-3m8tp-26hfj7)
- Adhi Karya is divesting at least three subsidiaries by 2027. — [IDNFinancials](https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/68616/danantara-asks-adhi-to-streamline-business-new-contracts-jump-118)

### Inferences
- Danantara centralises ownership under the presidency, which increases control concentration. At the same time it recycles brownfield infrastructure to private or foreign capital. The net effect on the state share of infrastructure assets is ambiguous to slightly negative.
- The IDN Times framing ("control of natural resource exports") suggests Danantara may become a vehicle for export governance. That is unconfirmed.
