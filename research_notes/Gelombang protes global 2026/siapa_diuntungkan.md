# Who Benefits from the 2025–2026 Wave of Anti-Government Protests and Unrest (Europe and Global), as of 2 November 2026

Labels used: **[DOCUMENTED]** = confirmed by official bodies, election results or several independent credible outlets; **[ALLEGED]** = claimed by governments, politicians or single sources and not independently verified; **[SPECULATIVE]** = analyst inference or forecast.

Context on the European wave (for the writer): From mid-September 2026, protests spread to about 17 European countries. They were largely youth- and Gen Z-led over housing, cost of living, austerity and school cuts, fuel and energy prices (linked to the "2026 Iran war fuel crisis"), conscription and rearmament (Germany), and corruption (Albania, Serbia). Peaks: France on 17 Oct (nearly one million reported, riots in Paris, Marseille, Lyon and Limoges) and 24 Oct (more than 400,000 per the Interior Ministry); Italy's "No Meloni Day" on 16 Oct and a general strike on 30 Oct; Albania's "Flamingo Revolution" against PM Edi Rama; housing camps in Spain and Portugal; more than 200 arrests in Germany on 31 Oct. — [Wikipedia: 2026 European unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_European_unrest); [CNN live, 24 Oct 2026](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/24/world/live-news/france-protests-paris-europe-intl); [OPB/AP, 20 Oct 2026](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/10/20/from-france-to-albania-social-unrest-is-setting-europe-s-streets-ablaze/); [CNN Italy, 30 Oct 2026](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/30/europe/italy-protests-anti-government-meloni-intl); [NPR, 25 Oct 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/25/nx-s1-6016126/thousands-of-protesters-across-europe)

## Q1. Domestic political beneficiaries in Europe: do protests translate into votes?

### Takeaway
In France, early polls after the riots show both ends of the spectrum gaining, with RN gaining most and LFI/Mélenchon second, while centrists (Philippe, Attal) slip. The only election held during the wave (Serbia, 25 Oct 2026) was won by the protest movement itself, not by populists. In Spain, the housing crisis that triggered protests is set to benefit the right (PP plus Vox) in the 29 Nov snap election. Reform UK still leads in the UK but has fallen from its 2025 peak, so "unrest leads to far-right votes" is not automatic.

### Inferences
- [SPECULATIVE] The French data fit a "polarisation dividend": order-and-immigration framing helps RN, and social-anger framing helps LFI, while the governing centre is squeezed. Both polls were taken within days of the riots and measure mood, not settled vote choice. The Rio Times makes the same caveat.
- [SPECULATIVE] Who benefits depends on who owns the grievance. Where protests were about corruption and incumbents (Serbia, Albania), the protest movement or opposition gains. Where they turn into riots framed as immigration or disorder (France, Italy), the national-populist right gains. Where they are about housing under a left government (Spain), the right opposition gains.
- [SPECULATIVE] Right-wing incumbents (Meloni, Rama, Vučić before the vote) and some centre-right leaders (Tusk, per Wikipedia) used migration or "antifa" framing to turn unrest into a security agenda. Vučić's defeat shows this does not always work.

## Q2. Foreign influence: Russian, Chinese and US actors (proven vs alleged)

### Takeaway
Russian information operations against France and the EU are well documented by Viginum and the EEAS (Storm-1516, Matryoshka, Portal Kombat/Pravda). For the October 2026 protests, however, Viginum and EU officials say Moscow did not organise or finance them. Russian state voices and networks opportunistically amplified them. US influence is overt and documented: the Dec 2025 National Security Strategy pledges support for "patriotic" European parties, and Musk intervened in UK politics. Covert US operations driving the protests are not evidenced.

### Cited Findings
**Russia: documented baseline operations**

Their main aim appears to be discrediting Ukraine to get Western aid suspended. Viginum also gave a possible (not definitive) link to GRU officer Yury Khoroshenky. - [SPECULATIVE, well grounded] Russia benefits from the unrest without needing to cause it. It is overt ideological and political sponsorship of national-populist parties (the NSS, Robinson's hosting, Musk's platform power), not covert protest organisation. It benefits from unrest framed as migration-driven, and it criticises the protests themselves.
- [SPECULATIVE] Commercial and domestic engagement-farming accounts may matter more for reach than state propaganda, per the reported 10x ratio. This complicates "foreign hand" explanations.

## Q3. "Foreign hand" and colour-revolution narratives by governments (Nepal, Indonesia, Serbia, Georgia, Madagascar)

### Takeaway
In every case examined, incumbents facing mass protests claimed foreign orchestration (USAID, NED, Soros, "major powers", Western states, mineral grabs), but none produced verifiable public evidence. Independent bodies (EUvsDisinfo, a Jakarta Post-reported media probe) found none, or found pro-Russia and pro-China disinformation pushing the colour-revolution narrative itself. Elections later rewarded the protest side in Nepal (March 2026) and Serbia (Oct 2026).

### Cited Findings
- **Serbia** [ALLEGED by government]: Vučić called the 2024–25 student protests a foreign-backed "colour revolution", citing USAID, NED and EED, "foreign instructors", and €28M to the Trag Foundation. [DOCUMENTED rebuttal]: EUvsDisinfo found "no evidence of UK, German or any other Western country's involvement". Ironically, ex-president Tadić claimed Russian services were behind the protests [ALLEGED]. — [European Western Balkans](https://europeanwesternbalkans.com/2025/02/20/in-the-midst-of-mass-protests-serbian-officials-are-recycling-the-narrative-of-a-colour-revolution/); [EUvsDisinfo](https://www.euvsdisinfo.eu/report/the-uk-organises-and-germany-finances-student-protests-in-serbia/); [KoSSev](https://kossev.info/en/tadic-serbian-delegation-in-munich-claims-russian-services-behind-protests/); [BTA, Vučić thanks Russia for warning on EU meddling](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/967955-serbia-s-president-vucic-thanks-russia-for-warning-on-alleged-eu-meddling). Outcome [DOCUMENTED]: the Student List won the 25 Oct 2026 election, and Vučić claims the winners will sanction Russia, which they deny. — [Meduza](https://meduza.io/en/feature/2026/10/26/serbia-s-student-backed-opposition-has-defeated-vucic-at-the-ballot-box-he-says-the-winners-will-impose-sanctions-on-russia-but-they-say-otherwise)
- **Indonesia** [ALLEGED by government]: after the August 2025 riots, Prabowo said he saw signs of "major power" involvement and warned of subversion and terrorism charges. His spokesperson called the protests a "colour revolution", and ministers insinuated oil tycoon Riza Chalid was the mastermind. In April 2025 he had claimed protesters were paid by foreign interests, citing USAID's NGO funding. ## Q4. Economic beneficiaries and losers (bonds, security industry, gold, defence, media)

### Takeaway
The clearest documented economic signal is French sovereign risk. The OAT-Bund spread has been at or near its widest since 2011–12, reportedly above 130bp after the 1 Oct 2026 budget, wider than Italy and Greece. This raises borrowing costs for France, benefits bond short-sellers and relative-value trades, and makes peripheral debt relatively more attractive. Gold and defence stocks have risen through repeated geopolitical shocks in 2025–26, but I found no evidence tying their October 2026 moves to the protests. Security-sector gains are inferred from policy (security decrees, police deployments), not from market data.

### Cited Findings

## Q5. Military and old elites as beneficiaries in Global South cases

### Takeaway
Madagascar is the clearest case of a military capturing a Gen Z uprising. In Oct 2025 the elite CAPSAT unit under Col. Michael Randrianirina sided with protesters, then seized power and promised a 2–3 year transition. It was suspended by the AU and has since deepened ties with Russia. In Nepal, by contrast, the protest generation's preferred outsider (Balen Shah, RSP) won at the ballot box in March 2026, displacing old parties.

### Cited Findings
- [DOCUMENTED] Madagascar: after parliament impeached Rajoelina in Oct 2025, Col. - [SPECULATIVE] Where elections are held quickly (Nepal, Serbia), new anti-establishment movements capture the dividend, and old elites lose.
