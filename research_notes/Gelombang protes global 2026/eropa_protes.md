# Anti-government protests and civil unrest in Europe, mid-2025 to 2 November 2026

Research date: 2 November 2026. Several 2026 figures come from Wikipedia overview pages ("2026 European unrest", "2026 French student protests", "2026 French fuel protests", "2026 Italian unrest"). These pages were changing fast, carry maintenance tags (the Italian page warns of possible LLM-generated text and "citation needed" tags), and contradict themselves in places. Wherever I could, I used the primary outlets they cite (Reuters, AP, AFP, Al Jazeera, Euronews, Le Monde, ANSA, RaiNews). Wikipedia-only figures are labelled as such. **Bold = police/government figure; *italic* = organiser/union figure.**

## Q1. France: "Bloquons tout" (2025), budget and austerity strikes, government crises, pension reform, and the 2026 wave

### Takeaway
France went through two linked waves. The first ran from September to November 2025: the leaderless "Bloquons tout" day on 10 September 2025 and union strikes against austerity budgets, against the backdrop of the Bayrou government's fall and PM Lecornu suspending the pension reform. The second, from September 2026, is the largest and most violent in years: school and student protests (from 17 September 2026) merged with fuel and cost-of-living anger (fuel price spike tied to the 2026 Iran war and the Hormuz blockade) and the yellow vests. It peaked on 17 October 2026 at **550,000** (Interior Ministry) / *800,000* (CGT and yellow vests), with mass looting, the Limoges town hall burned, curfews, protest bans, and thousands of arrests, most of them minors. The CGT has called a general strike from 5 November 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Background, 2025 (first wave)**
- 10 September 2025, "Bloquons tout": the Interior Ministry counted about **175,000 people** at 550 gatherings and 262 blockades (17:45 tally). **473 arrests** nationwide (203 in Paris) and 339 placed in custody. About **80,000 police and gendarmes** were deployed — [DH.be/AFP](https://www.dhnet.be/actu/monde/2025/09/10/direct-bloquons-tout-la-france-sattend-a-vivre-une-journee-chaotique-N2O7VKAXABDOPGLMCLVRKGHB3E/); [Reporterre](https://reporterre.net/10-septembre-la-mobilisation-a-Paris-fortement-reprimee); [Adnkronos](https://english.adnkronos.com/2025/09/09/france-tomorrow-block-everything-80000-agents-in-the-field/)
- Interior Minister Retailleau claimed the "failure of those who wanted to block the country" — [DH.be](https://www.dhnet.be/actu/monde/2025/09/10/direct-bloquons-tout-la-france-sattend-a-vivre-une-journee-chaotique-N2O7VKAXABDOPGLMCLVRKGHB3E/)
- On 10 September the unions mostly held back and saved their forces for the inter-union day of 18 September 2025 — [Freedom News](https://freedomnews.org.uk/2025/09/11/france-blockades-the-leaderless-escalate/) (partisan source). (I found no reliable turnout figure for 18 September or 2 October 2025; see Gaps.)
- PM François Bayrou was ousted over his plans to cut public spending (confidence vote, September 2025) — [Canberra Times/AAP](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9088796/french-pm-suspends-pension-reforms-plan-to-push-budget/)
- Sébastien Lecornu was reappointed PM and survived two no-confidence votes a few days later (October 2025) — [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40387813)
- To survive, Lecornu suspended the 2023 pension reform (retirement age 62 to 64): "No increase in the retirement age will take place from now until January 2028". This was a concession to the Socialists, most of whom abstained — [Connexion](https://www.connexionfrance.com/news/french-prime-minister-suspends-pension-reform-in-bid-for-budget-backing/748614); [Asiae](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2025101709453699683)
- 12 November 2025: the National Assembly voted for the suspension as part of the budget fight — [The Local](https://www.thelocal.fr/20251113/french-mps-back-suspending-pension-reform-as-budget-row-deepens)
- The CGT called for mobilisation against the 2026 budget from 6 November 2025, then a further round of strikes in December 2025 — [Connexion](https://www.connexionfrance.com/news/french-union-calls-for-anti-budget-protests-from-november-6/748962); [Connexion](https://www.connexionfrance.com/news/french-unions-call-for-new-round-of-strike-action-in-december/752323)

**2026 wave: fuel and cost of living**
- 15 September 2026: multi-sector protests began (fishermen, farmers, police, construction, energy, medical, university staff). On 17 September 2026 fishermen blockaded the ports of Nice and Sète and the Frontignan fuel depot — [RFI](https://www.rfi.fr/en/france/20260915-strikes-protests-across-france-from-various-sectors-claiming-more-pay-resources); [Le Monde](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/france/article/2026/09/17/french-fishermen-block-ports-over-fuel-prices_6757635_7.html)
- Trigger: the blockade of the Strait of Hormuz during the 2026 Iran war. Diesel hit €2.37/litre, 1 cent below its record — [Wikipedia: 2026 French fuel protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_fuel_protests), citing Le Monde
- Government response: on 16 September 2026, petrol subsidies for affected sectors were raised to the EU maximum until 31 December. On 7 October, 10 million barrels of diesel were released from strategic reserves. On 2 October the G7 agreed to release 100 million barrels over four months — [RFI](https://www.rfi.fr/en/france/20260917-french-fishermen-renew-protest-as-france-boosts-aid-over-soaring-fuel-prices); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/france-diesel-reserves-protests.html); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/02/nx-s1-5989397/the-g7-will-release-100-million-reserve-barrels-of-diesel-fuel-over-the-next-4-months)
- Calls for a yellow vest "comeback" on 17 October spread through TikTok — [Le Monde](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/politics/article/2026/09/19/how-a-tiktok-rumor-about-the-yellow-vests-return-spread_6757698_5.html)

**2026 wave: schools and students**
- Started 17 September 2026 (teachers' strike over staff and resources). Spread from Lycée Saint-Exupéry in Créteil from 21 September. Grievances: teacher shortages, classes of 32–36 pupils, run-down buildings, Parcoursup, heat (up to 40°C). Context: a budget with **€61 billion in new cuts**, plus inflation, rents and fuel — [Wikipedia: 2026 French student protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_student_protests)
- Escalation (Interior/Education Ministry figures, via Wikipedia): on 1 October 2026, 1,027 lycées were affected and 222 blockaded, with **1,949 arrests** that evening. 305 police and gendarmes were injured, and 1,793 people were taken into custody (about 90% minors) between 28 September and 2 October. 13 October: **161,500** nationally (Ministry) vs *480,000* (youth inter-union) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_student_protests)
- Organisers: lycée unions (USL, Union étudiante, etc.), FSU/SNALC, all labour confederations (CGT, FO, UNSA, CFDT, Solidaires, CFTC, CFE-CGC), Confédération paysanne, LFI and the PCF. The yellow vests joined on 17 October and called "Act II" for 24 October — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_student_protests)

**Peak, 17 October 2026, and after**
- **More than 550,000** people (Interior Ministry) across about 110 marches, 110,000 in Paris. *800,000* according to organisers (CGT, yellow vests) — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/cost-of-living-protests-hit-belgium-italy-portugal-and-france); [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/france/20261017-live-france-s-yellow-vests-throw-their-weight-behind-mounting-student-protests). Another officials' estimate (ABC/AP) put it at up to 170,000; this conflicts and is not reconciled — [Wikipedia fuel protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_fuel_protests)
- Violence: looting in Paris, Marseille, Nice, Nantes, Bordeaux, Limoges, Lille, Toulouse and Strasbourg. Limoges town hall (over 140 years old) largely destroyed by fire. Marseille and Lille city halls broken into. Lyon tourist office set on fire. PM **Lecornu: 1,200 arrests, 150 police injured** — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/cost-of-living-protests-hit-belgium-italy-portugal-and-france); [AFP](https://www.afp.com/en/clashes-blazes-and-wide-scale-looting-grip-france-day-protests)
- BBC: 1,500 shops and businesses destroyed or ransacked, and 3,000 more looted — [BBC live](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c893v4gx2e47t) (via Wikipedia)
- Cumulative: about 2,400 arrests and about 1,000 police injured by 19 October (Interior Ministry/BBC). On 22 October, Justice Minister **Darmanin: 2,700 arrests, more than 1,000 brought before courts, 217 jailed** after fast-track trials. The Wikipedia lead claims 6,700–6,800 arrests, mostly minors (not reconciled) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_student_protests); [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/24/protests-sweep-across-europe-from-france-to-albania-italy-spain-and-portugal)
- Injuries to young people: a 16-year-old lost an eye (rubber bullet), a 15-year-old in Lens lost a hand (stun grenade, IGPN inquiry opened), a 14-year-old had his jaw broken (family filed an attempted-murder complaint). One demonstrator died of cardiac arrest in Paris (Reuters/AP, around 18 October) — [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/16/french-police-injuries-teenagers-school-protests-arrests-custody); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_European_unrest)
- State response: Macron (18 October) blamed "unbridled violence" by the ultra-left and delinquents and promised a "firm response". Curfews in Paris and other cities from 19 October. On 22 October Macron announced a plan to ban "illegitimate" protests. Bills would make protesters liable for up to €10,000 in damages. Lyon, Marseille, Nice and Toulon banned protests on 24 October, while an attempted Paris ban was struck down by an administrative court. About 70,000 police and gendarmes with armoured vehicles and water cannon were deployed on 24 October, and the Louvre, Orsay and Grand Palais closed. The government accused LFI of orchestrating the unrest; LFI called this conspiracy theory — [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/19/world/europe/france-protests-riots.html); [KATV](https://katv.com/news/nation-world/macron-condemns-violent-fuel-price-protests-vows-crackdown-as-france-reels); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_student_protests)
- 24 October 2026: **at least 410,000** nationally (Ministry), about 110,000 in Paris. Clashes after a mostly peaceful day — [CNN live](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/24/world/live-news/france-protests-paris-europe-intl); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_student_protests)
- 31 October 2026 (third weekend in a row): **about 320,000** nationally, including 70,000 in Paris (Interior Ministry) — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/europe/europe-braces-massive-halloween-protests-cost-living-public-services-rcna606671); [IBTimes SG](https://www.ibtimes.sg/france-protests-320000-take-streets-general-strike-threat-looms-95782)
- Next steps: on 21 October 2026, after a meeting with Lecornu at Matignon, the CGT called a general strike from **5 November 2026**. The inter-union is focused on wages and the **2027 budget**. As of 20 October, no RATP/SNCF strike notice had been filed — [Sortiraparis](https://www.sortiraparis.com/en/news/in-paris/articles/354559-protest-and-strike-on-november-5-2026-transport-schools-and-routes-what-to-expect). Rail unions have announced action for that date, and Mélenchon has threatened a general strike if Macron does not respond — [Britannica Q&A](https://www.britannica.com/question/What-happened-in-the-protests-in-France-in-2026) (secondary source)
- Presidential candidate Raphaël Glucksmann criticised the government's "lack of preparedness" — [RFI](https://www.rfi.fr/en/france/20260917-french-fishermen-renew-protest-as-france-boosts-aid-over-soaring-fuel-prices)
- Trump blamed immigration for the unrest in France; JD Vance also criticised it — [Wikipedia: 2026 European unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_European_unrest)

### Inferences
- Lecornu was still PM as of late October 2026 (he reported arrest figures and met the CGT on 21 October), so his government appears to have survived from October 2025 through to now, despite the 2026 crisis.
- The 2025 wave was mainly union-led and budget-focused. The 2026 wave is broader and harder to control: young people and minors, the yellow vests, fuel. Hence the far higher arrest counts and use of curfews.
- Police and organiser figures differ by a factor of about 1.5–3 (13 October: 161,500 vs 480,000; 17 October: 550,000 vs 800,000).

### Gaps
- Turnout for the union days of 18 September and 2 October 2025 (police vs CGT) and the exact date and margin of Bayrou's confidence vote were not verified in this session.
- The final 2026 budget (adopted? via 49.3?) and the current state of the 2027 budget were not verified.
- Whether the 5 November 2026 strike goes ahead as planned is not yet known (it is in the future relative to today).
- The total arrest count for 2026 is contradictory (2,700 per Darmanin on 22 October vs 6,700–6,800 per the Wikipedia lead).

## Q2. Italy: Gaza/Flotilla general strikes in 2025 and the 2026 unrest

### Takeaway
In autumn 2025 Italy saw the largest pro-Palestine general strikes in Europe (3 October 2025: *more than 2 million* per CGIL vs **396,400** per the Interior Ministry). In 2026 the protests turned into a cost-of-living, education and anti-militarism wave: a pro-Gaza general strike on 18 May 2026, then "No Meloni Day" (16 October 2026), riots in Milan, Bologna and Turin, a trucker and farmer blockade in the south over diesel, the deployment of 500 soldiers to Milan, a new security decree, and the USB general strike of 30 October 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Background, 2025**
- 3 October 2025, general strike (CGIL + USB) after Israel intercepted the Global Sumud Flotilla (40 Italians on board, including 4 MPs): *more than 2 million at about 100 demonstrations* (CGIL) vs **29 demonstrations with 396,400 participants** (Interior Ministry). Milan: *100,000* (CGIL) vs **over 50,000** (police). Rome: *300,000* (CGIL). Ports of Naples, Livorno, Salerno and Genoa completely blocked — [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/amp/english/news/2025/10/03/more-than-2-million-people-in-100-demos-says-cgil_5041c9de-a253-42e7-8f4d-a165a88fb17e.html); [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/3/pressure-mounts-on-italian-govt-over-gaza-after-strike-paralyses-nation)
- Salvini threatened sanctions. One source says the strike was declared unlawful by the strike commission for insufficient notice (single source, unconfirmed) — [Ground News](https://ground.news/article/italians-stage-strike-in-support-of-gaza-palestinians_626aff)
- There was also a previous general strike on 22 September 2025 — [Wikipedia: 2025 Italian general strikes and protests for Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Italian_general_strikes_and_protests_for_Gaza) (details not verified)

**2026**
- 18 May 2026: a 24-hour general strike against the "genocide in Gaza" and the Meloni government's militarism — [WSWS](https://www.wsws.org/en/articles/2026/05/19/ital-m19.html) (partisan source, scale not verified)
- 16 October 2026, "No Meloni Day": student protests in Rome, Milan, Turin, Genoa, Bologna, Florence, Venice, Cagliari and elsewhere. US and Israeli flags were burned in Rome. Trigger: among others, the government's plan to cap the number of foreign students in classrooms — [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/cronaca/2026/10/16/gli-studenti-in-piazza-cortei-in-tutta-italia.-a-torino-lancio-di-sassi-contro_1e152b63-e67e-4efd-9ec7-0ca3507f55e3.html); [Wikipedia: 2026 European unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_European_unrest)
- 17 October 2026: Milan riots (stones, barricades set alight, looting, tear gas near the Duomo). About **50 detained**, many of them minors (LaPresse). Meloni blamed "hooligans". Milan mayor Sala said the reasons were understandable but the violence was not — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/cost-of-living-protests-hit-belgium-italy-portugal-and-france)
- 18–19 October: further clashes in Milan. **28 arrests** on 17 October across five cities. On 19 October, 271 detained (23 minors) and 32 arrested — [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/milan-police-clash-with-protesters-second-day-unrest-2026-10-18/); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/italian-police-detain-hundreds-stores-looted-weekend-unrest-milan-2026-10-19/)
- The leaderless "17 October Movement" emerged, calling for weekly Saturday protests until the government falls — [Sky TG24](https://tg24.sky.it/cronaca/2026/10/19/proteste-milano-movimento-17-ottobre-mobilitazione-permanente)
- Trucker and farmer blockade in the south (Calabria, Sicily, Puglia) over diesel (about one-third more expensive than before the Iran war). Empty supermarket shelves and fuel pumps in Naples. Food shortage warnings. Salvini promised tax credits. Extra €105 million allocated. The blockade was winding down around 23 October — [AP](https://apnews.com/article/italy-trucker-protest-empty-shelves-pumps-f488c47abce9295b30e040c1584e31ae); [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/italian-retailers-warn-food-shortages-caused-by-truck-drivers-protest-2026-10-21/); [RaiNews](https://www.rainews.it/maratona/2026/10/emergenza-sud-carburanti-blocchi-tir-dl-misure-sostegno-protesta-autotrasporto-agricoltura-disagi-rete-nazionale-34e5c228-5522-45d1-8882-60f103767510.html)
- Government response: **500 soldiers** (including the Folgore paratroopers) sent to Milan. A **security decree** created the offence of "violent participation in a demonstration" (2–5 years in prison, fines up to €20,000, parents liable for damage caused by minors). Parliament has 60 days to convert it. Salvini: "This is not social distress." Meloni said nearly half of those arrested in Milan had immigrant backgrounds — [AP](https://apnews.com/article/italy-protests-security-measures-government-78dd53cb94d34a1109069f2d2eba3efe); [Wikipedia: 2026 Italian unrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Italian_unrest)
- 24 October 2026: protests in eight cities (Milan around 10,000, the Colosseum occupied in Rome, clashes at Termini, Florence, Bologna with 17 injured). **9 arrests and 371 temporarily detained, including 110 minors** (police statement) — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/24/protests-sweep-across-europe-from-france-to-albania-italy-spain-and-portugal); [BolognaToday](https://www.bolognatoday.it/cronaca/scontri-a-bologna-quattro-ore-di-guerriglia-17-feriti-barricate-e-negozi-devastati.html)
- Cumulative as of 25 October (upper estimates, Wikipedia): about 314 injured, about 69 arrested, about 642 detained — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Italian_unrest)
- 30 October 2026, general strike (USB and grassroots unions) against the cost of living, wages and military spending, in more than 40 cities. Transport only partially running. Water cannon in Turin and Rome (a march on parliament). **8 detained** in Rome. Turnout "tens of thousands" (wire services); *about 1 million* per organisers (via Wikipedia) — [US News/Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-10-30/mass-protests-sweep-italy-during-nationwide-strike); [HNGN](https://www.hngn.com/articles/273660/20261031/italys-general-strike-fills-40-cities-clashes-colosseum.htm); [US Embassy Rome](https://it.usembassy.gov/demonstration-alert-u-s-embassy-rome-italy-october-29-30-2026/)
- Analysis: Donatella della Porta links the movement to a generation shaped by austerity, climate and COVID, with the Gaza protests acting as a "social bond" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Italian_unrest)

### Inferences
- The 2025 Gaza mobilisation built the networks (students, USB, CGIL) that reactivated in 2026 around economic and anti-militarism issues. The "No Meloni Day" slogan carried over from 2025.
- The Meloni government's response is primarily securitarian (military, criminal decree, a migration framing), unlike its fuel response (tax credits).

### Gaps
- No police figure for national turnout on 30 October 2026. The 1 million claim comes only from organisers and is unverified.
- The scale of the 18 May 2026 strike comes only from a partisan source.
- No data found on Italian labour protests in the first half of 2026 (contract renewals, etc.).

## Q3. United Kingdom: Unite the Kingdom, asylum hotels, Palestine Action, farmers, 2026

### Takeaway
UK protests in 2025–2026 have been driven more by culture wars than by economic austerity. On one side are the far-right/anti-immigration Unite the Kingdom (UTK) rallies (13 September 2025: **about 110,000**; 16 May 2026: **about 60,000**) and the asylum-hotel/military-base protests. On the other is mass civil disobedience against the proscription of Palestine Action, with more than 4,000 arrests since July 2025, a High Court ruling that the ban was unlawful (13 February 2026), and a Supreme Court hearing in early November 2026.

### Cited Findings
- 13 September 2025, Unite the Kingdom (Tommy Robinson), London: **about 110,000** (Met, based on CCTV and helicopter footage), with some estimates up to 150,000. Robinson claimed about 3 million. **At least 25 arrests and 26 officers injured (4 seriously)**. A Stand Up To Racism counter-protest was held. Elon Musk gave a "fight back or die" speech — [STV/PA](https://news.stv.tv/world/25-arrested-and-26-police-officers-hurt-at-unite-the-kingdom-rally); [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/europe/uk-police-arrest-9-violence-far-right-rally); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/europe/whats-happening-in-the-uk-musks-fight-back-or-die-speech-escalates-london-rally-rhetoric-1.500268725)
- Summer 2025: asylum-hotel protests started at the Bell Hotel, Epping, after an asylum seeker was charged with (and later convicted of) sexual assault (jailed September 2025). Some of the protests turned violent and led to arrests — [Euronews](https://euronews.com/2025/09/23/asylum-seeker-jailed-for-sex-attacks-that-sparked-uk-anti-migrant-protests); [Al Jazeera video](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2025/8/24/anti-refugee-protesters-in-the-uk-rally-against-hotels-for-asylum-seekers); [Wikipedia: 2025 British anti-immigration protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_British_anti-immigration_protests)
- 31 January 2026: 800–1,000 people (with Robinson) marched in Braintree against the asylum centre at RAF Wethersfield. The Home Office says it will close all asylum hotels and move people to sites such as military bases — [ITV](https://www.itv.com/news/anglia/2026-01-31/hundreds-turn-out-for-anti-asylum-centre-march)
- 16 May 2026, second UTK rally: **about 60,000** (early Met estimate), less than half of September 2025, far short of the "a million or more" organisers hoped for. About 4,000 officers deployed (an operation costing about £4.5 million) to keep it apart from the Nakba Day march. **43 arrests** across both events (20 linked to UTK) — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/05/16/london-police-out-in-force-as-tens-of-thousands-attend-rival-rallies); [Spectator AU](https://www.spectator.com.au/2026/05/did-millions-attend-tommy-robinsons-unite-the-kingdom-rally/); [Searchlight](https://searchlightmagazine.com/2026/05/tommy-robinsons-unite-the-kingdom-march-goes-nowhere/)
- Palestine Action (proscribed in July 2025 under the Terrorism Act; support carries up to 14 years in prison): 9 August 2025, **522 arrested** in London — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/8/10/uk-police-arrested-522-people-at-palestine-action-protests-in-london)
- 13 February 2026: the High Court ruled the proscription unlawful. On 25 March 2026 police said they would resume arrests. April 2026: **more than 500 arrested** at Trafalgar Square. 28 September 2026: arrests outside the Labour conference in Liverpool — [France 24/AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261031-uk-police-arrest-palestine-action-supporters-ahead-of-supreme-court-hearing); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/4/11/police-begin-arrests-at-uk-protest-against-palestine-action-ban); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/28/palestine-action-protesters-arrested-outside-uk-labour-party-conference)
- 31 October 2026, protest outside the US Embassy in London (organiser: Defend Our Juries): **325 arrested** (Met, 320 for supporting a proscribed organisation). Earlier tallies said 139. Defend Our Juries says the total since July 2025 is **more than 4,000**. Comes ahead of a two-day Supreme Court hearing on the proscription — [France 24/AFP](https://www.france24.com/en/live-news/20261101-uk-police-say-325-people-were-arrested-at-palestine-action-demo); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/31/london-police-arrest-48-as-hundreds-protest-palestine-action-ban)

### Inferences
- The UK has been relatively absent from the October 2026 continental cost-of-living wave; I found no reports of comparable mass UK protests in October 2026.
- UTK's momentum appears to have fallen (110,000 to 60,000), while Palestine Action civil disobedience has continued to grow (more than 4,000 arrests in total).

### Gaps
- UK farmers' protests (inheritance tax, 2025–2026): no sources found in this session.
- The Supreme Court ruling on Palestine Action is not yet known.
- No data on UK union or cost-of-living strikes in 2026.

## Q4. Other European countries (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
The October 2026 wave reached at least 17 countries. It was led largely by Gen Z and students, and centred on housing, education, cost of living and rearmament/conscription. The biggest cases outside France and Italy are Spain (housing camp, snap election 29 November 2026), Serbia (the movement won the 25 October 2026 election), Germany (Gen Z regelt, about 120,000 on 31 October 2026), Portugal, Belgium, Albania, Czechia, Ireland and the Netherlands.

## Q5. Common themes across countries

### Takeaway
The recurring threads are: (1) cost of living, energy and fuel (sharpened by the 2026 Iran war and the Hormuz blockade); (2) housing (Spain, Portugal, Luxembourg, Greece); (3) austerity budgets and underfunded education (France with €61 billion in cuts, Belgium's fees, Austria, Ireland); (4) rearmament and conscription vs social spending (Germany, Italy); (5) Gaza and Palestine (Italy 2025, the UK); (6) migration as an issue on both the far right and in government framing (UK UTK, The Hague, Vilnius, Meloni, Trump); and (7) distrust of elites and corruption (Serbia, Spain's penthouse scandal, Albania/Kushner). The common actors are Gen Z and minors organising through TikTok, Telegram and Instagram, often leaderless and alongside traditional unions. The common state response is securitarian: curfews, protest bans, the military in Milan, criminal decrees, and parental liability.

### Cited Findings
- Shared grievances: underfunded education, rising living costs, uncertain prospects for graduates (Alemanno) — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/17/cost-of-living-protests-hit-belgium-italy-portugal-and-france)
- Living costs, schooling and a housing crisis across France and several European countries — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/24/protests-sweep-across-europe-from-france-to-albania-italy-spain-and-portugal); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/20/france-protests-europe-cost-living-violence.html)
- The fuel trigger: the Hormuz blockade in the 2026 Iran war; diesel at €2.37 in France; Italian diesel about a third more expensive — [Wikipedia fuel protests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_fuel_protests); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/italy-trucker-protest-empty-shelves-pumps-f488c47abce9295b30e040c1584e31ae)
- Anti-militarism: Italy's 30 October strike against military spending; Germany against conscription and the defence budget — [US News/Reuters](https://www.usnews.com/news/world/articles/2026-10-30/mass-protests-sweep-italy-during-nationwide-strike); [TRT World](https://www.trtworld.com/article/1aa63b7c7a5b)
- Leaders linking the unrest to migration: Trump, Tusk, Magyar, Weidel, and Meloni (on the immigrant background of those arrested) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_European_unrest)
- The Economist framed it as "The agony of liberal Europe" — [Economist](https://www.economist.com/leaders/2026/10/22/the-agony-of-liberal-europe)
- Parallel legal responses: France (a ban on "illegitimate" protests, €10,000 liability, parental civil liability); Italy (the "violent participation" offence, 2–5 years) — [Wikipedia FR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_French_student_protests); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/italy-protests-security-measures-government-78dd53cb94d34a1109069f2d2eba3efe)

### Inferences
- The 2025 wave was more union-led and issue-specific (budgets, Gaza). The 2026 wave is more of a youth/Gen Z uprising that crosses issues and is less disciplined (looting, minors), similar to the 2025 Gen Z protests in Morocco, Nepal and elsewhere.
- The defence-vs-social-spending tension is a new theme (Germany and Italy) that did not feature in the 2022 inflation wave.
