# Upah Minimum 2027 (UMP/UMK) dan Respons Peritel Indonesia: Bahan Prediksi Dampak

Research cut-off: 23 Nov 2026. Status labels used below: **[CONFIRMED]** means a decision or regulation has been issued or announced. **[PROPOSAL]** means a demand or recommendation. **[SIMULATION]** means a media or website projection. Several 2027 figures come from news aggregators published 20–23 Nov 2026, before all Kepgub (governor decrees) were available, so they should be re-checked against the decrees.

## 1. Keputusan upah minimum 2027

### Takeaway
UMP 2027 is set under the new **UU No. 27/2026 tentang Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan** and the Menaker letter of 16 Nov 2026. The formula is inflation + (growth × α), with α widened to **0.50–1.0** (it was 0.5–0.9 for 2026) and KHL now an explicit reference. As of 23 Nov 2026, at least 28 provinces had announced. DKI Jakarta rises **7.1% to Rp6,136,941**. Some provinces with low UMPs jumped by double digits toward KHL: Jabar +29.4%, Jateng +14.1%, Jatim +11.6%, and Bali reportedly +42%. That makes 2027 the largest increase since at least 2020 in several Java provinces. UMK (deadline 25 Nov) and UMSP/UMSK (26 and 30 Nov) were still pending.

- Menaker Yassierli issued Surat Menteri Ketenagakerjaan **No. B-M/209/HI.01.00/XI/2026** dated **16 Nov 2026** to all governors. — [JawaPos, 19 Nov 2026](https://www.jawapos.com/ekonomi/2611190274/mekanisme-upah-minimum-2027-mengacu-uu-pelindungan-pekerja-baru-begini-pedomannya)
- The 2027 guidance follows from **UU No. 27 Tahun 2026 tentang Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan**. - Governors **must** set both UMP and **UMSP** (provincial sectoral minimum wage). They **may** set UMK and UMSK if requirements are met. — [JawaPos](https://www.jawapos.com/ekonomi/2611190274/mekanisme-upah-minimum-2027-mengacu-uu-pelindungan-pekerja-baru-begini-pedomannya)

### Inferences
- DKI Jakarta's +7.1% sits inside the union demand range (7.5%–9.5%) only at its lower edge. It is roughly consistent with α near the top of the 0.5–1 range applied to Jakarta inflation and growth.
- Several provinces with low UMPs (Jabar, Jateng, Jatim, DIY, Bali) appear to have used the new KHL reference to make catch-up jumps. This is the key structural change for 2027: a pure formula outcome would be roughly 5–8%.
- For retailers, the effective floor in Java is usually the UMK (and UMSK if set), not the UMP. Industrial-area UMKs such as Bekasi (proposed about +8%) are rising less, in percentage terms, than the low UMPs.
- Retail chains with heavy exposure to Central Java, East Java, DIY and Bali store networks (minimarkets, supermarkets) are likely to face the largest percentage increases in their minimum-wage floor for 2027. This holds if the UMKs there follow the UMP direction, which is not yet known.
- With Jakarta at +7.1%, Jabar at +29%, Bali at +42% and NTT at +6%, the simple average of announced provinces is probably near or above 10%. That would be well above 2026 (about 5.8%) and 2025 (6.5%). I did not find an official national average.

### Gaps
- No official national average % for UMP 2027 had been published.
- The official Kepgub texts were not checked. The Bali +42.15% figure is an outlier and needs verification against the Kepgub. A loker.id simulation had projected only Rp3,396,699 ([loker.id](https://www.loker.id/upah-minimum/2027/bali)).
- Exact α values chosen per province are not known.
- UMK 2027 for key retail cities (Surabaya, Bandung, Semarang, Medan, Denpasar, Tangerang) was pending as of 23 Nov 2026.
- No 2027 UMSP/UMSK figure exists for the retail or trade sector, and I found no Aprindo, Hippindo or Aprindo-Solihin statement on the 2027 decision.
- The primary text of Surat Menaker B-M/209/HI.01.00/XI/2026 was not obtained.

## 2. Riwayat kenaikan UMP 2020–2026 dan regulasinya

### Takeaway
Increases swung sharply with each regulatory regime: the PP 78/2015 era (8.51% in 2020), a COVID freeze (2021), PP 36/2021 (about 1% in 2022), Permenaker 18/2022 (about 7.5% in 2023), PP 51/2023 (about 3.6% in 2024), a presidential decree-style flat 6.5% (2025), and PP 49/2025 with α 0.5–0.9 (2026, Jakarta +6.17%). 2027 moves to UU 27/2026, α 0.5–1 plus KHL.

### Inferences
- Cumulative DKI UMP growth from 2020 to 2027 is Rp4,267,349 → Rp6,136,941, or +43.8% (computed), about 5.3% CAGR over 7 years.
- From 2024 onward, the floor in Jakarta has stepped up by 6–7% per year (+6.5%, +6.17%, +7.1%), clearly above inflation (about 2–3%). That implies a real increase of roughly 4% per year in the wage floor.
- Regulatory volatility (five different regimes in seven years) is itself a planning risk that retailers repeatedly cite.

## 3. Kerangka regulasi dan perubahan biaya tenaga kerja lainnya 2026–2027

### Takeaway
UU 27/2026 (Pelindungan Ketenagakerjaan, passed by the DPR on 6 Oct 2026, following MK 168/PUU-XXI/2023) reshapes wage setting with α 0.5–1 plus KHL and mandatory UMSP. It reportedly also restricts outsourcing to four job types and restores UU 13/2003-style severance (dropping the 0.5× multiplier). Each of these raises retailers' labour-cost and flexibility risk. On the offsetting side, the government plans to extend PPh 21 DTP in 2027 to workers in all sectors earning up to Rp10m per month. That is an employee-side tax relief, not yet final.

- **Outsourcing** is limited to four types of work plus special provisions for mining (per news reports). — [FSP KEP SPSI](https://spkep-spsi.org/uu-ketenagakerjaan-2026-disahkan-ini-perubahan-upah-phk-outsourcing-dan-hak-pekerja/)
- **PKWT**: a sidebar headline says contract workers become permanent if PKWT rules are violated. No detail was available. — [FSP KEP SPSI](https://spkep-spsi.org/uu-ketenagakerjaan-2026-disahkan-ini-perubahan-upah-phk-outsourcing-dan-hak-pekerja/)
- **PPh 21 DTP 2027 [PROPOSAL]**: the government plans to expand it to all sectors for workers earning up to Rp10m per month gross, covering about 7.5 million workers. DJP said on 11 Nov 2026 that the scheme is "not yet final" and its form may change. 2026 already had DTP for selected sectors (PMK 72/2025 expansion). — [Investortrust](https://investortrust.id/macro/121983/pemerintah-akan-perluas-target-insentif-pph-21-dtp-pada-2027); [IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/articles/pph-21-dtp-2027-belum-final-djp-sebut-cakupan-penerima-bisa-berubah); [DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1823568/bebas-pajak-karyawan-hingga-kenaikan-ptkp-masuk-paket-insentif-2027); [PMK 72/2025](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2025-11/PMK%2072%20Tahun%202025%20Perluasan%20PPh%2021%20DTP.pdf)
- A **PTKP increase** is reportedly also in the 2027 incentive package [PROPOSAL]. — [DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1822906/fasilitas-pajak-2027-disiapkan-ada-kenaikan-ptkp-hingga-pph-21-dtp)
- **Tapera** (PP 21/2024) sets a 3% saving: 0.5% employer and 2.5% worker. Apindo put total mandatory employer levies (BPJS TK, BPJS Kesehatan and others) at **18.24%–19.74%** of payroll before Tapera. — [Investortrust (2024)](https://investortrust.id/business/29610/potongan-wajib-perusahaan-capai-19-75-tapera-menambah-beban); [Bisnis (2024)](https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20240528/15/1768890/deretan-potongan-gaji-swasta-usai-jokowi-keluarkan-beleid-tapera)

### Inferences
- Statutory on-costs of roughly 18–20% of payroll scale directly with the wage base. A 7–14% UMP/UMK rise therefore lifts total employer cost for minimum-wage staff by about the same percentage.
- Restricting outsourcing and returning to UU 13/2003 severance raise the cost of flexible staffing. This could limit retailers' past tools: outsourced cashiers, security and cleaning, and rolling PKWT contracts.
- PPh 21 DTP benefits employees' take-home pay and may support consumer spending. It does not reduce employers' wage bill.

### Gaps
- The official UU 27/2026 text (part-time rules, working hours, PKWT maximum duration, transition) was not reviewed.
- I found no 2027 change to BPJS Ketenagakerjaan or BPJS Kesehatan contribution rates.
- Current Tapera status is unclear. My recollection is that a September 2025 MK ruling ordered the Tapera law to be revised, but I did not verify this in this session.
- No 2026–2027 changes to THR rules were found.

## 4. Respons peritel/pengusaha terhadap kenaikan upah dan konteks pasar

### Takeaway
Evidence of retail responses comes mainly from store-network rationalisation rather than explicit wage-driven layoffs:
- Alfamart closed about 400 stores in 2024 while opening over 1,000.
- Matahari closed 13 stores in 2024 and about 8 more in 2025.
- Hypermart, Giant, Transmart and Centro have closed stores.
- Hypermart has introduced self-checkout.

Hippindo frames closures as portfolio pruning. Layoffs rose economy-wide: Kemnaker counted 88,519 in 2025, with wholesale and retail trade the second-largest sector. Retail demand remains weak: BI real sales index +0.6% yoy in Sept 2026. Historically, Aprindo has protested increases but complied. Its recent asks have been for tax and consumption incentives, not wage deferrals.

### Inferences
- For 2027, the base case is that retailers will:
  1. Absorb the increase for entry-level store staff, since UMP/UMK binds mainly for workers under 1 year of service.
  2. Compress wage-scale differentials.
  3. Continue pruning underperforming stores and slowing hiring.
  4. Increase automation (self-checkout) selectively in hypermarkets and supermarkets.
  5. Pass part of the cost through prices where demand allows.
- With real sales growing only about 1% yoy, price pass-through room is limited, so margin compression is likely.
- Retailers most exposed: minimarket chains (AMRT, MIDI) and supermarkets with dense networks in Jateng, Jatim, DIY and Bali, where 2027 floors jumped 11–42%. Department stores and hypermarkets already shrinking (Matahari, Hypermart) face the risk of further closures.
- The new limits on outsourcing and the stricter severance rules (UU 27/2026) make layoffs and flexible staffing costlier. This may push retailers toward hiring freezes, attrition and automation rather than outright layoffs.

### Gaps
- No quantified labour cost share of retail revenue or opex was found from BPS or the associations in this session. Report writers should take it from company financials (e.g., AMRT, MIDI, LPPF, MPPA, RALS employee expense / revenue), which another researcher may be covering.
- No data was found on part-time staffing shifts, franchise conversion, relocation to low-UMK regions, or reduced store hours tied explicitly to wages.
- No Aprindo 2026 or 2027 sales-growth forecast was retrieved, and no mall occupancy or rent data was retrieved.
- PLN tariff and VAT 12% (luxury-only, 2025) context was not researched in this session due to tool-call limits.
- Retail-sector layoff counts for 2026 were not broken out.
